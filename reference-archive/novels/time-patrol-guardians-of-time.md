## Blurb
Manse Everard's Time Patrol—policing history from a far-future base—collected around *Guardians of Time*, including the Carthage-wins shocker *Delenda Est*.

## Detailed plot summary
Core shorts (*Time Patrol*, *Brave to Be a King*, *Only Game in Town*, *Delenda Est*, later *Gibraltar Falls*, etc.) and novels (*Shield of Time*). Patrol restores a preferred timeline against freewill and rivals. *Delenda Est*: Neldorians help Carthage beat Rome → Indo-European civilization erased; Patrol must undo. Foundational **organizational changewar** opposite Asimov's Eternity (Patrol preserves, Eternity rewrites for happiness).

## Temporal structure
Agency preserves one history; enemies create branches that must be pruned.
