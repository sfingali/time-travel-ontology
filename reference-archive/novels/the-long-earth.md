## Blurb
A simple stepper box opens an infinite chain of parallel Earths; natural stepper Joshua and AI Lobsang explore Eastward while Datum Earth convulsively empties.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Overnight, DIY **stepper** iron/potato circuits let most humans "step" to parallel Earths arranged in an ordered chain (**East** / **West** from **Datum** Earth). Iron cannot be carried; stepwise colonization, "Long Earth" logistics, and phobic non-steppers reshape politics. **Joshua Valienté**, natural stepper (no box nausea), is recruited by soft-AI **Lobsang** (transmigrated Tibetan motorcycle repairman personality in corporate substrate) for a dirigible survey deep East. They find trolls, elves, and worlds increasingly alien; mysteries of stepping's origin; Datum's social fracture as people leave.

Mechanism: **interacting** parallel worlds via mass migration tech—not closed alt-history. Series continues: *The Long War*, *The Long Mars*, *The Long Utopia*, *The Long Cosmos*.

## Temporal structure
Spatialized multiverse (ordinal Earths). Travel is sideways in possibility, not along time—yet colony timelines diverge; contact is constant.

```
... W2 - W1 - DATUM - E1 - E2 - E3 ... (infinite)
```
