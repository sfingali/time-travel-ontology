## Blurb
Hugo/Nebula-winning novelette that launched Willis's Oxford historians: a student is sent to St Paul's in the Blitz instead of the first century—and learns humility amid incendiaries.

## Detailed plot summary
Narrator Bartholomew prepares for a first-century drop but is reassigned to 1940 London. He serves on the St Paul's fire watch, bonds with locals, fails to prevent some losses, and returns changed. Establishes net rules, time-lag, and moral stakes later expanded in *Doomsday Book* and *Blackout*/*All Clear*.
