## Blurb
Jack Havig's mutant time-jumping draws him into the racist future Eyrie—and a recruitment war across history.

## Detailed plot summary
Born 1933 Midwest with genetic TT. Learns of 21st-c. catastrophe; infiltrates/rebels against **Eyrie** (future authoritarians). Recruits a counter-'tribe' of jumpers. Extended medieval Constantinople episode (Fourth Crusade). Hugo finalist. Pair with Anderson's **Time Patrol**.

## Temporal structure
Personal genetic TT + opposing future factions = changewar.
