## Blurb
Ursula Todd is born again and again on 11 February 1910; each death returns her to darkness and a new loop—flu, abuse, Blitz, even a path to shoot Hitler—without ever confirming whether the world outside her loops truly changed.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Ursula Todd**, upper-middle-class Buckinghamshire, born 11 Feb 1910. First version: strangled by umbilical cord, stillborn. Later childhood deaths: drowning; fall from window retrieving a doll. Spanish flu 1918 kills her repeatedly despite growing foreknowledge; fourth avoidance succeeds.

A life of rape, illegal abortion, and abusive marriage ends when husband kills her fleeing; later loops she pre-emptively strikes the would-be rapist and also saves neighbour **Nancy** from a child-murderer—Nancy later loves brother **Teddy** (seed for companion novel *A God in Ruins*).

WWII strands: War Office work in London; repeated Blitz, including Argyll Road shelter hit (sometimes victim, sometimes rescuer). One life: marries a German in 1934, trapped in bombed Berlin. Strong déjà vu crystallizes purpose: befriend Eva Braun in 1930 Munich shop-girl years, get close to Hitler with a gun, assassinate him—and be shot by Nazis immediately after. Each sequence ends in "darkness"; the novel never shows aftermath, leaving open whether Nazis were prevented or merely reshuffled, and whether lives are objective branches or subjective experience. 1967 Ursula still speculates with a nephew about might-have-beens.

Costa Novel Award; Walter Scott Prize; major 21st-c. lists. BBC One miniseries 2022 (Thomasin McKenzie).
