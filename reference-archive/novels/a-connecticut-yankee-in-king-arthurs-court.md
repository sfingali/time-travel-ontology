## Blurb
A 19th-century American engineer is knocked back to 6th-century Camelot and tries to remake Arthurian Britain with Yankee technology and republican ideals—until the past strikes back.

## Detailed plot summary
Hank Morgan, a Hartford arms-factory superintendent, is struck on the head and awakens in Camelot (c. 528). Condemned to burn, he "predicts" a solar eclipse and becomes "Sir Boss," modernizing the kingdom with factories, newspapers, schools, and gunpowder while undermining chivalry and the Church. He befriends Clarence, falls for Sandy, and joins quests that satirize Malory. After Arthur's death and civil war, Hank and a few followers make a last stand with electrified wire and Gatling guns against knights; Merlin's enchantment puts Hank into a centuries-long sleep, and he awakens (or dies dreaming) back in his own century.

## Temporal structure
Unexplained time slip (blow to the head); single displacement into medieval past; return via Merlin's long sleep. No interacting parallel worlds—satirical fish-out-of-water alternate-history-in-the-past.
