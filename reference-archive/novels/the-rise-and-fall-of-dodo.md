## Blurb
Epistolary techno-fantasy: the Department of Diachronic Operations uses revived magic (and a witch) to send agents into history until bureaucracy and Tristan/Melisande's entanglement spiral.

## Detailed plot summary
Linguist Melisande Stokes and Tristan Lyons build D.O.D.O. around physics-magic that enables time travel when magic still worked historically. Missions (Elizabethan London, etc.), multiple Magical Interlocutors, and corporate-government farce escalate until diachronic warping threatens the present. Tags: branching_timeline | reality_rewrite (magic's historical decay).

## Temporal structure
