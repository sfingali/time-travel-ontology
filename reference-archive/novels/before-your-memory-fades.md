## Blurb
Hakodate's Café Donna Donna inherits the coffee-timer legend: four stories of orphaned anger, sister-grief, and Tokita-bloodline pouring rules—same immutable present, new northern setting.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
JP 2018; EN *Before Your Memory Fades*. Moves the device to **Café Donna Donna** in Hakodate. Rules persist (seat, café-graph, no present change, coffee cools); added constraint: time-coffee poured by a **Tokita** woman aged seven+. Episodes include **Yayoi** confronting dead parents' abandonment and learning her mother Miyuki's parallel pain; **Reiko** accepting sister Yukika's death after a quiet past visit Yukika arranged. Grief-processing anthology continuing Kawaguchi's emotional-TT mode.
