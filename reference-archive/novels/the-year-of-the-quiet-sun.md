## Blurb
Demographer-biblical scholar Brian Chaney is drafted into a US TDV chrononaut team; forward surveys of 1980–2000+ map racial civil war, nuclear Chicago, and a trap that strands him in a future that fears him.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Summer 1978: **Kathryn van Hise** (Bureau of Standards) recruits **Brian Chaney** after his Dead Sea Scrolls book; his think-tank contract is purchased—little choice. At a base south of Joliet he teams with USAF Maj. **William Moresby** and USN Lt. Cdr. **Arthur Saltus**. Both Chaney and Saltus are drawn to Kathryn.

First mission: Thursday after the 1980 election. Navigation error lands Chaney earliest. They find President Meeks won via handling Chicago race riots; a wall down Cermak Road; martial law after a Joint Chiefs coup attempt that *their own future report will foil*. Saltus finds a marriage license—himself and Kathryn.

Authorized deeper jumps: Moresby to 4 July 1999 dies in racial civil war after Chinese nuke support for "ramjet" guerrillas. Saltus (his 50th birthday, 2000) is wounded; an unknown figure helps him home with Moresby's tape. Chaney, disillusioned, jumps to indeterminate **2000-plus**; reactor power failure traps the vehicle. Outside: neglect, Saltus's grave. Arthur and Kathryn's children lead him to elderly Kathryn: civilization collapsed because advance knowledge drove Meeks into disastrous China war then civil war. Chaney reported none of this—**no return power**. Late reveal: Chaney is Black; "Everyone fears you… I am the only one here who does not fear a black man."

Nebula/Hugo finalist; retrospective Campbell Memorial Award 1976. Biblical Qumran motifs under the Will County Armageddon (Miesel intro, Gregg Press).
