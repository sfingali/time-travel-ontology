## Blurb
Four women invent time travel in 1967; decades later a bolted greenroom corpse and a Conclave that pathologizes emotion turn feminist institutional TT into a puzzle-box murder across generations.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Lake District lab, 1967: **Margaret** (baroness-cosmologist), **Lucille** (faster-than-light radio), **Grace** (matter behavior), **Barbara** (nuclear fission) achieve TT—first a rabbit, then meetings with future selves. On TV, Barbara has a manic-depressive breakdown and exits; the remaining three found the **Conclave** to control the technology (including encounters with one's own death).

Margaret's institutional response to Barbara's crisis: recruit for emotional flatness, treat traveler psychology as risk management, breed an empathy-poor elite. 2017–18: Barbara's granddaughter **Ruby** gets a warning from Grace about an elderly woman's violent death. Seychellois museum volunteer **Odette** finds a body in a room locked from inside. Investigation braids romance (Ruby/Grace), Conclave SOPs, and Gen-1 vs later-generation trauma. Head of Zeus (UK 2018) / Crooked Lane (US 2019). Debut praised as genre-savvy puzzle box about the human cost of non-linear life.
