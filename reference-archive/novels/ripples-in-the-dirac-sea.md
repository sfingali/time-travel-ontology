## Blurb
Nebula-winning short: the inventor of time travel cannot escape dying in a hotel fire—no matter how many millions of intervening lives he packs between the nanoseconds.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
*Asimov's* Oct 1988. Inventor uses a Dirac-sea-inspired method to travel in time yet remains doomed to a particular hotel fire. He iterates endlessly—living other stretches "between" the critical instants—but the death event will not unhappen. Compact fatalism classic; Nebula Best Short Story 1988; Hugo finalist 1989. Frequently anthologized (*Time Traveler's Almanac*, etc.).

## Temporal structure
Unavoidable fixed death + subjective freedom in the interstices; information/experience without grandfather escape.
