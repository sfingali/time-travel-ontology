## Blurb
LN #1 (and franchise key): Haruhi's unconscious god-powers warp reality; SOS Brigade hides that Mikuru is a time traveler, Yuki an alien interface, Koizumi an esper—while later volumes stack closed-space disasters and the Endless Eight summer loop.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Kyon** narrates: eccentric **Haruhi** founds the SOS Brigade to find aliens/time-travelers/espers—and unknowingly *is* a reality-god whose moods spawn closed spaces and can unmake the world. Recruits: bookish **Yuki Nagato** (Data Integration Thought Entity interface), shy **Mikuru Asahina** (time traveler from the future), transfer **Itsuki Koizumi** (Agency esper). Their orgs observe Haruhi to keep her happy/ignorant.

TT-relevant arcs across the LN/anime: Mikuru's future origin and "Bamboo Leaf Rhapsody" past visit; **Endless Eight** (*The Sigh of Haruhi Suzumiya* material)—Haruhi resets summer ~15,532 times until Kyon breaks the loop; *The Disappearance of Haruhi Suzumiya* alters the continuum into a mundane world without powers. Franchise: 13+ LN vols (Kadokawa/Yen); KyoAni anime 2006 + 2009 rebroadcast; film *Disappearance* (2010). Archive AV: `tv/haruhi-endless-eight.md`.
