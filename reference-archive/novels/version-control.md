## Blurb
Near-future New Jersey: physicist Philip Steiner's "causality violation device" (never call it a time machine) may already have rewritten the marriage, the presidency, and the car crash that killed Rebecca and Philip's son Sean.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Rebecca Wright** works customer support at dating site **Lovability** (where she met Philip). Persistent uncanny valley: wrong president on TV, rooms entered with forgotten intent, dreams of discontinuity. Philip has spent a decade on the **CVD**—physics-community laughingstock—with lab colleagues including ambitious **Alicia**.

Central trauma: son **Sean** died in an autonomous-vehicle accident; Rebecca's guilt and the couple's mutual failure to help each other structure the book more than gadget spectacle. As experiments proceed, Rebecca's sense that "this world is a dream" intensifies; a Lovability client seems to share the perception. Literary SF about algorithms, race, online intimacy, and the wish to undo grief—ambiguous whether the CVD successfully branches history or only mirrors denial. Pantheon 2016.
