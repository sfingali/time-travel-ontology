## Blurb
American archaeologist Martin Padway is struck to 6th-century Rome and tries to stave off the Dark Ages with distilled liquor, double-entry bookkeeping, and newspapers.

## Detailed plot summary
Padway arrives under Justinian's Gothic War, navigates Belisarius-era politics, introduces technologies and institutions, and alters the trajectory of Western history. Prototype of competent-man timeslip engineering; influential on alternate-history SF.
