## Blurb
Kyoto University third-year relives campus life across parallel club choices; a fortune-teller's "Colosseo" hint and an endless four-and-a-half-mat tatami cosmos reveal that no path yields the rose-colored life—only acceptance of ordinary friendship.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Morimi novel (2004). Unnamed protagonist cycles four timelines by club: Film Ablutions under tyrant Jōgasaki; "Disciples Wanted" with Ozu/Akashi under eccentric Higuchi; Mellow Softball cult-ish club; Lucky Cat Chinese Food underground. Each run rhymes characters and a loose Colosseo prophecy. Final refusal of meetings drops him into the **Tatami Galaxy**—infinite 4.5-mat rooms as alternate timelines; ~80 subjective days until he admits he misses friends and that idealized campus life was never available. Escape = growth without a single "correct" branch.

Anime 2010 (Yuasa); spiritual sequels *Night Is Short, Walk On Girl*; archive-adjacent TV *Tatami Time Machine Blues* (`tv/tatami-time-machine-blues.md`) is a later time-machine riff on the same milieu—cross-link, not identical plot.
