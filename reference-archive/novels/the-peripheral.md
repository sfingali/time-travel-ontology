## Blurb
A future London elite puppets "peripherals" (remote android bodies) in a past that is only a branching stub continuum—until a trailer-park gamer witnesses a murder that yanks both futures into conspiracy.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Dual strand: near-future rural US (**Flynn Fisher**, drone/game operator) and a further-future London after the **Jackpot** (cascading crises). Future operators contact Flynn's world via mysterious server—her "game" is actually a **peripheral** body in their London. Crucial Gibson move: the past they touch is not *their* ancestral past but a **stub**—a freshly branched continuum—so changes there do not erase the operators' present (softens grandfather paradox into colonial multiverse labor).

Flynn witnesses a murder; siblings and veterans entangled with future kleptocrats, PR dynasties, and cops. Themes: economic extraction across time-like branches; body telepresence; class.

Sequel: *Agency* (alternate stub / political branch).
