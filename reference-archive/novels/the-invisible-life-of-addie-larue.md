## Blurb
Addie bargains for freedom in 1714 France and is cursed to be forgotten by everyone she meets—living three centuries until someone remembers her. Included as **borderline**: longevity across eras without timeline interaction or parallel-world contact.

## Detailed plot summary
Addie escapes marriage via a deal with luciferian Luc; she cannot be remembered, photographed, or leave a lasting mark, wandering history as living anonymity. In 2014 New York she meets Henry, who remembers her—revealing mirrored bargains. Romance and art-against-oblivion themes. **Archive note:** Not core time travel or interacting multiverse; retained per scope expansion only if relevant—tagged as long-life curse, not chrono-mechanics.
