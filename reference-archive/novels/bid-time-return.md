## Blurb
Dying screenwriter Richard Collier hypnotizes himself from 1970s Hotel del Coronado into 1896 to love stage actress Elise McKenna—until a modern coin shatters the trance. Filmed as *Somewhere in Time*.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Richard Collier**, 36, inoperable brain tumor, flips a coin and spends last days at **Hotel del Coronado**. Obsessed by a gallery photo of 1890s actress **Elise McKenna**, he researches her life: overprotective manager **William Fawcett Robinson**; never married; brief 1896 affair with a mysterious man. Convinced he *is* that man, he studies time theory and **self-hypnotizes** into 1896 (Matheson method akin to Finney's belief-travel).

Disorientation and fear of snap-back fade. Elise asks "Is it you?"—two psychics foretold him. Romance blooms under period constraints; headaches vanish; he hopes future-memory will fade. Robinson, assuming gold-digging, has Richard abducted while Elise's train departs—but she never left. Hotel-room consummation; Richard steps out, scuffles with Robinson, casually produces a **1971 coin**—anachronism hurls him to the present. He dies soon after. A doctor calls it dying fantasy; brother **Robert** publishes the journal, unconvinced. Elise/McKenna biography draws on actress **Maude Adams**; title from Shakespeare's *Richard II*. World Fantasy Award Best Novel 1976.
