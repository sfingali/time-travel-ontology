## Blurb
WWII nurse Claire Randall touches standing stones at Craigh na Dun and falls into 1743 Scotland, torn between husband Frank and Highlander Jamie Fraser as Jacobite history looms.

## Detailed plot summary
On a second honeymoon in 1945, Claire passes through the stones into clan warfare, British redcoats, and forced marriage to Jamie for protection. She uses medical skill, navigates Black Jack Randall (Frank's ancestor), and chooses to stay in the past. Series continues through Culloden, America, etc. Cultural phenomenon; stones recur as travel mechanism. Genealogy bootstrap tensions with Frank's line.

## Temporal structure
Stone portals at specific sites/times; limited controllable returns in later books.
