## Blurb
Inventor Dan Davis, betrayed into cold sleep, wakes in 2000, then uses a real time machine to go back, set traps for his swindlers, and arrange his own future with engineer Belle and ward Ricky.

## Detailed plot summary
In 1970 engineer Dan Davis is cut out of Hired Girl/Flexible Frank robotics by fiancée Belle and partner Miles; drugged, he is cold-slept to 2000. In the future he finds Flexible Frank ubiquitous, meets doctor/engineer "Doc" Twitchell with a working time machine, and learns cryptic facts about his own past success. He goes back, restructures companies, plants evidence, cold-sleeps again on his own terms, and reunites with grown Ricky (whom he marries). Bootstrap: future knowledge funds past actions that create that future.

## Temporal structure
Cryonics + limited physical time machine; mostly pastward corrective loop. Optimistic engineering romance.
