## Blurb
A Victorian inventor demonstrates a time machine, then recounts a journey to AD 802,701 where humanity has split into the childlike Eloi and subterranean Morlocks, before leaping further to Earth's dying future.

## Detailed plot summary
A nameless Time Traveller hosts dinner guests and shows a miniature machine vanish through time. The next week he returns bedraggled and narrates: he rode the full machine forward, watching ages flash by, and stopped in 802,701. There he meets the Eloi—small, gentle, fruit-eating humanoids living amid decaying grandeur—and rescues Weena from drowning. His machine is stolen and stored inside a Sphinx statue by the Morlocks, ape-like underground dwellers who tend machinery and prey on Eloi by night. Exploring Morlock tunnels and a ruined museum (the Palace of Green Porcelain), he recovers matches and fights his way back; Weena is lost in a forest fire. Using the machine as bait, the Morlocks nearly trap him, but he escapes and jumps millions of years ahead to a red-sun beach with crab-like creatures, then further still to a near-lifeless Earth. He returns to Victorian England with two white flowers as evidence, then departs again and never returns.

## Temporal structure
Frame narrative (Victorian present) → far future (802,701) → further future (~30 million years) → return. One-way exploratory travel via mechanical time machine; no causal loops or timeline branching. Class degeneration allegory projected onto deep time.
