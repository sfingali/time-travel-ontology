## Blurb
Dick Young takes a drug that drops his consciousness into 14th-century Cornwall while his body wanders the present—addiction and medieval intrigue destroy his modern life.

## Detailed plot summary
At a friend's Cornish house, Dick samples a potion and experiences vivid medieval lives (Isolda, Otto Bodrugan, etc.). Each trip risks physical harm in the present; marriage to Vita frays; the drug's inventor Magnus dies. Ends on dependency and uncertain continuation. Psychological timeslip; no machine.

## Temporal structure
Drug-mediated subjective past; body remains in present.
