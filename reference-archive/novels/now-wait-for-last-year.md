## Blurb
Under extraterrestrial war pressure, a toxic drug JJ-180 sends users into alternate pasts/futures; UN secretary Eric Sweetscent tries to save his wife and choose which wartime reality to back.

## Detailed plot summary
Earth is proxy-battleground between Lilistar and reegs. Kathy Sweetscent becomes addicted to JJ-180, which induces time travel with lethal withdrawal. Eric navigates political assassination plots and multiple wartime outcomes, including versions of leader Gino Molinari. Classic Dick: drugs, loyalty, unstable reality. Tags: branching_timeline | reality_rewrite | parallel_worlds.

## Temporal structure
Drug trips into alternate temporalities; political stakes for which war-history solidifies.
