## Blurb
Daniel Eakins inherits a timebelt and multiplies into a society of his own temporal doubles—exploring identity, sex, free will, and eventual exhaustion of possibility.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
1975: college student **Daniel Eakins** is visited by **Uncle Jim**, who raises his allowance if Danny keeps a diary. Jim dies; Danny inherits a **Time-belt**. Short jumps introduce him to a future self calling himself **"Don"**; they win at the races. Next day Danny *is* Don, guiding yesterday's self—first lesson in obligatory role-rotation through a personal loop.

Danny meets ever more alternate Daniels (Don, ultra-Don…). Changes erase traces of his childhood. He forms sexual/romantic relationships with male versions of himself; later jumps to deep past meet a female counterpart **"Diane"** (with her own Aunt Jane / Donna mirrors). Diane becomes pregnant; each secretly uses future tech to force the child's sex they want—splitting timelines so each raises a same-sex child who will grow into themselves (**closed parental loops** for both man and woman).

Aging Daniel realizes he *is* Uncle Jim; his son *is* young Danny. He arranges inheritance so the diary and belt restart the experience. An obsessive deranged variant interferes with access to Diane. Late set-piece: a 1999 house party of dozens of ages of Daniel; an elderly jump-death confronts him with mortality. Ending: young Daniel, diary complete, must choose whether to use the belt.

2003 revised edition inserts later historical disasters (AA191; 9/11) into the timestream chatter.

## Temporal structure
Extreme **bootstrap + personal multiverse**: almost every named character is a Daniel-variant. Second-person shading noted by critics (Lain). Queer identity thought-experiment (Huang/Tor). Less "changewar" than Heinlein loops; more fractal self-society.

```
Jim (=old Daniel) → belt → young Daniel ⇄ Don ⇄ ultra-Don …
                 ↘ Diane/Donna/Jane female loop
                 ↘ son = young Daniel (male closed loop)
```
