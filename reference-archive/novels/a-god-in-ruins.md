## Blurb
Companion to *Life After Life*: Teddy Todd's single long life as WWII bomber pilot, husband, father, grandfather—until a late structural turn reframes what "happened" across Ursula's many lives.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Focuses on **Edward "Teddy" Todd**, Ursula's beloved brother from *Life After Life*, through RAF bombing war, marriage to Nancy (saved in Ursula's loops), fraught parenthood, and old age. Atkinson plays a quieter game than Ursula's rebirths: the novel mostly reads as linear historical fiction until metafictional and timeline-aware gestures near the end reconnect to Ursula's branching cosmology—raising which of Teddy's possible lives "counts" and how fiction itself selects. Costa Novel Award winner. Include as *Life After Life* deepen companion rather than standalone TT engine.

## Temporal structure
Mostly linear lifespan; late reconnection to Ursula's rebirth cosmology. Tags: `companion_branch` | `war_memory`.
