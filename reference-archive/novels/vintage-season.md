## Blurb
Mysteriously refined future tourists rent a house for a "perfect" May—then reveal they travel to savor history's most exquisite disasters. Classic short; basis for *Timescape* film confusion avoided—this is the Moore/Kuttner tale.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Architect **Oliver Wilson** sublets his house for May to a party of strangely beautiful, oddly mannered visitors led by **Kleph** (with Cenbe, Klia, others). They pay extravagantly, speak of "vintage seasons," show advanced curios, and grow ecstatic as local tensions rise toward a catastrophic meteor strike / disaster the city does not yet know is coming. Oliver falls for Kleph; learns too late they are time tourists who collect peak aesthetic moments of destruction the way oenophiles collect vintages. Attempts to warn or alter events fail against their experienced indifference; the disaster arrives as scheduled spectacle. Oliver's world ends as their holiday climax. Frequently anthologized; major influence on "time tourist at disasters" subgenre (*Millennium*, Varley; Doctor Who set pieces).
