## Blurb
Charles Yu, time-machine repairman in Minor Universe 31, hunts his father inside a book that is also a time machine—metafictional grief comedy.

## Detailed plot summary
Narrator Charles services TM-31 units with TAMMY (AI) and dog Ed, enters a loop where he shoots his future self, and reads/writes the manual that contains his story. Father-son physics career regrets structure the paradoxes. Literary SF; hangul and immigrant family subtext.
