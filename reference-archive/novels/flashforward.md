## Blurb
A CERN Higgs run knocks out human consciousness for two minutes; nearly everyone experiences ~21.5 years of their future selves—deaths, marriages, murders—then races to fulfill or escape the visions.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
2009 CERN: physicist **Lloyd Simcoe** and fiancée **Michiko Kamura** (daughter Tamiko) run the LHC for the Higgs. Side effect: global blackout of awareness ~two minutes; during it, people inhabit their senses ~21 years 6 months ahead. Planes and cars crash; Tamiko dies when a vehicle hits her school. No recording devices capture the interval—cited as observer-effect evidence.

Those who saw *nothing* are presumed dead by that future date—including Lloyd's colleague **Theo Procopides**, who races to prevent his own murder. Some suicides follow bleak visions; others hustle to make predicted futures real. Planned repeat run (with warnings) yields *no* flashforward—only the Higgs. Cause isolated: coincident **neutrino pulses** from SN 1987A quark-star remnant starquakes plus collider conditions. Early-warning satellite planned. A billionaire immortality project lists Lloyd.

At the calendar date of the visions, most people see nothing (futures already altered); Lloyd is among few who flash *billions* of years ahead in an artificial immortal body. He refuses the philanthropist's offer without wife Doreen—changing that future; implication the treatment may go to Theo. Theo contacts Michiko on long-held feelings.

Discusses free will, Copenhagen vs transactional interpretation, Omega Point. Loosely adapted as ABC *FlashForward* (2009–10) with major changes (FBI leads; 6-month not 21-year offset; terrorist framing).

## Temporal structure
```
Collider + neutrino pulse --> 2-min awareness gap

```
