## Blurb
A Tang-dynasty Chinese mandarin slips into 1980s Munich and writes home—epistolary satire of modern Europe.

## Detailed plot summary
Official Shi from 10th-century China finds himself in contemporary Munich; letters 'home' describe cars, bureaucracy, art, sex, and Cold War Europe with anthropological bemusement. Best-selling German comic TT; English often *Letters Back to Ancient China*.

## Temporal structure
One-way timeslip; satire via outsider gaze; minimal paradox machinery.
