## Blurb
Archaeology students in Israel find evidence a modern camcorder filmed the historical Jesus—and parties race to control the tape.

## Detailed plot summary
During a dig, students uncover clues that a 21st-century video camera recorded Jesus. Thriller chase among churches, intelligence services, and believers; Eschbach's breakout. Sequel *Der Jesus-Deal* (2014).

## Temporal structure
Object-from-future in past; hunt plot more than physics lecture.
