## Blurb
His Dark Materials #3: multiverse war against the Authority, harrowing of the dead, mulefa Dust ecology, and the forced closing of almost all Subtle Knife windows—including the path between Lyra's and Will's Oxfords.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Prophecy names **Lyra** second Eve; Magisterium orders her death. **Mrs Coulter** hides drugged Lyra in a cave; Lyra dreams of dead friend **Roger** and vows help. In Cittàgazze, angels **Balthamos** and **Baruch** urge **Will** to bring the Subtle Knife to **Lord Asriel**; Will insists on finding Lyra first. Escape from **Metatron** via knife windows. Angels explain: first angel from Dust falsely claimed Creator status as the **Authority**; aged, he delegated to regent Metatron.

**Mary Malone** reaches the **mulefa** world; lacquer spyglass lets her see **Dust** streaming away, starving the wheel-trees. Will + **Iorek** free Lyra as Magisterium attacks; knife breaks, Iorek reforges it. Lyra and Will enter the **land of the dead**—dæmons stripped at the border (Will's becomes visible). They bargain with harpies, open an exit window; the dead dissolve joyfully into the universe.

Asriel's republic battles Magisterium. Coulter allies then betrays Metatron; she, Asriel, and Metatron fall into an abyss. Lyra and Will free the demented Authority from a crystal litter; wind dissolves him. In mulefa world Mary recounts leaving holy orders after love revealed an empty heaven; Lyra and Will's mutual confession / kiss gathers Dust. Witch **Serafina** explains: each window births Spectres and drains Dust—**all windows must close** except the dead's exit. Lyra and Will cannot survive long outside home worlds; midsummer Botanic Garden bench vow; Will breaks the knife. Lyra loses intuitive alethiometry, chooses academic study, resolves to build the Republic of Heaven *in her own world*.

Whitbread Book of the Year (first children's winner); BBC/HBO series 3 (2022). US edition softens some sexual-awakening passages.
