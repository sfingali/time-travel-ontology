## Blurb
Future humans snatch plane-crash victims before impact—until a present-day crash investigator intersects the operation.

## Detailed plot summary
Far-future survivors (sterile, damaged) steal doomed passengers via time gates to restock. Present investigator Bill Smith overlaps a gate failure. Film *Millennium* (1989). Body horror + bureaucracy of salvation.

## Temporal structure
Future→past extraction; contamination paradoxes.
