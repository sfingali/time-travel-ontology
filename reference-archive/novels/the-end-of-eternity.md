## Blurb
Technician Andrew Harlan implements "Minimum Necessary Changes" for Eternity—an organization outside time that edits history for safety—until love and a woman from the Hidden Centuries force him to choose Infinity over Eternity.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Humanity builds **Eternity**: a corridor outside physio-time whose members (Eternals) ride **kettles** "upwhen/downwhen," observe centuries, and enact **Minimum Necessary Changes (M.N.C.)**—tiny interventions that rewrite entire realities to reduce suffering. Trade between centuries props up needy eras. Cost: art, ambition, and galactic expansion are repeatedly pruned. Eternals cannot enter before the 27th-century **downwhen terminus**. From roughly the 70,000th–150,000th centuries stretch the **Hidden Centuries**; by ~150,000th century Earth is empty.

**Andrew Harlan**, Technician specializing in Primitive history, is ordered by Senior Computer **Laban Twissell** to tutor **Brinsley Sheridan Cooper**. Parallel assignment from Assistant Computer **Finge**: a week in the 482nd with aristocrat **Noÿs Lambent**. Harlan falls in love; discovers an impending reality change will erase her as she is; illegally hides her in empty Eternity sections inside the Hidden Centuries.

A mysterious **kettle block** at the 100,000th century prevents return. Harlan confronts Twissell: he has deduced Cooper is destined to *become* **Vikkor Mallansohn**, inventor of the temporal field—Eternity's founding is a **bootstrap**. Twissell confirms Mallansohn's memoirs: Cooper must be sent ignorant into the 24th so the loop closes. Harlan tries blackmail; Twissell forces him into the control room with only Cooper's send-lever live. Enraged, Harlan sabotages power; Cooper lands in the early 20th instead.

Eternity still exists, so repair may be possible. They hunt a Primitive magazine for Cooper's signal: a 1932 ad whose acrostic **ATOM** marks him. Harlan withholds until Noÿs is retrieved. They pass the block and find her. Harlan insists she accompany the 1932 retrieval.

In 1932 Harlan holds Noÿs at gunpoint: she is **from the Hidden Centuries**. Her people see *many* futures. In every future where Eternity persists, safety-first editing leaves humanity Earthbound until other species own the galaxy; humans decline into extinction. Her mission: the minimal change that **prevents Eternity's founding**, chosen among paths that keep Harlan with her. Remembering Eternals' sterile culture and people whose homewhens were erased, Harlan spares her. Reality changes; the kettle vanishes—"**the end of Eternity – and the beginning of Infinity**."
