## Blurb
Time Safari Inc. takes hunters to the Mesozoic on a floating Path; Eckels panics, steps off, crushes a butterfly—and returns to a Deutscher dictatorship and altered English.

## Detailed plot summary
2055: Eckels pays to hunt a T. rex tagged to die naturally. Guides Travis and Lesperance enforce Path rules. Eckels flees the Path into mud; the group kills the dinosaur, makes him retrieve bullets, returns. Orthography, air, and politics have shifted—dictator Deutscher won. Dead butterfly on Eckels's boot; Travis fires ("a sound of thunder"). Popularizer of literary butterfly-effect causality (term later from Lorenz meteorology).

## Temporal structure
Pastward tourism with strict conservation; tiny change cascades to present rewrite. Tags: branching_timeline | reality_rewrite.
