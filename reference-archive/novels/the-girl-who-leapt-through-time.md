## Blurb
Middle-schooler Kazuko Yoshiyama faints on a lavender scent in the science lab and begins leaping 24 hours at a time—until the "friend" Kazuo is revealed as Ken Sogoru, a time traveler from AD 2660 whose drug caused the accident.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Kazuko Yoshiyama**, third-year middle school, cleans the science lab with classmates **Kazuo Fukamachi** and **Gorō Asakura**; smells lavender-like scent; faints for three days. Subsequent events: earthquake and fire at Gorō's house. Next morning, at the exact moment of a car accident, she is flung **24 hours into the past**.

She relives the day, warns friends; they disbelief until her quake/fire prediction and Gorō's near truck-hit prove her. Science teacher **Fukushima** frames the ability as teleportation / **time-leap**. To solve the origin she must leap back four days. Determination enables the jump: in the science room she meets a man wearing Kazuo's identity—actually **"Ken Sogoru"** from **AD 2660**. His intersection with her life is accidental fallout from a time-leaping drug. Ken stays a month; Kazuko falls in love; when he leaves he erases everyone's memories of him. Closing image: faint memory of a promised reunion whenever she smells lavender.

Scholar Ulrich Heinze: first fully formed Japanese TT-as-self-exploration, limiting jumps to adolescence rather than galactic expedition; early time-loop design anticipating *Groundhog Day*-style repetition. Massive adaptation lineage (see below).
