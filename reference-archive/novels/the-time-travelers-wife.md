## Blurb
Henry DeTamble's genetic Chrono-Impairment flings him naked through his own timeline; Clare Abshire grows up visited by future-Henry, then must wait through absences, miscarriages, and the foreknown death that ends their marriage's present.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Alternating first-person: librarian **Henry** (b. 1963) and artist **Clare** (b. 1971). Henry's **Chrono-Impairment** causes involuntary jumps tied to subconscious anchors; stress triggers travel; he arrives naked, surviving via lockpicking, theft, self-defense—skills often taught by older selves (**bootstrap**). He cannot bring objects.

Their timelines first "naturally" meet at Newberry Library in 1991 when Clare is 20 and Henry 28—yet Clare has known him since childhood: from 1977 (Clare age 6) onward, future-Henry visits her Michigan home with a date-list so she can stock clothes/food. He accidentally reveals their future marriage. On her 18th birthday (1989) they first have sex; then two years' gap until the library meeting.

Marriage; repeated miscarriages (fetus likely inherits the disorder); Henry's vasectomy; a past-Henry visit impregnates Clare with daughter **Alba**, who has partial control of her own jumps. Future-Henry meets 10-year-old Alba and learns he dies when she is five. At 43, frostbite from a winter garage jump costs Henry's feet—without running ability, later jumps are fatal. New Year's Eve 2006 he jumps to 1984 Michigan woods and is accidentally shot by Clare's brother (foreshadowed). He dies in Clare's arms. Letter tells her to stop waiting—yet also describes a future reunion. Final scene: 82-year-old Clare meets 43-year-old Henry; the book closes on a lifetime of waiting.

~2.5M copies by 2009; Boeke Prize; British Book Award. Film (2009); HBO series (2022, cancelled); West End musical.
