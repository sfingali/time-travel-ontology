## Blurb
Astronaut Hal Bregg returns after 10 subjective years to an Earth 127 years older—betrizated, risk-averse, alien to him.

## Detailed plot summary
Mission to Fomalhaut: time dilation makes Earth age ~127 years while Hal ages ~10. Society abolished aggression via **betrization**; spaceflight is childish risk. Returning astronauts are 'Neanderthals.' Utopia as exile. **Relativistic one-way TT** as social novel. English 1980.

## Temporal structure
Special-relativistic forward travel; no return to past.
