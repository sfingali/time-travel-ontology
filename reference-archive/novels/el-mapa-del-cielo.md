## Blurb
Victorian trilogy #2: H. G. Wells again central as *War of the Worlds* invasion theater and the 1835 Great Moon Hoax lineage spiral—dreams become nightmares when staged wonders turn unexpectedly real.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Plaza & Janés / EN *The Map of the Sky* (2012). Continues Palma's metafictional Victorian mode: Wells entangled with Martian-invasion spectacle; dual strands link New York and London deceptions to Emma's ancestral Moon Hoax heritage. Time-travel / temporal illusion motifs from *El mapa del tiempo* recur amid steampunk intrigue and questions of desire made manifest. Premio Ignotus Best Novel 2013.
