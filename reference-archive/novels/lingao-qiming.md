## Blurb
500+ modern Chinese castaways wormhole into 1628 Ming Hainan and industrialize history—flagship *chuanyue* collaborative epic.

## Detailed plot summary
Online Qidian origin (2009); print vols from 2017. Settlers in Lingao County build industry, institutions, and alt-history Ming outcomes. Collective engineering fantasy; cousin to Russian *popadantsy* at Chinese webnovel scale.
