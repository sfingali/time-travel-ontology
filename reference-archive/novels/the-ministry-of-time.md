## Blurb
A British civil servant becomes "bridge" to Victorian Arctic explorer Graham Gore, pulled from the 1845 Franklin expedition into a near-future Ministry that collects "expats" from history.

## Detailed plot summary
Narrator (unnamed in marketing often described as mixed-race civil servant) houses Gore; other expats from plague and war eras adjust to modern life. Romance, spy-thriller reveals about the Ministry's true agenda, and questions of empire, race, and who owns time. Bestseller; zeitgeist literary time-travel of mid-2020s.

## Temporal structure
Institutional retrieval from fixed past points; bridges monitor change risk.
