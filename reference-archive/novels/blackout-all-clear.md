## Blurb
Published as two volumes, this Hugo-winning WWII epic sends Oxford historians into the Blitz—only for retrievals to fail as they realize they may be the agents who make history happen.

## Detailed plot summary
Michael Davies, Merope (Eileen), and Polly Churchill drop into 1940 Britain (Dunkirk, children-evacuation, London Blitz). Drops and returns malfunction; the historians become air-raid wardens, shopgirls, and Dunkirk helpers, fearing their presence endangers the Allied victory. Over hundreds of pages they discover that "coincidences" saving them are often each other, and that historians from the future are already woven into the war's survival. Dual-volume structure; combined Hugo Award for Best Novel.

## Temporal structure
Oxford net; prolonged stranding; bootstrap of historical outcomes via future observers.
