## Blurb
Professor Henry Hassel builds a time machine to murder his wife's past lovers—and history's great figures—only to learn he is erasing himself from everyone else's reality.

## Detailed plot summary
Discovering his wife's affairs, Hassel kills her grandfather, then Mohammed, Columbus, etc., returning each time to a wife who still smiles. A fellow researcher explains: changing the past only moves the traveler onto a private worldline; the public universe ignores him. Savage short satire of revenge and grandfather paradoxes. Tags: branching_timeline (personal) | reality_rewrite (subjective).
