## Blurb
Funiculi Funicula sequel: four new customers use the haunted seat—best friend dead 22 years, missed mother's funeral, unmarriageable love, detective's gift for a late wife—under the same coffee-timer rules that forbid changing the present.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
JP 2017 (Sunmark); EN *Before the Coffee Gets Cold: Tales from the Cafe* (tr. Geoffrey Trousselot). Same Tokyo basement café, ghost-occupied seat, Kazu/Nagare staff, immutable present, ~1h coffee limit.

Four visitors (per Picador marketing / reviews): a man revisits his best friend who died 22 years earlier; a son who missed his mother's funeral; a man who sees the woman he could not marry; an aging detective bringing something for his late wife. Emotional closure without causal rewrite; series expands the ghost-waitress lore and staff backstory. Continues directly from *Before the Coffee Gets Cold* (2015).
