## Blurb
Future historians of Pastwatch, studying the Columbus voyage via temporal viewers, conclude they must send agents back to rewrite first contact and avert a future catastrophe.

## Detailed plot summary
In a resource-scarce future, Pastwatch scholars replay history. Tagiri and colleagues focus on Columbus and Atlantic slavery's long consequences; they discover prior interventions and eventually authorize a one-way mission to divert Columbus's project toward a different Atlantic history. Moral debate over changing the past vs responsibility for descendants. Tags: branching_timeline | reality_rewrite.

## Temporal structure
Viewing technology first; then limited physical intervention.
