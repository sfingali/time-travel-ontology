# Wrong Place Wrong Time (2022)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Reverse-chronology jump maps (2022 → 2003 inception) in reader explainers; CTC / “inception of the crime” physics chats inside the novel.

## Blurb
Lawyer Jen Brotherhood watches her 18-year-old son Todd stab a stranger on Halloween night. She falls asleep and wakes *yesterday*—then keeps drifting further into the past. To stop the murder she must find its root cause decades earlier.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**30 Oct 2022, Liverpool:** Jen sees Todd kill Joseph Jones outside their home. After arrest trauma she sleeps and wakes on the day *before*—murder unhappened. Further sleeps fling her backward by days, then years. Physicist Andy (via friend Rakesh) frames a **closed timelike curve**: she must reach the crime’s inception.

Investigation reveals husband “Kelly” is ex-undercover officer **Ryan Hiles**; Jen’s late father leaked empty-house addresses to criminals. In 2003 a botched theft kidnapped baby **Eve Green**; Joseph Jones’s path through prison and revenge traces to that night. Todd’s violence was twisted protection after learning Ryan’s secrets when Joseph returned.

Jen reaches **12 Jan 2003**, tips police, stops the kidnapping. Timeline rewrites: she returns to a 2022 where Ryan is a legitimate cop, Joseph never seeks revenge, Todd is not a killer, and Todd’s partner is grown Eve Green. Bittersweet coda in some summaries: another mother begins her own loop—costs transferred, not erased from the universe.

## Paradox analysis
**Whydunnit** time thriller: cause sought in past, not perpetrator punished in present. Soft branching—successful intervention replaces Jen’s world. Diegetic bootstrap/CTC talk; emotional engine is maternal sacrifice across decades of family secrets.
