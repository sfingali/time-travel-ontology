# All You Need Is Kill (2004)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Loop-count / Jacket training progressions and Mimic Server–Antenna ecology charts appear in adaptation explainers comparing novel vs *Edge of Tomorrow*.

## Blurb
UDF recruit Keiji Kiriya dies on his first Mimic sortie—and wakes the morning before, again and again. Skill stacking makes him a monster soldier; fellow looper Rita Vrataski holds the key to ending the Mimics’ temporal weapon—at a cost the Hollywood film refused.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Earth’s United Defense Force fights exoskeleton (“Jacket”) war against alien **Mimics**. Green recruit **Keiji Kiriya** is killed after destroying an unusual Mimic, then resets to the prior morning. Each death–reset loop trains him: weapons, tactics, Jacket mastery. After dozens of loops he recognizes kinship with ace **Rita Vrataski** (“Full Metal Bitch”), who once looped herself and now fights with a battle axe because guns jam/empty.

Rita and mechanic confide Mimic temporal biology: dying Mimics can signal the past; groups include nexus nodes that loop the day and **antenna** Mimics. Humans who absorb the signal become antennas. Keiji and Rita hunt the central intelligence (Server/Omega-class in various summaries).

### Ending (novel ≠ film)
They destroy the Server, but Rita has become an antenna—so the loop ecology can continue while she lives. She forces a duel: if Keiji dies, eternal loop; if she dies, loop ends and war can be won. Keiji kills Rita, survives as decorated hero—tragic inversion of *Edge of Tomorrow*’s Cage/Rita mutual survival ending.

Viz/Haikasoru EN edition; Yoshitoshi Abe illustrations; manga adaptation; 2014 Liman film heavily alters rules (blood transfusion loses power) and tone.

## Paradox analysis
Combat **Groundhog Day** with alien physics justification. Not branching multiverse—single day overwritten. Antenna rule creates zero-sum between loopers: only one can remain if the signal path must close. Film softens to shared victory.
