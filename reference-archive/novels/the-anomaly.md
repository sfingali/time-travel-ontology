## Blurb
Prix Goncourt: a Paris–NY flight exists twice—June and March versions of the same passengers must share one world.

## Detailed plot summary
Air France 006 lands in March; an identical flight/passengers appears in June. Governments quarantine; doubles confront lovers, crimes, art, and statistics. Philosophical comedy of **parallel selves forced to interact**.

## Temporal structure
Single duplicative event → interacting doubles (not a machine).
