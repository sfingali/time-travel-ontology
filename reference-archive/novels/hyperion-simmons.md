## Blurb
Seven pilgrims cross to Outback world Hyperion to petition the Shrike at the Time Tombs—structures moving backward through time—each tale revealing personal entanglement with anti-entropic mystery and coming Ouster war.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
~2732: **Hegemony of Man** linked by farcasters; uneasy alliance with AI **TechnoCore**; **Ousters** in interstellar conflict. Outback planet **Hyperion** hosts **Time Tombs** moving *backward* in time, guarded by metallic **Shrike**. On eve of Ouster invasion, final pilgrimage; pilgrims tell Canterbury-style tales:

1. **Priest (Hoyt/Duré):** Cruciform parasites grant degraded immortality among Bikura; Duré electrocutes himself on a tesla tree for years; Hoyt inherits cruciform pain.
2. **Soldier (Kassad):** FORCE colonel; simulation lover **Moneta** is Shrike-linked; time-altered combat on Hyperion; seeks to kill Shrike to prevent engineered interstellar slaughter.
3. **Poet (Silenus):** Immortal-ish foul poet; Shrike as muse; Sad King Billy taken; must finish *Hyperion Cantos*.
4. **Scholar (Weintraub):** Daughter **Rachel** ages *backward* after Shrike encounter at Tombs (Merlin sickness); Binding-of-Isaac dreams demand sacrifice.
5. **Detective (Lamia):** Cybrid **Johnny** (Keats personality); Core UI god-project factions; pregnant with Johnny's child; Shrike Church asylum → pilgrimage.
6. **Consul:** Grandparents' time-dilation love on Maui-Covenant; Consul triggered Ouster device emptying Tombs / releasing Shrike as revenge on Hegemony.
7. **Templar Het Masteen** vanishes overnight in bloody cabin after treeship destruction.

Epilogue: pilgrims walk toward glowing Tombs; narrative cuts—continued in *Fall of Hyperion*. Hugo + Locus 1990.
