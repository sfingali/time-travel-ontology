## Blurb
Will Parry finds a window into Cittàgazze and claims Æsahættr—the Subtle Knife that cuts between worlds—while Lyra's Dust quest becomes fully multiversal war against the Authority.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Twelve-year-old **Will Parry** (Oxford, our world) cares for his mentally ill mother; after accidentally killing an intruder searching for his missing explorer-father's letters, he flees and discovers a window into **Cittàgazze**, a ruined city haunted by soul-eating **Spectres** that ignore children. There he meets **Lyra Silvertongue** and dæmon **Pantalaimon**, who arrived via Lord Asriel's sky-breach from *Northern Lights*.

**Mrs Coulter** tortures a witch for a prophecy about Lyra; **Serafina Pekkala** kills the victim before details emerge and seeks Lyra. Aeronaut **Lee Scoresby** hunts rumored-dead **Stanislaus Grumman** for an object that could protect Lyra.

Will returns home seeking father-clues; Lyra visits physicist **Dr Mary Malone**, whose dark-matter computer communicates with Dust-analogues. Con man **Sir Charles Latrom** steals Lyra's alethiometer; he will return it for a powerful knife in Cittàgazze. Will fights a youth for the knife, loses two fingers—the mark of the new bearer—and learns from dying holder **Giacomo Paradisi** that Æsahættr cuts any matter *and* the fabric between worlds. They recover the alethiometer; Lyra recognizes Latrom as **Lord Boreal** from her world. Will overhears that his father found interworld windows.

Malone refuses Boreal's funding; Dust-consciousness tells her to destroy her computer, cross Will's window, and "play the serpent." Scoresby finds Grumman living as shaman **Jopari**—actually **John Parry**, Will's father—who must reach the knife-bearer and aid Asriel's rebellion against angelic **Authority**. Soldiers force them down; Scoresby dies covering Grumman's escape after calling Serafina (who arrives too late).

Mrs Coulter poisons Boreal, controls Spectres, and learns the prophecy: Lyra is the **second Eve**; she plans to destroy her daughter rather than risk another Fall. Will meets his father, who binds his wound and directs him to Asriel; a vengeful witch kills Grumman. Angels wait to guide Will. He returns to camp to find Lyra kidnapped and witch-guardians' souls Spectre-drained—cliffhanger into *The Amber Spyglass*.
