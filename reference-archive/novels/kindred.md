## Blurb
Black writer Dana Franklin is repeatedly yanked from 1976 Los Angeles to an antebellum Maryland plantation whenever her white ancestor Rufus Weylin's life is endangered—forced to keep him alive so she herself can be born.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Prologue:** Dana wakes in hospital with her left arm amputated at the wall-line where Rufus held her; police suspect husband **Kevin**; they cannot tell the truth.

**The River (1976 bicentennial birthday):** Unpacking a new house, Dana vanishes to a riverside where red-haired boy **Rufus** is drowning; she saves him; a white man aims a gun; dizziness returns her minutes later (subjective hours).

**The Fire:** She returns to young Rufus setting curtains ablaze; learns she is in ~1815 Maryland. Rufus casually uses racial slurs. She shelters with free Black **Alice Greenwood**—and realizes Alice and Rufus are her ancestors (their child Hagar will found Dana's maternal line). She witnesses Alice's father whipped for lacking papers; a white man attempts to rape Dana; terror returns her to 1976. She and Kevin pack survival kit and research free-papers forgery.

**The Fall:** Kevin grips her during a transfer; they arrive to Rufus's broken leg. They prove future-origin via coin dates; Kevin poses as her owner and accepts tutoring work. Weeks of plantation cruelty follow; Weylin whips Dana for teaching slave boy **Nigel** to read; she returns alone—Kevin stranded five years in the past.

**The Fight:** Dana finds adult Rufus being beaten by Alice's husband **Isaac** after attempted rape. She saves Rufus; Alice and Isaac flee; capture brings Isaac's mutilation/sale and Alice's enslavement. Rufus buys Alice and forces Dana to nurse her and persuade her into concubinage. Rufus withholds Dana's letters to Kevin; Dana flees, is betrayed, whipped. Kevin arrives; Rufus threatens them; they escape to 1976 together. Kevin is cold, scarred by five years of abolitionist underground work.

**The Storm:** Dana finds drunk Rufus drowning in rain; nurses malaria with smuggled aspirin. Rufus and Alice have surviving children **Joe** and later **Hagar**. After Tom Weylin's heart-attack death, Rufus punishes Dana in the fields, sells slaves, uses her writing for creditors. When Rufus hits her over another sale, she slits her wrists to force a return.

**The Rope:** Alice tries to flee after Dana vanishes; Rufus lies that he sold the children; Alice hangs herself. Rufus wants Dana as Alice's replacement. When he attempts rape, Dana stabs him twice; as he dies gripping her arm, she returns—arm fused into the wall and torn off.

**Epilogue:** Archival scraps show house fire (Nigel covering murder) and slave sale omitting Nigel, Carrie, Joe, Hagar. Dana feels responsible; Kevin says the past cannot be fixed now that Rufus is dead.

Butler called it "grim fantasy"; foundational neo-slave narrative + involuntary time travel.
