## Blurb
Indestructible monuments celebrating warlord Kuin's victories begin materializing twenty years *before* those victories—turning prophecy into self-fulfilling geopolitics. Campbell Memorial co-winner; Hugo nominee.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Software designer **Scott Warden**, between contracts in early-21st-c. Thailand with family, and friend **Hitch Paley** find a jungle monolith of unknown indestructible matter inscribed to commemorate a military victory by **"Kuin"**—dated twenty years in the future. Over two decades, grander chronoliths appear across Asia then the world. Pro- and anti-Kuin movements, cults, economic collapse, and open war follow.

Scott rejoins mentor physicist **Sue Chopra**'s research team predicting appearances (Jerusalem sighting). Daughter **Kaitlin** joins a pro-Kuin youth cult; searching, Scott meets **Ashlee**, whose son **Adam Mills** joined the same cult. In Mexico a chronolith appearance seemingly kills Adam; Scott quits Sue, marries Ashlee.

Sue theorizes Kuin sends chronoliths specifically to inspire fear and pre-emptive surrender—making future victories inevitable by recruiting the present. Adam is alive leading worshippers; Sue recruits Scott to destroy the first chronolith predicted on US soil. It appears then **self-destructs**—sabotaged by a maker who exceeded tech limits. Adam's militants kidnap Sue; before capture she tells Scott she believes her imprisonment is the path to sabotage that chronolith. Twenty-year war follows; no physical Kuin appears—Scott notes Adam's middle name **Quinn**, closest thing to a real Kuin. Scott survives to see Kuinism collapse and a scientific renaissance from Chopra's research; with Kaitlin he watches an interstellar probe launch apparently based on chronolith tech.

Inverse time travel: future matter/info colonizes the present.
