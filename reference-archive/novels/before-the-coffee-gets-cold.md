## Blurb
Tokyo café Funiculi Funicula lets customers time-travel from one haunted seat—if they return before the coffee gets cold, meet only prior café visitors, and accept that the present will not change.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Began as a 2010 play; novel 2015 (Sunmark); EN tr. Geoffrey Trousselot (Picador 2019). Rules: only one seat works; available when its ghost occupant toilets; traveler cannot leave the seat; can meet only people who have visited the café; **whatever happens in the past, the present does not change**; must return before coffee cools (~one hour) or become a ghost.

Four customers, guided by barista **Kazu** and staff:

1. **Fumiko** — businesswoman seeks to repair things with boyfriend who left for a US job.
2. **Kohtake** — nurse seeks a letter from her Alzheimer's-stricken husband.
3. **Hirai** — bar owner confronts estranged sister she has avoided.
4. **Kei** — café co-owner travels *to the future* to meet her yet-unborn daughter.

Through constrained visits the book argues: past unalterable in outcome, yet conversations can still transform how one faces an open future. Sequels: *Tales from the Cafe* (2017); *Before Your Memory Fades* (2018); *Before We Say Goodbye* (2021); *Before We Forget Kindness* (2023/EN 2024); *Before I Knew I Loved You* (JP; EN reported 2026). Film *Cafe Funiculi Funicula* (2018, Kasumi Arimura); TV series development announced.
