## Blurb
Spanish novel predating Wells: Don Sindulfo's Anacronópete flies around Earth to travel time—often credited as the first time-machine novel.

## Detailed plot summary
At the 1878 Exposition, Don Sindulfo García unveils the **Anacronópete**, traveling backward by circling Earth. Motives: control of niece Clara; friend Benjamín seeks immortality in ancient China. Insulating fluid prevents rejuvenation erasure. Satirical stops critique eurocentrism. Written c.1881 Macao; published Barcelona 1887. English: *The Time Ship: A Chrononautical Journey*.

## Temporal structure
Mechanical time travel before Wells 1895; humor-social critique more than paradox logic.
