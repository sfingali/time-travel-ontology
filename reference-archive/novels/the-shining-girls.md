## Blurb
A house in Chicago lets Harper Curtis drift across the 20th century murdering "shining girls"—until one survivor hunts him through time.

## Detailed plot summary
Depression-era Harper finds a house that opens to other decades; he stalks gifted women across years, leaving trophies. Kirby Mazrachi survives an attack in the 1980s and, with reporter Dan, pieces together anachronistic evidence. Nonlinear serial-killer structure. Tags: machine/house portal | closed loops of predation.

## Temporal structure
House as multidirectional gate; hunter and prey cross decades.
