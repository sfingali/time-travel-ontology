## Blurb
Physicist-turned-professor Jason Dessen is kidnapped into a parallel Chicago where he never married—and must fight other selves across the multiverse to reclaim his family.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Jason Dessen** teaches college physics in Chicago, having once been a rising quantum researcher; he chose marriage to artist **Daniela** and son **Charlie** over a career-defining path. Abducted and drugged, he wakes inside a metal **cube** in a lab—an alternate Chicago where he *did* prioritize career, built the cube, and never married Daniela.

Lab staff believe he is **Jason2** returning from the first successful inter-world trip. In truth Jason2 visited Jason's world, envied the family life, and swapped places—stranding Jason in Jason2's world so Jason2 can inhabit Daniela and Charlie's lives.

Jason and Jason2's therapist **Amanda** escape into the cube. Navigation is not dial-a-world: subjective state / decisions steer which branch they enter. They traverse dystopias and utopias. Eventually Jason reaches *his* Chicago, finds Jason2 installed with his family, convinces Daniela and Charlie of his identity, and hides. Jason2 attacks; Jason mortally wounds him.

But every decision Jason made while cube-traveling forked **more Jasons**—a swarm of variants hunting the "original" domestic life. Jason flees again with Daniela and Charlie into the cube to start over in some other world.

Mechanism: many-worlds interpretation made navigable; selves can **invade** each other's worlds. Tags: `multiverse_contact` | `parallel_worlds`.

## Temporal structure
```
[Jason-home] --abduct--> [Jason2-lab world]
                cube hops: W1, W2, ... Wn
         return --> confront Jason2
         flee --> open set of remaining worlds
         meanwhile: decision-forked Jason-swarm
```
Not classical time travel; **interacting** parallel presents. Apple TV+ adaptation expands world-maps episode by episode.
