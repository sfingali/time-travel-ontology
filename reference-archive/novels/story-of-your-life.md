## Blurb
Linguist Louise Banks learns Heptapod B and begins to experience her daughter's whole life—including her death—as simultaneous, reframing free will and motherhood.

## Detailed plot summary
Aliens ("heptapods") arrive; Louise and physicist Gary Donnelly decode their language. Heptapod B's nonlinear written form rewires Louise's cognition toward a teleological, block-universe perception: she "remembers" future events including choosing to have a daughter who will die young. The story intercuts first-contact procedure with second-person addresses to the child. Nebula Award winner; basis for *Arrival*. Not a machine time traveler—linguistic access to all times at once.

## Temporal structure
Block-universe phenomenology via language; past/present/future co-present subjectively. No paradox engineering.
