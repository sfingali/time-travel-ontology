## Blurb
Enemy agents Red and Blue braid history across mutually exclusive empires, leaving taunting then loving letters—until a poisoned message, a faked death, and a childhood immunity rewrite turn the Time War into shared desertion.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Red** and **Blue** are temporal operatives for warring empires whose desired histories cannot coexist; they travel strands of the braid altering pasts. In secret they leave messages—taunts → flirtation → love. Red's commander detects contact and forces Red to send a letter that will **poison Blue** when read. Red warns her; Blue reads anyway to protect Red from treason charges and apparently dies.

Listless Red finds a hint that Blue faked death. She revisits each correspondence node, collects Blue's DNA traces, infiltrates Blue's empire, and grants childhood Blue **immunity** to the poison (an incident foreshadowed in an earlier letter). Discovery: Red arrested by her own side; eve of execution, Blue's final letter—prison break, invitation to fight *both* empires together.

Formal method: Gladstone wrote all Red letters; El-Mohtar all Blue; reactions composed with genuine surprise. Nebula, Hugo, Locus, BSFA, Ignyte, Aurora wins. 2023 viral "bigolas dickolas" tweets rocketed sales. TV option announced; authors insist genders not negotiable.
