## Blurb
In medieval Baghdad/Cairo, a merchant hears nested tales of a shop gate that opens twenty years forward or back—and learns that what is written is written.

## Detailed plot summary
Frame: Fuwaad ibn Abbas petitions the Caliph after using Bashaarat's gate. Nested stories (Hassan the rope-maker; Ajib the merchant) show travelers seeking to change fates; outcomes reveal self-consistency—actions in the past were always part of history. Fuwaad's own trip to see his dead wife yields understanding rather than alteration. Hugo and Nebula winner. Islamic Golden Age setting; elegant predestination.

## Temporal structure
Fixed twenty-year gates; nested narratives; Novikov-like consistency.
