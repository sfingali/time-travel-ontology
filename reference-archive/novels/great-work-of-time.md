## Blurb
Caspar Last sells a time machine and a unique Guiana stamp to the Otherhood—a secret society that edits history to preserve and expand the British Empire—until contradictory Winterset missions force the "true" catastrophic twentieth century to reassert itself.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Caspar Last** invents a time machine, travels to 19th-c. British Guiana for the unique **1c magenta** stamp, plans never to use time travel again. Shadowy **Otherhood** buys stamp and machine to preserve the Empire. New timeline: Empire dominates the twentieth century.

**Denys Winterset**, rising Colonial Service officer in 1950s Africa, rides Cape-to-Cairo rail, meets a stranger, joins Otherhood. Mission: travel to 1893 and assassinate founder **Cecil Rhodes** before Rhodes changes his will and dissipates the fortune that funds Otherhood—otherwise the original timeline returns.

In another strand, a different Winterset manifestation travels *forward* (forbidden) and learns excessive tinkering spawned countless unintended worlds. Future citizens beg him to *stop* the Rhodes assassination and restore the "true" timeline.

1893 Cape Town: Winterset gains Rhodes's trust; at the kill-moment a mysterious force (implied future Winterset) blocks the trigger. Mission fails; Winterset is trapped serving Rhodes, witnessing brutal colony-building. **Original Situation** reasserts: two World Wars, Empire breakup.

Final chapter: young Winterset in "true" mid-century history joins the doomed Colonial Service; meets older self in 1956 Africa; helps him escape chaos; last London meeting years later. Older Winterset notes *Times* sale of the 1856 Guiana magenta once owned by Otherhood in 1956—time travel means the story remains rewrite-vulnerable.

World Fantasy Award Best Novella (1990). Critic Susan Young notes structural kinship to Asimov's *End of Eternity* (recruit → founding change → future actors shut the apparatus down).

## Temporal structure
```
Last's machine --> Otherhood Empire-optimal timeline
Winterset-A: kill Rhodes to *preserve* Otherhood loop
Winterset-B (future): stop kill to *erase* Otherhood
Failed assassination --> Original Situation returns
Stamp provenance --> residual vulnerability
```
