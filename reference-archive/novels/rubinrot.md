## Blurb
London YA: Gwyneth Shepherd unexpectedly inherits the female time-travel gene meant for cousin Charlotte, is bound to Guardians' chronograph with arrogant partner Gideon, and stumbles into Count Saint-Germain's twelve-blood conspiracy.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Gwyneth** (16) time-slips uncontrolled; cousin **Charlotte Montrose** was raised as the destined carrier. Mother forged birth papers to hide Gwen from the **Guardians**. After verified jumps (including seeing her double kiss a stranger at an 18th-c. ball), Gwen's blood is read into the **chronograph** for controlled travel; **Gideon de Villiers** is assigned as minder.

They meet lodge founder **Count Saint-Germain** (telepathic suspicions). Attack after the visit; later Gwen must fetch blood from ancestor **Margaret Tilney**. Renegades **Lucy** and **Paul** warn against completing all twelve bloods; mention *The Green Rider*. Escape kiss in a confessional. EN tr. Anthea Bell (*Ruby Red*, 2011). Trilogy: *Saphirblau* / *Sapphire Blue* (2010); *Smaragdgrün* / *Emerald Green* (2010 DE numbering—see wiki). German films 2013–16 (Ehrich / Niewöhner). Later books: gargoyle **Xemerius**, second chronograph, Philosopher's Stone twist (Gideon immortality; Whitman = Saint-Germain)—file sequels separately if deepened.
