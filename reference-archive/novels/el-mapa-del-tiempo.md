## Blurb
Victorian London trilogy opener: Jack the Ripper grief, Murray's commercial "time travel" theater, a New Woman's crush on a supposed year-2000 captain, and H. G. Wells discovering that the Map of Time is not a toy—hoax and genuine temporal meddling braided by an omnipresent narrator.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Three interwoven lines (Palma: required an omniscient narrator bouncing among casts):

1. **Andrew Harrington** loved Mary Jane Kelly; after the Ripper murder he slides into opium and planned suicide. Cousin Charles drags him to a man who claims he can take Andrew back to save her.

2. **Claire Haggerty**, feminist "New Woman," rejects fortune-hunting suitors, finds **Murray's Time Travel**, and becomes enthralled by **Captain Derek Shackleton**, advertised hero of humanity's year-2000 war against the machines—spectacle vs possible authenticity.

3. **H. G. Wells** himself probes whether the fourth dimension is physics or storytelling device; in a reputed haunted London house he finds the **Map of Time** and learns meddling has cascading costs.

Part mystery / fantasy / historical pastiche; XL Ateneo de Sevilla Prize 2008; EN tr. 2011 (*The Map of Time*). Sequels: *El mapa del cielo* / *The Map of the Sky* (Wells + War of the Worlds material); *El mapa del caos* / *The Map of Chaos*.
