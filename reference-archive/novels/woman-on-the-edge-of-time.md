## Blurb
Mexican-American Connie Ramos, unjustly committed in 1970s New York, receives from androgynous Luciente's 2137 Mattapoisett utopia—and glimpses a gendered corporate dystopia—then poisons experimenters to keep the good future possible.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Consuelo "Connie" Ramos**, 37, Spanish Harlem, previously lost custody after drug-related neglect; recommitted after striking niece Dolly's pimp to stop a forced abortion. Empathic, she has been receiving **Luciente** from **2137 Mattapoisett**: decentralized eco-anarchist community—non-gendered pronouns ("per"), shared three-mother childrearing via brooder tech, men who breastfeed, rotated power, ritual, respect for chosen mental breaks, income-sharing, anti-racist and anti-imperial. Violence and war exist only as rare extremes.

Hospital experimenters (brother signs consent) plan brain implants releasing chemicals on "negative" emotion—control tech that could midwife the bad future. While comatose between visits she samples **New New York** dystopia: richies on platforms, organ farming, numbered sex-workers like **Gildina**, climate-forced skyscraper flacks, surgical mood control. Ambiguity remains: psychosis vs real travel. Connie steals fast poison at a family visit, says goodbye to Mattapoisett (Jackrabbit dead in their war), doses the doctors' coffee—ending the experiments and her planned lobotomy track, accepting likely permanent incarceration/death: "I did fight them." Landmark feminist utopia/dystopia; often paired with Le Guin / Russ / Atwood.
