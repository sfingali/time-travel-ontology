## Blurb
2072: January Cole runs security at the Paradox Hotel beside the Einstein Intercentury Timeport—until an impossible corpse only she can see, a blizzard lockdown, and a privatization summit force her Unstuck mind into a locked-room murder across time.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Regulated US time tourism: wealthy guests costume up at the **Paradox Hotel**, then tram to **Einstein** for "flights" to witness Hamlet premieres or Gettysburg. Frequent travel risks **Unstuck**—perception slips into past/future without warning. Ex–time-cop **January Cole** left field work for hotel security; Retronim only slows Stage 2. Her slips often show late girlfriend **Mena**, who worked on-site—grief that keeps January from retiring.

A senator and four trillionaires convene to privatize Einstein. Amid staffing chaos and rumor of hallway "ghosts," January finds a body in **room 526** that only she perceives. Blizzard traps guests. "Accidents" stalk the bidders. With AI aide **Ruby** and a large supporting cast, January investigates hotel history, timeport radiation effects, and a killer who weaponizes temporal invisibility. Speculative noir: institutional TT, class inequity, and grief as literal haunting. (US release Feb 2022; often listed 2021 UK/advance.)
