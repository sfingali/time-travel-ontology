## Blurb
Tom Barren wrecks a utopian 2016 (clean tech since 1965) by botching a trip to the day the miracle technology was invented—and wakes in our messy world.

## Detailed plot summary
In a jetpack utopia, loser Tom steals a time machine to impress his scientist father, interferes in 1965, and returns to a timeline matching our history. He navigates love, identity, and whether to "fix" the world back—knowing utopia had its own moral costs. Comic metafiction; screenwriter Mastai's debut.

## Temporal structure
Origin-point sabotage; wholesale reality_rewrite between two present-days.
