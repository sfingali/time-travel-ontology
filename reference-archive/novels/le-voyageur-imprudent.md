# Le Voyageur imprudent / Future Times Three (1944)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Widely cited as first novelization of the grandfather paradox; 1958 postscript “To be and not to be” is the classic philosophical diagram-in-prose.

## Blurb
Mathematician-soldier Pierre Saint-Menoux helps physicist Noël Essaillon test a substance that enables time travel. Future tourism, wartime past-raiding, and plastic history culminate when Pierre tries to kill young Napoleon—and instead kills his own ancestor, erasing himself.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

WWII-era France. Saint-Menoux meets Essaillon, inventor of a temporal substance / diving-suit apparatus. Together (aided by Essaillon’s daughter **Annette**) they probe futures: societal collapse mid-21st century; year **100,000** hive humanity without individuality (Wellsian far-future echo). An accident kills Essaillon; temporal gymnastics undo the death, but the restored scientist, disturbed at usurping God’s timeline, chooses to die anyway and leaves Pierre the work.

Without Essaillon, Pierre uses travel for wartime comfort—raiding prosperous past years for supplies—causing incidents and even captivity in a prior century until Annette rescues him. Newspapers and pulp novels about the “imprudent traveler” appear in the present *because* of his trips; their contents shift as he makes new past intrusions—**plastic time**.

A “Green Devil” persona / past interference prevents architect Michelet’s parents’ marriage; Michelet never existed, yet his hated building somehow remains—hinting at mysterious fatality. Finally Pierre attempts to assassinate **Napoleon** before power. A soldier takes the bullet: that soldier is Pierre’s ancestor. Pierre is wiped from existence. Annette is unmarried; Essaillon (in the erased line’s wake) fails to make the machine work.

**1958 edition** adds postscript analyzing the paradox: the traveler oscillates between existing and not—logic of self-erasure.

## Paradox analysis
Often credited as first *novel* to stage the grandfather paradox (earlier shorts exist; claim debated). Model is **mutable past with annihilation cost**, not branching multiverse. Media bootstraps (books about the traveler appearing after travel) prefigure later information loops.
