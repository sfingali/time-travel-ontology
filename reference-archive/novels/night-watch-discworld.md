# Night Watch (Discworld) (2002)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Glorious 25th of May chronologies; Vimes/Keel identity overlay diagrams on L-Space / Discwiki.

## Blurb
On the anniversary of the Glorious Revolution, Sam Vimes and murderer Carcer are blasted thirty years into Ankh-Morpork’s past. History Monk Lu-Tze tells Vimes he must *become* his murdered mentor John Keel—training young Sam and midwifing the republic’s bloody birth—so the present still exists.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Present-day Commander Vimes pursues psychopath **Carcer** onto Unseen University’s library roof during a magical storm (tied to *Thief of Time* temporal damage). Both are thrown ~30 years back, to days before the **Glorious Twenty-Fifth of May**.

Young Vimes arrests older Vimes for curfew; Carcer bribes free and joins the **Unmentionables** (Cable Street secret police under Homicidal Lord Winder). Lu-Tze freezes time: the real Sergeant-at-Arms **John Keel**—Vimes’s formative mentor—was murdered by Carcer on arrival. Vimes must impersonate Keel, or history (and present Vimes) unravels.

As Keel, Vimes drills the ragtag Night Watch—including his younger self—into competence and decency, tries to reduce body counts inside the necessary revolution, and squares off against Carcer’s escalating cruelty. Young **Havelock Vetinari** assassinates Winder; Lord Snapcase takes power and still sends Carcer’s death squad after “Keel.” Barricade battle recapitulates the original martyrs (including Reg Shoe). Vimes seizes Carcer; Monks return them to the present, planting Keel’s corpse in the past fight so records match. Present Vimes arrests Carcer for good; Sybil births young Sam.

## Paradox analysis
Classic **bootstrap mentor**: Vimes becomes the man who made him. Monks enforce approximate Novikov—bodies and graves must line up. Carcer is the free radical who *can* break history if unchecked. Emotional core: policing as moral time travel—making the past slightly less cruel without erasing it.
