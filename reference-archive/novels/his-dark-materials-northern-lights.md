## Blurb
Lyra Belacqua's Oxford—with daemons—opens onto Dust, armored bears, and the first cracks toward a multiverse war; later volumes make window-cutting and world-travel central.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers) — Book 1 + series structure
**Northern Lights / Golden Compass:** Lyra, daemon **Pantalaimon**, Jordan College. Master attempts to poison Lord **Asriel**; Lyra spies on Dust talk. Gobblers (General Oblation Board) kidnap children including friend Roger. Lyra's alethiometer; rescued by Gyptians; alliance with aeronaut **Lee Scoresby** and armored bear **Iorek Byrnison**. At Bolvangar, children separated from daemons (intercision). Mrs **Coulter** revealed as Lyra's mother; Asriel her father. Asriel's Arctic experiment severs Roger to tear an opening toward another world—Lyra follows.

**Series multiverse (interaction):** *The Subtle Knife* introduces **Cittàgazze** and the **subtle knife** that cuts windows between worlds; Will Parry. *The Amber Spyglass* escalates Authority vs Republic of Heaven across many worlds; mulefa; world of the dead; windows must largely be closed; Dust ecology. *Book of Dust* sequels expand.

Archive tags this file under parallel_worlds / window travel even though Book 1 is mostly single-world setup—because the trilogy is the major Anglophone **interacting multiverse** epic for YA/lit crossover.

## Temporal structure
Parallel worlds coexisting; travel via tear (Asriel) then knife-windows; not machine time travel. Fan maps of world-graph extensively documented.
