## Blurb
After a suicide attempt, Nora Seed wakes in a liminal library of unlived lives; guided by Mrs Elm she samples marriages, stardom, Olympics, Arctic science, and motherhood—then chooses the blank book of her root life.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Nora Seed** overdoses; wakes in **Midnight Library** (clocks frozen at midnight) run by manifestation of school librarian **Mrs Elm**. Every book = a life branching from a different choice; stay if fulfilled; total loss of will = death. *Book of Regrets* catalogues lamented decisions.

Samples: stayed with cheating fiancé Dan; cat-alive / brother-dead; rock-star life (emotionally brittle; Joe still overdoses); Olympic swimming (costly perfection, strained mother); Arctic glaciologist (isolation; resentful dead mother); Cambridge philosophy lecturer (plagiarism from dead brother). Seductive life as wife of kind surgeon **Ash** with daughter **Molly**—genuine love, but she is wearing another Nora's accumulated choices, not building her own; library pulls her back.

Library burns/collapses—sign she has chosen to live. Only unburned book is **root life**, pages blank; she writes **I AM ALIVE**. Wakes ill, gets help from neighbor, hospital recovery; reconciles with brother Joe; reconnects with friend Izzy and piano student; visits real elderly Mrs Elm for chess. Essay insight: mourn unlived lives less—be one person who is already infinite.

Bestseller; Goodreads Choice Fiction 2020; BBC Radio 4; film in development (Florence Pugh reported).

## Temporal structure
```
Suicide attempt --> Midnight Library (between life/death)
Each book = alternate branch from a choice node
Dissatisfaction / inauthenticity --> return to library
Will to live --> root book blank for rewriting present
Not mechanical TT; philosophical many-worlds sampler
```
