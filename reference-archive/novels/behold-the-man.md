## Blurb
Neurotic Karl Glogauer time-travels to 28 AD seeking Jesus and becomes the crucified Christ of history—Nebula-winning theological shock.

## Detailed plot summary
Expanded from a 1966 novella: Glogauer's Jungian obsession and sexual guilt drive him into a past where the historical Yeshua is intellectually disabled. Glogauer steps into the messianic role, fulfilling scripture through performance and suffering. Bootstrap of Christianity's central image.

## Temporal structure
One-way mission into biblical past; identity replacement closes mythic loop.
