## Blurb
Axis victory partitioned America; characters read a banned novel where the Allies won—and the I Ching hints that *that* history is the inner truth, while Tagomi briefly bleeds into another San Francisco.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Alt-history setup:** Zangara kills FDR (1933); US isolation; Axis wins WWII (1947). By 1962: **Pacific States of America** under Japan; Nazi East/South puppets; Rocky Mountain buffer; cold war between Reich and Japan; Nazis drain Mediterranean, colonize space; Hitler syphilitic; Bormann then succession struggle (Goebbels / Heydrich et al.).

**San Francisco threads:** Antique dealer **Robert Childan** sells Americana (often faked by Wyndam-Matson) to Japanese clients. **Frank Frink** (crypto-Jew), fired, blackmails Wyndam-Matson and starts jewelry with wu/spirit. Trade official **Nobusuke Tagomi** meets "Swedish" industrialist **Baynes**—actually Nazi defector **Rudolf Wegener**—to warn Japan of Goebbels's **Operation Dandelion** (nuclear sneak attack on Home Islands). SD agents confront them; Tagomi kills two with an antique Colt. Spiritual shock → Tagomi sits with Frink's jewelry and **momentarily perceives another San Francisco** (Embarcadero freeway; whites not deferring to Japanese)—the archive's key **reality bleed**. He later forces Frink's release.

**Rocky Mountain / Juliana:** Ex-wife **Juliana Frink** travels with **Joe Cinnadella**, who is a Swiss Nazi assassin targeting **Hawthorne Abendsen**, author of *The Grasshopper Lies Heavy* (Allies-win counterfactual). Juliana kills Joe, finds Abendsen living ordinarily (not in a fortress "High Castle"). He admits the **I Ching** guided the novel; Juliana infers the book's "Inner Truth": at a higher level, Japan and Germany **lost**.

Dick used the I Ching in composing the novel; thematic debt to Moore's *Bring the Jubilee*. Hugo Best Novel 1963.
