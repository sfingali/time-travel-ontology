## Blurb
Near-future stray Polly is dragged backward through history by a parasitic tor toward prehistoric apex predator **Cowl**—engineered Heliothane weapon turned time-war refugee—while programmed soldier Tack learns the 43rd-century Heliothane/Umbrathane war is fought across all eras.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Expanded from novella *Cowl At The Beginning*. Homeless teen **Polly** acquires a **tor** (biological time engine); it latches and hauls her into the past, leeching energy, brushing British historical figures, toward the eponymous **Cowl**. Programmed soldier **Tack** is ordered to retrieve the tor; traveler **Traveler** shows him deep-future human speciation: dominant **Heliothane** vs uprising **Umbrathane**, battles spanning time.

**Cowl**: genetically perfected human male Heliothane weapon who slaughtered his birth station and fled into prehistory with multidimensional **Torbeast**. Heliothane theory: he means to erase/replace humanity in favor of Umbrathane progress, checking via Torbeast scales that yank victims back to him. Fast-moving time-war predation thriller; mixed character depth per reviewers, strong adventure update of classic changewar.
