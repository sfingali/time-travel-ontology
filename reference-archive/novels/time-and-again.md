## Blurb
Illustrator Si Morley joins a secret project that uses hypnosis and historically perfect immersion to reach January 1882 New York—then falls in love and confronts whether to alter a key death.

## Detailed plot summary
Recruited in the 1970s, Si lives in the Dakota apartments reconstructed as 1882 and walks into the past. He investigates a half-burned letter tied to "fire which will destroy the whole world," photographs vanished Manhattan, and loves Julia. Morality of changing history vs staying becomes central; Finney includes period photographs. Stephen King called it "THE great time-travel story." Sequel *From Time to Time*.

## Temporal structure
Mind-driven slippage into a past that can be changed; photographic documentary texture.
