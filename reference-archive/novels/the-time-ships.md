## Blurb
Authorized Wells centenary sequel: the Time Traveller tries to rescue Weena, fractures history into Morlock Dyson-sphere futures, twenty-four-year WWI timelines, Paleocene colonies, and Constructor gods—then closes the causal circle by becoming the man who gave himself Plattnerite.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
After *The Time Machine*, the Traveller (younger self nicknamed **Moses**) prepares to return to 802,701 for **Weena**. His quartz machine is infused with radioactive **Plattnerite** from a mysterious 1871 benefactor.

En route he stops in 657,208: sky permanently dark. Advanced **Morlocks** of a rewritten timeline (the Writer's publication of *The Time Machine* prevented the Eloi/Morlock split he knew) have built a **Dyson sphere**; peaceful Morlock **Nebogipfel** explains. The Traveller flees with Nebogipfel clinging on, back to **1873** to stop Moses's research—proving timeline mutability. A war **Juggernaut** from an alternate **1938**—WWI stretched 24 years via time travel—abducts them. Domed London; **Kurt Gödel** aids British timeline-war aims. During a German raid, Gödel's Plattnerite Time-Car escapes; Moses dies; Traveller and Nebogipfel wreck in the **Paleocene**.

**Chronic Expeditionary Force** from 1944 finds them; a German **Carolinum** (nuclear-analogue) bomb devastates most. Survivors found **First London**; over 50 million years of forward travel the colony expands to Moon/orbit then ruins Earth and flees to the stars. At journey's end, nanotech **Universal Constructors** harvest solar energy to build Plattnerite timeships toward the universe's beginning. Integrated into a timeship, Traveller and Nebogipfel ride to the origin; Constructor-successors place them in **1871**—the Traveller *is* **"Gottfried Plattner"** who gifts young-self the sample, closing the bootstrap. Nebogipfel departs with enhanced Constructors to wider historical dimensions.

Final trip along original axis: Traveller barely saves Weena, destroys the machine to avoid re-branching, urges Agrarian Revolution against Morlock dependence, seals his account in a Plattnerite time-capsule (no appendix found).

Awards: BSFA, Campbell Memorial, Philip K. Dick.
