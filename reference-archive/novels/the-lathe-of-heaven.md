## Blurb
George Orr's dreams alter reality; therapist William Haber hijacks them to "improve" the world—each fix birthing new horrors—in Le Guin's Taoist masterpiece.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Near-future **Portland** (novel's 2002): overcrowding, rain, war, climate stress. Draftsman **George Orr** has **effective dreams** that rewrite past and present into a new timeline; only he (and anyone watching at the instant) remembers the prior world. He self-medicates to stop dreaming; court-mandated therapy with sleep researcher **Dr. William Haber**.

Haber witnesses a dream change a picture in his office, believes, and builds the **Augmentor** (biofeedback/EEG) to *direct* Orr's dreams toward utilitarian "improvements":

1. **Overpopulation** → Orr dreams a plague; billions die.
2. Further "cures" cascade (eugenic logics; cancer "solutions" that normalize killing genetic threats).
3. **"Peace on Earth"** → alien invasion of the Moon unites humanity against an outside enemy (new war in cislunar space).

Lawyer **Heather Lelache** observes a session, retains dual memories, becomes ally. Orr claims the "real" world died in 1998 nuclear war and he dreamed existence back while dying in the ruins—current reality may already be a dream-scaffold.

Heather hypnotically suggests Haber become more benevolent *and* that aliens leave the Moon—unintentionally triggering **Earth invasion**. Haber orders a dream of peaceful aliens; temporary stability. Then Haber's anti-racism suggestion turns everyone **gray**, rewriting history (no Gettysburg Address memory; no MLK). Heather—biracial in prior reality—never existed; Orr dreams a gray, milder Heather and marries her.

Aliens (cryptically wise about effective dreaming) counsel Orr. Final session: Haber "cures" Orr by having him dream that dreams no longer affect reality, then uses the Augmentor to seize effective dreaming himself. Haber's first solo dream nearly dissolves coherent existence into chaos; gray Heather is annihilated. Orr shuts off the Augmentor. World stabilizes as a **mash of random elements** from several realities. Haber survives mind-broken; a version of Heather returns with only faint memory of Orr; Orr works for an alien at a kitchen store and begins again with coffee—Taoist refusal of total control.

## Temporal structure
Sequential **reality_rewrite** overwrites (not side-by-side multiverse tourism). Memory of prior stacks is uneven. Philosophy: anti-utilitarian / anti-behaviorist; Taoist "lathe of heaven" title (Legge mistranslation of Zhuangzi that Le Guin later acknowledged).

```
World0 --dream1--> World1 --dream2--> World2 ... --Haber-chaos--> World_composite
```
