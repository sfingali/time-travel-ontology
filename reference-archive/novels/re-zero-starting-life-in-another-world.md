## Blurb
Isekai LN: Subaru Natsuki's **Return by Death** rewinds him to hidden checkpoints whenever he dies—only he retains memories—turning each arc into trauma-priced save-scumming to protect Emilia and allies.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Web novel 2012 (Narō); MF Bunko J LN from Jan 2014 (ill. Shinichirou Otsuka); 45+ vols. Convenience-store NEET **Subaru** is summoned to Lugunica, dies helping silver-haired candidate **Emilia**, and revives hours earlier. Repeated deaths teach him the power's rules (and its psychological cost). He becomes a mansion servant under **Roswaal**, bonds with twins Rem/Ram, battles Witch Cult sin archbishops across arcs, and must learn to rely on allies (**Otto**, **Garfiel**, etc.) rather than solo looping.

Mechanics: checkpoint locations shift; Subaru cannot freely speak of the ability (Witch of Envy constraints in-story); failed loops' suffering remains his alone. Distinct from pure Groundhog Day by escalating stakes, lore dumps deferred, and isekai + time-loop hybrid. Yen Press EN. Anime: White Fox 2016– (S1–S3+); archive TV entry `tv/re-zero.md` covers screen adaptation—**this file is the LN source of truth**.

## Temporal structure
```
Death → rewind to last checkpoint (Subaru memory only)
Checkpoints move with story progress
```
