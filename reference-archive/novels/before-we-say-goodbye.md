## Blurb
Back at Funiculi Funicula: four more last conversations—including a couple's farewell to beloved dog Apollo—under coffee-cold rules that heal speech, not history.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
JP 2021; EN *Before We Say Goodbye*. Returns to Tokyo's Funiculi Funicula. Same seat/ghost/coffee constraints. Representative strand: **Mutsuo** and **Sunao Hikita** mourning golden retriever Apollo; Sunao's guilt at missing the death prompts a past visit to hold and thank the dog—grief reframed, present unchanged. Other patrons seek reconciliation across death, distance, or silence. Reinforces series thesis: unsaid words matter even when causality will not budge. Later sequels: *Before We Forget Kindness* (JP 2023 / EN 2024); *Before I Knew I Loved You* (EN reported 2026).
