## Blurb
Lazarus Long (~2000+ years) narrates interlocking tales to earn a reason to keep living; the TT core is **Da Capo**—a jump to early-20th-c. America where he loves his own mother Maureen and nearly dies in WWI trenches before future kin extract him.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Framing: ancient Howard Families mutant **Lazarus Long** (Woodrow Wilson Smith) is talked out of suicide by descendant **Ira Weatheral** via reverse-*Arabian Nights* storytelling.

Embedded tales (many non-TT): constructive laziness of David Lamb; manumitted "twins who weren't" (genetic recombination experiment); frontier love with mortal **Dora**; later **Boondock** poly family and female clones of Lazarus. Intermissions = *Notebooks of Lazarus Long* aphorisms.

**Da Capo (TT climax):** Lazarus tries for 1919 adulthood revisit; navigation error → 1916. He falls for mother **Maureen** (already pregnant with his younger self—so he is *not* his own father despite some blurbs). Enlists to keep family esteem; consummates with Maureen; is mortally wounded in France; rescued by future companions and returned. Links to *Methuselah's Children*, *Orphans of the Sky*, *I Will Fear No Evil*, *Number of the Beast*, *Cat Who Walks Through Walls*, *To Sail Beyond the Sunset*. Nebula/Hugo/Locus shortlist; Prometheus Hall of Fame 1988.
