## Blurb
In a world where the South won Gettysburg, historian Hodge Backmaker time-travels to the battle—and accidentally creates our timeline.

## Detailed plot summary
Alt-history USA impoverished after Confederate victory; Hodge joins a utopian community with a time machine, goes to observe Gettysburg, inadvertently causes Union success, and is stranded in the resulting world (ours), unable to return to a now-never past. Major inspiration for Dick's *High Castle*. Tags: branching_timeline | reality_rewrite.

## Temporal structure
Observer effect becomes history change; one-way exile into new present.
