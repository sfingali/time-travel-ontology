## Blurb
Professor Brendan Doyle joins a 1983 millionaire's Coleridge lecture junket through Egyptian magic gates—and becomes trapped in Regency London as body-swappers, beggar-kings, and the discovery that he himself is the poet William Ashbless.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
1801–02: Egyptian magicians fail to summon Anubis to destroy British power; the botched rite opens **predictable gates** across time. 1983: dying tycoon **J. Cochran Darrow** exploits the gates for rich tourists to hear **Coleridge** in 1810; hires literature professor **Brendan Doyle**. Magician **Doctor Romany** kidnaps Doyle before return; Doyle escapes into nineteenth-century London, trapped.

Doyle joins a beggars' guild, meets **Jacky**, plans to befriend studied poet **William Ashbless** for patronage. Romany hunts with clown-magician **Horrabin**'s killer beggars. Darrow stays in-period seeking **Dog-Face Joe**, a body-swapping werewolf, for a healthy body. Joe targets Doyle with Darrow's poisoned former body; Doyle cures himself and realizes *he is Ashbless*—copying remembered poems into history (**bootstrap**).

Doyle thwarts magicians; follows Romany to 1684; stops past-change. Darrow deals with Joe for immortal body-rotation. Kidnapped to Muhammad Ali's Egypt, Doyle refuses the Master's offer to resurrect his dead wife for gate-secrets and kills the Master. Jacky kills Darrow and Joe. Final magician Romanelli drugs Doyle/Jacky/Coleridge; Coleridge frees Horrabin's monsters; Romanelli flees with Doyle to the underworld and is eaten by **Apep** while Doyle rejuvenates on Ra's sunboat. Reveal: Jacky is a woman—and Doyle's future wife. Decades later, Ashbless/Doyle meets his "death": kills a duplicate the Master made, supplying the historical corpse, and boats into unknown future.

Philip K. Dick Award 1983; Fantasy Masterworks.
