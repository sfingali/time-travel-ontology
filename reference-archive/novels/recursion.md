## Blurb
False Memory Syndrome sweeps the world; a detective and a neuroscientist race through stacked timelines created by a "memory chair" that turns vivid recall into reality-rewrite.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**2018 — Barry Sutton** (NYPD): a woman with **False Memory Syndrome (FMS)**—memories of lives never lived—suicides after he fails to talk her down. Investigating FMS leads to **Hotel Memory** and magnate **Marcus Slade**, who force-sends Barry into the night his daughter **Meghan** died. Barry saves her, changing his life. As "present" approaches anniversary, timeline inconsistencies escalate (NYC skyline shifts); mass FMS. When original-timeline memories return, Meghan—overwhelmed—kills herself.

**2007 strand — Helena Smith**: Alzheimer's researcher building a **memory chair** to replay experience. Funder Slade isolates her on an oil rig, pushing toward a deprivation tank that enables **time travel via death/resurrection into "dead memory."** Reveal: in the *original* timeline Slade was her lab assistant; they accidentally caused subjects to time-travel; Slade jumped back to become a billionaire and captive-control Helena's research. She escapes by jumping, knowing Slade will regain memories when time catches up.

Barry and Helena converge across shared timelines: kill Slade at Hotel Memory (Barry dies; DARPA seizes tech); Helena detained into "benevolent" undo projects that militarize; chairs proliferate among states/terrorists/corps; each attempt to fix the crisis fails because on departure-date the world remembers *all* prior timelines → global war. Helena loops to teen savepoints; mental health collapses; she dies. Barry, stopped from suicide by a memory surge, recalls kidnapping Slade and learning Slade *faked* the dead-memory lethality warning—dead memory is the key to overwriting false memories. Barry returns to the **first** timeline and kills lab-assistant Slade the day Slade would have killed Helena—restoring the original. Reunion with Helena at a bar.
