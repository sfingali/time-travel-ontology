## Blurb
Scottish hard-SF: a tau-wave machine sends messages into the past, overwriting timelines like taped video—used thrice to avert microscopic black holes, a bioweapon fallout, and finally to restore a love that the first reset erased.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
December 2009: **Murdoch Ross** and friend **Lee Walker** visit grandfather **Sir Charles Ross** at Storbannon castle. Charles, Nobel-adjacent for quark work, privately built a machine that sends information via **tau waves**—his radical claim that when a nucleon decays, two quarks are "propagated back in time." A demo: Murdoch types random characters; the printout already showed them a minute earlier.

Experiments establish that refusing to send a received future message floods the channel; a broken-jar warning implies that sending a corrective message **erases** the sender's future selves (timeline overwrite, not branching coexistence). Protocol and range expand to ~24 hours. Murdoch meets physicist **Anne Patterson** (Burghead fusion plant); **Elizabeth Muir** and RAF veteran **Ted Cartland** join.

Burghead's inertial-confinement tests birth millions of microscopic black holes that tunnel toward Earth's core, emit tau interference, and appear as "bugophants" drilling tiny holes. Lee collapses with a fast-progressing MS-like disease. With the machine about to swamp, Murdoch and Anne bootstrap a self-repeating program past the 24-hour limit to warn the past—knowing they will be reset. Past selves shut Burghead down; the tau flood of that message subtly alters quantum odds so Anne never trips over the kitten—**their relationship never begins**.

In the second timeline the black-hole crisis is avoided, but Lee's disease and a West-Coast epidemic continue: secret orbital lab **QX-37 / Centurion** broke up in the Perseids, seeding mutant **omnisclerosis Californians**. A vaccine batch kills 811 people; public trust collapses. The team persuades officials via a five-day pilot reset (faulty batch never released), then prepare a multi-month warning with vaccine data.

**Epilogue / third timeline:** the July→January message arrives the same afternoon as the black-hole warning. Murdoch and Lee shop in Kingussie; Anne trips over the kitten again. Title = three tellings of "once upon a time." Cousin to Benford's *Timescape*; often paired with *Steins;Gate*-style information TT.
