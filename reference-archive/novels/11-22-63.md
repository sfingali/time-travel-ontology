## Blurb
English teacher Jake Epping inherits a diner portal that always opens on 9 September 1958 and spends years becoming "George Amberson" to stop Lee Harvey Oswald—only to find history fights back and saved Kennedys can break the world.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Lisbon Falls, Maine, 2011: dying diner-owner **Al Templeton** shows teacher **Jake Epping** a pantry portal to **9 Sept 1958** that always resets to that day; return to 2011 costs two minutes. Al spent four subjective years stalking Oswald toward JFK's assassination but cancer forces him to recruit Jake. Test case: Jake saves janitor **Harry Dunning**'s family from hammer-murderer father Frank in Derry—returns to find Harry died in Vietnam instead; Al suicides.

Jake resets, kills Frank early, settles in **Jodie, Texas** as teacher "George Amberson," falls for librarian **Sadie Dunhill**, stages plays, waits years building cover while tracking Oswald (abusive marriage to Marina; Walker attempt; de Mohrenschildt). He reveals the mission to Sadie; they rush the Book Depository; Jake stops Oswald; **Sadie dies**.

Return to 2011 yields dystopia: Kennedy two terms → segregationist Wallace → nuclear escalation / WWIII-ish ruin, King killed by FBI, Civil Rights Act never passed, constant disasters, sky tearing—each past trip creates a new reality strand and overuse frays time. Jake resets by re-entering without saving Kennedy. Sadie lives, celebrated as Citizen of the Century; he attends her party without reopening the wound. (Alternate ending King posted online: Jake finds 2013 article of aged married Sadie with descendants—scrapped at Joe Hill's suggestion.)

## Temporal structure
```
Portal always --> 1958-09-09 (same day reset)
Stay any duration; return = +2 min present
Changes overwrite on next entry (full reset)
"Harmonics" / past resists (accidents, illness)
Excessive intervention --> frayed multiverse catastrophe
```
