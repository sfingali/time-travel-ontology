## Blurb
US military projects a time war into the Miocene to seize Middle Eastern oil at the source—anachronistic relics prove the catastrophe already failed.

## Detailed plot summary
Framed by anomalous finds (high-tech 'relics'; Jeep in Gibraltar strata). US program sends forces into the deep past to control oil geology; protagonist arrives decades 'late' into trench-war hell among stranded time soldiers. Peak-oil + theology-tinged SF. English *The Last Day of Creation* (1982).

## Temporal structure
Military changewar into deep past; bootstrap evidence in the present; no clean return.
