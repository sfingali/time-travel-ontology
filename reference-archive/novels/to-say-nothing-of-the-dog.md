## Blurb
A comic Oxford time-travel romp: Ned Henry, suffering time-lag, and historian Verity Kindle scramble through Victorian England to restore a Victorian objet and the continuum—homaging *Three Men in a Boat*.

## Detailed plot summary
Lady Schrapnell bankrolls Oxford's net to rebuild Coventry Cathedral and find the "bishop's bird stump." Ned's excessive jumps leave him time-lagged; Verity has brought a Victorian cat forward, risking incongruity. They navigate 1888 river society, romantic mix-ups, and self-correcting history (slippage as safety valve). Jumper and eccentric casts converge so that history's needed outcomes occur. Hugo Award winner; lighter counterpart to *Doomsday Book*.

## Temporal structure
Same Willis Oxford rules: observers, slippage, incongruity danger; mystery-comedy plotting.
