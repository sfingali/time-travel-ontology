## Blurb
Linked lives from 1912 Vancouver Island to 2200s moon colonies investigate an anomaly—a shared glitch that may prove reality is a simulation—and a pandemic echo of Mandel's *Station Eleven*.

## Detailed plot summary
Edwin St. John St. Andrew (1912), author Olive Llewellyn (on a 2020 book tour as pandemic begins), gas-station witness Mirella, and Gaspery-Jacques Roberts (Time Institute detective from future lunar colony) intersect around a violin/forest anomaly. Gaspery's investigation violates rules and loops into the anomaly's cause. Compact, elegiac; time travel as institutional bureaucracy plus metaphysical doubt.

## Temporal structure
Institutional travel; investigation creates participation; simulation hypothesis overlay.
