## Blurb
Naval Criminal Investigative Service agent Shannon Moss hunts a murder tied to a missing Navy deep-space ship—while traveling into possible future "Terms" where humanity's extinction already blooms.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Shannon Moss** investigates a brutal murder linked to the disappeared ship *Libra*. US Navy secret program: travel into **Terms**—possible futures—for intelligence. Each Term is a branch; bringing knowledge back is strategically valuable and personally corrosive. Moss, missing a leg from a prior Term injury that "stuck," walks crime scenes across futures where she sees versions of the **White Hole** / extinction event approaching. Murder mystery and cosmological horror braid: suspects, cult logics, and the ethics of colonizing possibility-space.

Tags: `branching_timeline` | future-espionage | extinction spoilers as plot engine.

## Temporal structure
Present investigation ⊕ excursions into multiple future Terms; information flows backward; ontology closer to many-worlds scouting than single fixed future.
