## Blurb
A Temporal Bureau bartender recruits a confession-magazine writer—and the story reveals they are the same intersex person: mother, father, and child of themselves.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**1970:** A Bartender draws out **"the Unmarried Mother,"** a writer of confession stories. Flashback identity: born **Jane** (1945 orphanage); in 1963 seduced and abandoned; Caesarean damages female organs → surgical reassignment male; baby kidnapped.

Bartender time-travels the man to **1963** to confront the seducer—who **is himself**. Bartender kidnaps the infant to **1945** (creating Jane), retrieves the Unmarried Mother to **1985** Temporal Bureau enlistment, returns to 1970. All major roles—Jane, seducer, baby, Bartender—are **one person** on a closed causal loop. Closing line: "I know where I came from—but where did all you zombies come from?"

Philosopher **David Lewis** cited the story as "perfectly consistent" time travel (no grandfather contradiction; information/person bootstrap).

## Temporal structure
Nested trips: 1945 ← 1963–64 ↔ 1970 → 1985 (and references to 1993). Perfect **predestination/bootstrap** ouroboros. Best-in-class public diagram on Wikimedia Commons.

```
1945: baby Jane abandoned (by Bartender)
1963: Jane seduced by future male self; baby born, stolen → 1945
1970: confession; Bartender = older self recruits
1985: Temporal Bureau enlistment
```
