## Blurb
In a nursing home, Patricia remembers two mutually exclusive lives—and two divergent world-histories.

## Detailed plot summary
At a choice point (marriage proposal), lives fork: different partners, children, careers, and geopolitical backgrounds. Late Le Guin intimate multiverse—no gadget, pure branch memory.

## Temporal structure
Two stacked biographies; world-history differs with each.
