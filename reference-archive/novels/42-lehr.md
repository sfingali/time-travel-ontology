## Blurb
Literary DE thriller: ~70 CERN visitors emerge into a Europe frozen at 12:47:42—personal time continues inside a photographed world—until rare seconds of resumed world-time enable a desperate final experiment.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Hanser; often cited 2005/later reprints. A tour group leaves the underground accelerator complex near Geneva to find Switzerland/Europe in Sleeping-Beauty stasis at **12:47 and 42 seconds**. The **Chronifizierten** still age, argue, birth children, and invent theories—are they trapped forever in that second? After ~five subjective years, world-time briefly advances three precious seconds, jolting survivors toward a "final experiment" that may involve accelerator-enabled travel into the past. Physics ⊕ poetry; compared by critics to high literary modernism as much as genre SF. On DE *Liste von Zeitreiseromanen* harvest lists.
