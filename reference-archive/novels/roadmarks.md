## Blurb
A highway exits into many times; Red runs it seeking exit ramps through history and alternate flow.

## Detailed plot summary
Zelazny's picaresque: the Road links eras and maybes; assassins, dragons, and deadends. Companion spirit to Amber's Shadow-walking but literalized as highway.

## Temporal structure
Spatialized time; exits as era-gates.
