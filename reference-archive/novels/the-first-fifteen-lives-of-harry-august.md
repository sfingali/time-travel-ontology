## Blurb
Harry August is a kalachakra—reborn to the same 1919 life with memories intact—who learns through the Cronus Club that each of his lives the world ends sooner, because fellow ouroboran Vincent Rankis is accelerating technology toward catastrophe.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**Harry August** is born in a Berwick-upon-Tweed station washroom in 1919, lives unremarkably, dies in Newcastle hospital in 1989—and wakes again as the same infant with prior-life memory. He is an **Ouroboran / Kalachakra**, destined to loop. The **Cronus Club**, a network of similar immortals-through-rebirth, finds and mentors child-Harry across lives (mnemonics who remember everything).

Across lives Harry masters sciences; as Cambridge physics professor he befriends brilliant undergraduate **Vincent Rankis**. Both eventually recognize the other as kalachakra. Cronus Club members warn Harry the end of the world is arriving *earlier* each of his loops—technology and catastrophe accelerating. Vincent's drive to understand and control time/quantum reality (and to escape the loop's limits) becomes the engine of that acceleration. The emotional core is Harry's friendship-and-nemesis bond with Vincent: the only reliable way to permanently kill a kalachakra is to learn the precise circumstances of their birth and prevent it, making trust lethally intimate.

John W. Campbell Memorial Award; Clarke and BSFA nominee. Film adaptation announced (Wes Ball) as of 2020 reporting.
