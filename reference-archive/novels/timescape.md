## Blurb
Dying 1998 Earth beams tachyon warnings to 1962–63 physicists to avert ecological catastrophe; Nebula and Campbell Memorial hard-SF classic of information-only time travel.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Dual narrative. **1998 Cambridge**: ocean bloom / ecological collapse threatens civilization. Physicist **John Renfrew** and colleagues attempt to encode warnings into a beam of **tachyons** aimed at past detectors—gambling that past action can fork a livable branch.

**1962–63 UC La Jolla**: young physicist **Gordon Bernstein** detects anomalous signals amid departmental politics, career anxiety, and the era's scientific culture. Decoding proceeds slowly; the novel interleaves lab realism, marriage strain, and the ethics of whether a warned past can act enough to matter. Cause and effect across decades remain contested: does successful warning erase the 1998 that sent it, branch a new world, or only partially mitigate?

Benford (working physicist) grounds speculation in tachyon / advanced-wave discussion of the period. Won **1980 Nebula Award for Best Novel** and **John W. Campbell Memorial Award**. Often cited as the serious hard-SF counterpart to more adventure-oriented TT.
