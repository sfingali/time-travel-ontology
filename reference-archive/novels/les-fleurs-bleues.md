## Blurb
Oulipo masterwork: a modern bargeman and a medieval duke may be dreaming each other across centuries.

## Detailed plot summary
Dual narrative—20th-century Bashelier and historical Duc d'Auge—suggests reciprocal dreams as time bridge. Linguistic play; major French **time-twist** novel outside pulp SF.

## Temporal structure
Dream identity across centuries; ambiguous which era is 'real'.
