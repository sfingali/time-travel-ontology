## Blurb
Archaeology grad students dive through ITC's quantum "time" tech into a parallel 1357 Dordogne to rescue Professor Johnston from Hundred Years' War sieges—while transcription errors threaten to erase returning bodies.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Arizona desert: an elderly ITC worker dies with inexplicable blood-vessel damage. In France's Dordogne, Professor **Edward Johnston**'s team digs Castelgard/La Roque; suspicious of funder **ITC**'s too-precise knowledge, Johnston visits NM HQ and vanishes. Students find his eyeglass lens in ruins and a 600-year-old written plea. **Chris Hughes**, **Kate Erickson**, **André Marek**, tech **David Stern** fly to ITC Black Rock. VP **Gordon** reveals Johnston went to **1357** via quantum multiverse mapping (not our past but an isomorphic parallel). Stern stays; others plus two ITC guards transit.

Arrival: knights attack; guards die; one's grenade wrecks present-day pad. Kate/Marek find Johnston at a monastery; Lord **Oliver de Vannes** captives him believing he knows La Roque's secret passage. Chris follows disguised Lady **Claire**, is challenged to joust with Marek against Sir Guy; they survive via Marek's medieval combat knowledge. Escapes, pursuits by Oliver's advisor **Robert de Kere**—actually ITC veteran **Rob Deckard**, insane from cumulative transcription errors—who wants to strand them. Marek infiltrates La Roque; Chris/Kate find the passage; Arnaut's siege proceeds as history predicts Oliver's loss. Johnston builds an incendiary for Oliver while betting on historical defeat. Kate kills Guy; team frees Johnston; Chris burns Deckard. Pad repaired just in time. **Marek stays** (always wanted the Middle Ages), marries Claire (graves found later). Returners confront ITC president **Doniger**; Gordon drugs him and ships him to **1348 Black Death**. Chris and Kate implied couple; pregnancy.

Popularized multiverse-as-time-travel for mass audiences. Film 2003 (Donner) poorly received.
