# Reborn Rich (재벌집 막내아들, 2022)

## Short blurb
Murdered Soonyang manager Yoon Hyun-woo wakes in 1987 as Jin Do-jun, youngest grandson of the chaebol that killed him, and uses future knowledge to hijack the family’s empire through the 1997 IMF crisis.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 16-episode JTBC juggernaut (Nov–Dec 2022) adapted from San Gyung’s web novel; Song Joong-ki, Lee Sung-min, Shin Hyun-been. Loyal Future Asset manager Hyun-woo is betrayed and killed to cover Soonyang tax evasion. He reincarnates as child Jin Do-jun. Armed with foresight of markets, politics, and family betrayals, he partners with Oh Se-hyeon / Mason Oh, navigates founder Jin Yang-chul’s succession war among sons Young-ki, Dong-ki, and daughter Hwa-young, and plots a hostile takeover. Prosecutor Seo Min-young becomes ally/love interest. Backdrop: chaebol power and the Asian financial crisis. Finale ratings ~26.9% nationwide—second-highest Korean cable drama ever. International Emmy nomination (Best TV Movie/Mini-Series); Thai remake announced. Season 2 confirmed.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Classic rebirth/regression: one consciousness, past body, future memory.
- Butterfly risk bounded by revenge goal; period detail doubles as time-travel payoff.
- Not a looping show—single continuous rewrite from 1987 forward.
