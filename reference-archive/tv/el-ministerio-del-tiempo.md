# El ministerio del tiempo (2015–2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Door network across Spanish history; episode missions map cleanly to timelines.

## Short blurb
Spain's secret Ministry of Time fields patrols through hidden doors so past intruders cannot rewrite the present—newest team: 16th-c. soldier Alonso, 19th-c. student Amelia, and 21st-c. paramedic Julián.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Autonomous agency reports to PM and monarch. Pilot: Julián is recruited after meeting Napoleonic-era soldiers; with Amelia Folch and Alonso de Entrerríos he protects guerrilla Juan Martín Díez. Later missions: ensure Lope de Vega boards the right Armada ship; stop Himmler using a Montserrat door during Hitler–Franco Hendaye talks; save Rabbi Abraham Levi (source of the Book of Doors) from Torquemada; recover Guernica paperwork; foil Philip II rewriting the Armada defeat. Antagonists include Darrow Corp time tourists, Exterminating Angel absolutists, Sons of Padilla. Four seasons (42 eps); Ondas and Feroz awards. Lawsuit vs NBC *Timeless* settled; Portuguese adaptation existed.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Founding myth: Isabella I receives Book of Doors 1491.
- Patrols "repair" history rather than tourist it—Spanish civic mythos via SF.
