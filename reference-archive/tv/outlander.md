# Outlander (2014-)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Stones-location maps (Craigh na Dun, Abandawe, Ocracoke); “Hitchhiker’s Guide” rule lists on Outlander Wiki; season genealogy of travelers.

## Short blurb
WWII nurse Claire Randall touches the stone circle at Craigh na Dun and falls from 1945 to 1743 Scotland. Medicine, Jacobite politics, and marriage to Jamie Fraser span decades and continents; daughter Brianna and Roger later inherit the gene and the stones’ dangers.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

### Core loop (S1–2)
Claire, married to historian Frank, vanishes at Beltane-adjacent standing stones. In the 1740s she is taken for a spy, forced into marriage with Jamie for protection, and pulled into Clan MacKenzie / Jacobite intrigue. She knows Culloden’s doom; attempts to steer history largely **fail or fulfill** recorded outcomes. After Paris and the Rising, Jamie sends pregnant Claire through the stones to save her and the child in the 20th century.

### Reunion & Americas (S3–)
In 1968 Claire returns through the stones (gemstone ring consumed) to find Jamie alive. Brianna and Roger research, then travel (solstice timing; gem protocols from Geillis’s grimoire) when they fear Claire/Jamie die in a 1770s fire. Later seasons: Revolution-era America, Fraser’s Ridge, more stone sites (Jamaica’s Abandawe cave; Carolina circles). Geillis/Gillian Edgars is a fellow traveler trying to alter Jacobite success—foil to Claire’s reluctant interference.

### Ongoing series
As of archive date the show continues adapting Gabaldon’s books; TT remains logistics for romance/historical epic rather than puzzle-box physics.

## Time-travel rules (show/book consensus)

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hereditary ability | Claire → Brianna → Jemmy/Mandy; Geillis line → Roger |
| Place | Stone circles / certain caves; not arbitrary |
| When | Solstices, fire festivals (Beltane etc.); stones “hum” |
| Gems | Precious stones believed needed for survival/steering (EP Maril Davis; Claire’s watch/retcon; ring gem missing after transit) |
| Default span | Often ~200 years, not precise dial-a-date |
| Change history? | Extremely hard; actions tend to produce the future travelers came from |
| Cost | Agonizing passage; death risk without protections |

Jamie cannot travel (ghost sightings ≠ body TT, per Gabaldon).

## Paradox analysis

- **Soft Novikov:** Big-history events resist change; personal lives still contingent.
- **Gene + place magic:** Hybrid of science-fictional heredity and folkloric thin places—not machines.
- **Geillis contrast:** Activist changewar vs Claire’s survival ethics.
- **Information:** 20th-century knowledge in 18th century (medicine, politics) is the real “technology.”
