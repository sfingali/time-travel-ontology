# Steins;Gate 0 (シュタインズ・ゲート ゼロ, 2018)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Beta worldline / Amadeus routes charted alongside main Steins;Gate divergence maps.

## Short blurb
On the Beta attractor where Okabe failed to save Kurisu, PTSD-stricken Okabe tests the Amadeus AI built from her memories while Suzuha pushes him toward Steins Gate—and World War III looms over time-travel arms races.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia VN/anime synopsis.) After Okabe accidentally kills Kurisu on the July 2010 attempt and refuses to retry, he meets Maho Hiyajo and Alexis Leskinen and becomes tester for Amadeus Kurisu. Suzuha searches for Kagari (Mayuri's future adoptive daughter, separated in 1998). Story splits into major branches (Promised Rinascimento, Vega and Altair, side endings Recursive Mother Goose, Twin Automata, Gehenna's Stigma) depending on whether Okabe answers Amadeus calls. Key beats: rebuilding the PhoneWave, memory overwrites of Kagari, leaps to 2036 wartime, Leskinen's Strategic Focus betrayal, Mayuri/Suzuha's destroyed time machine, and finally "Milky-way Crossing," in which 2025 Okabe sends the D-Mail video that teaches 2010 Okabe how to deceive the world—enabling the original game's True End / Steins Gate. 2018 White Fox anime adapts the VN.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Canonical "what if Okabe stays on Beta" path between Steins;Gate and Steins Gate arrival.
- Amadeus phone answers are the player/viewer branch key (vs original's microwave triggers).
- Completes the bootstrap that delivers the video D-Mail of the first series' climax.
