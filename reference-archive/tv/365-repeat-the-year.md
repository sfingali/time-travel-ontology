# 365: Repeat the Year (365: 운명을 거스르는 1년, 2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Ten-person reset cohort + serial-killer identity swaps invite cast/timeline boards; based on Kurumi Inui’s *Repeat*.

## Short blurb
Ten strangers get one year rewound; detective Ji Hyung-joo, webtoonist Shin Ga-hyun, and multi-reset psychiatrist Lee Shin discover their “gift” is an experiment—and a partner-cop serial killer is erasing resetters.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia cast/synopsis.) 24 half-episodes (12 nights) on MBC (Mar–Apr 2020), starring Lee Joon-hyuk, Nam Ji-hyun, Kim Ji-soo, Yang Dong-geun. Adapted from Inui’s novel *Repeat* (also basis for Japan’s *Wheel of Fortune*). After partner Park Sun-ho is murdered, Hyung-joo resets one year; Ga-hyun resets after a wheelchair accident; Shin has already reset ≥6 times to save her daughter. Cohort includes ex-con Bae Jung-tae, guilt-ridden Seo Yeon-soo, cafe-owner/professor Hwang No-seop (secret mastermind testing who survives), lottery-seeking guard Choi, stockbroker Cha, pro-gamer Go, Munchausen student Kim Se-rin. Sun-ho is revealed as the killer detective, staging cohort deaths (stairs, heart attack, suicide, framing, manhole). Florist So Hye-in refused the reset while pregnant and is killed anyway. Hyung-joo arrests Sun-ho and Hwang in a subsequent reset. Related: NTV *Wheel of Fortune* (2018).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Shared one-year rewind for a fixed cohort; memory retention varies.
- Nested resets (Shin’s sixth+) vs first-timers.
- Killer inside the police partner bond = Frequency/Signal adjacent.
