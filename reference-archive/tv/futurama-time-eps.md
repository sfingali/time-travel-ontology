# Futurama (time-travel episodes) (1999–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Infosphere and fan essays extensively diagram Bender duplicates, Lars=Fry, and forward-only cosmologies.

## Short blurb
Anthology of rigorously jokey paradox stories: *Roswell That Ends Well* (1947 bootstrap), *The Late Philip J. Fry* (forward-only heat-death rebirth), and film *Bender’s Big Score* (tattoo time code, doomed duplicates, dozens of Benders tearing spacetime).

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**“Roswell That Ends Well”:** A supernova + microwave mishap flings the ship to 1947 Roswell. Fry’s grandfather dies; Fry panics into sleeping with his grandmother—becoming his own grandfather. Bender’s head is left buried for centuries (classic object bootstrap). Emmy-winning.

**“The Late Philip J. Fry”:** Farnsworth’s forward-only time button overshoots; Fry/Bender/Farnsworth skip to universe heat death and rebirth, eventually looping to a near-identical cosmos where they can resume—elegiac comedy about missing Leela’s life.

**Bender’s Big Score (2007 film / S5):** Nude-beach scammers find a binary time code in a Bender tattoo on Fry’s butt. Infected Bender steals history’s treasures, creating self-duplicates each trip. Fry flees to 1 Jan 2000; Bender hunts him for years. Fry duplicates himself: one stays in the past (becomes Lars Fillmore, Leela’s suitor), one returns via cryo. Paradox duplicates are “doomed to die prematurely.” Lars cancels the wedding to spare Leela, then dies stopping scammer Nudar by triggering a self-destructing Bender copy—revealing Lars’s tattoo. Final Bender gathers all logically-should-have-left duplicates; their massed presence tears the universe (lead-in to later films). Nibbler’s warnings and “doom field” math (Keeler) underline that comedy still tracks conservation rules.

Other notables: “Time Keeps on Slippin’,” “The Why of Fry,” “Meanwhile” (button freezes time globally).

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Roswell bootstrap** — ancestry + buried head.
2. **Forward-only button** — irreversible tourism to cosmic reset.
3. **Tattoo code economy** — scammers → Bender heists → duplicate swarm.
4. **Lars reveal** — duplicate Fry’s tragic romance arc.
5. **Duplicate pileup catastrophe** — too many Benders = spacetime tear.

Ken Keeler (math PhD) designed paradox mechanics to be internally consistent enough for fan proofs.

## Paradox analysis

- **Bootstrap central:** Fry as grandfather; Bender head; tattoo placement by future Bender.
- **Doomed duplicates:** Explicit mortality rule for copies—drama engine for Lars.
- **Doom field / copy limits:** Humorous physics preventing infinite free theft.
- **Forward-only constraint:** *Late Philip J. Fry* refuses rewind comfort.
- **Comedy rigor:** Jokes land because rules are stated and paid off.
