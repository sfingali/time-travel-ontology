# Tokyo Revengers (東京卍リベンジャーズ, 2021–)

## Short blurb
Deadbeat Takemichi Hanagaki leaps twelve years into his middle-school past via contact with Naoto Tachibana and repeatedly rewrites Tokyo Manji Gang history to save his ex-girlfriend Hinata and friends.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) In 2017, Takemichi learns Hinata and her brother Naoto were killed by the Tokyo Manji Gang. After being pushed onto a platform he is transported to 2005. Naoto later deduces that physical contact between them triggers Takemichi's leaps. Across arcs (Moebius, Bloody Halloween/Valhalla, Black Dragons, Tenjiku, Bonten/Three Titans), Takemichi joins and steers the gang around Mikey (Manjiro Sano), repeatedly returning to a present that improves then collapses again—often because of Tetta Kisaki's ambition. Late revelations involve Shinichiro's original death and a stolen time-leap ability. In the manga's resolution, Takemichi and Mikey leap to 1998, reform the gang peacefully, prevent key deaths, and reach a future where Takemichi marries Hinata.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Trigger: handshake/contact with Naoto (later other conditions); ~12-year jumps.
- Present updates after past interventions; multiple failed "good" presents before final rewrite.
- Anime seasons cover through Tenjiku; War of the Three Titans arc anime set for 2026.
