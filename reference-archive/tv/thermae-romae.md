# Thermae Romae (テルマエ・ロマエ, 2012–)

## Short blurb
Stumped Roman bath architect Lucius keeps slipping through tunnels under spas into modern Japanese bathhouses, steals “flat-faced slave” innovations, and revolutionizes Hadrian-era thermae—one gag at a time.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Mari Yamazaki manga (Manga Taishō; Tezuka Short Story Award). Each chapter: Lucius faces a design crisis, slips to Japan (sento, home baths, waterparks, fertility festivals, zoos), misreads modernity as exotic slave tech, returns with solutions for Emperor Hadrian. Friend sculptor Marcus; polyglot geisha Satsuki in some adaptations. Media: 2012 DLE Noitamina flash (6 eps); live-action films 2012 & *II* 2014; Netflix ONA *Thermae Romae Novae* (2022); *Redux* manga sequel (2024–). Soft TT comedy: portal is bath-linked, not a machine with rules diagrams.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Repeatable Rome ↔ Japan slips triggered by water/steam.
- Bootstrap of Roman bath tech via Japanese “future.”
- Anthology problem-of-the-week structure.
