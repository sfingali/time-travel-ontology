# Alice (앨리스, 2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Agency tourism (2050 ↔ past), Book of Prophecy, mother/son identity knots — community explainers map eras and “Teacher” faction.

## Short blurb
In 2050, temporal agency Alice sends clients to visit the dead; detective Park Jin-gyeom (alexithymia) investigates time travelers while meeting physicist Yoon Tae-yi, who resembles his murdered mother.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) In 2050, Alice agents Yoon Tae-yi and Yoo Min-hyuk travel to 1992 Seoul for the Book of Prophecy (predicts fates / end of time travel). Rogue “Teacher” agent Lee Se-hoon murders Tae-yi’s father over the book; the father gives the last page to young Tae-yi before adult Tae-yi arrives. Pregnant Tae-yi refuses abortion (time-travel radiation risk), disappears with the book as Park Sun-young, and raises son Park Jin-gyeom—born with alexithymia and latent life-or-death time manipulation. In 2010 Jin-gyeom finds his mother murdered; by 2020 he is a detective uncovering Alice, the Teacher, and the truth of her death while Tae-yi’s lookalike physicist enters his life. AsianWiki: he struggles to stop Alice-driven harm as they unravel time-travel secrets.
