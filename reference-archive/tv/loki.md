# Loki (2021-2023)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Sacred Timeline / loom / branch diagrams bridge Endgame’s time-heist lecture to Multiverse Saga.

## Short blurb
Variant Loki arrested by the TVA for nexus deviation learns the Sacred Timeline was pruned for He Who Remains—until Sylvie’s murder of him explodes infinite branches; S2 ends with Loki becoming the living stabilizer of the timeline tree.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**S1:** Post-Endgame variant Loki (escaped with Tesseract) tried by TVA. Mobius partners him to hunt Sylvie. Reveals: TVA workers are variants; Time-Keepers fake; Citadel at End of Time holds He Who Remains (Kang variant) who weaponized Alioth and pruned branches to stop multiversal Kang wars. Sylvie kills him; branches bloom freely—Multiverse Saga ignition.

**S2:** Temporal Loom overload; Ouroboros tech; Ravonna, Judge/General Dox pruning crimes; Victor Timely (another Kang variant) as loom engineer. Spaghettification crises. Finale: Loki time-slips across TVA history, accepts burden, destroys loom, and physically holds branching timelines as a Yggdrasil-like throne—alone, allowing free branches without Kang conquest—at personal cost of endless vigil.

## Temporal structure
- TVA outside time; Timedoor hop across history.
- Pruning = timeline deletion (or exile to Void).
- Loom = throughput device for sacred/branched flow.
- Loki’s end state = living mechanism replacing loom.

## Paradox / theme analysis
- Bureaucracy as destiny manipulation.
- Bootstrap: future Loki influences past TVA events via time-slipping.
- Free will vs peace: He Who Remains’s authoritarian stability vs Sylvie’s freedom vs Loki’s sacrificial stewardship.
- Direct continuity from Endgame “changing past branches” rule clarification.
