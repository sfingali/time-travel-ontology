# Puella Magi Madoka Magica (魔法少女まどか☆マギカ, 2011)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Homura's repeated timelines / month loops extensively charted by fans.

## Short blurb
Middle-school girls contract with the alien Kyubey to become magical girls; Homura Akemi has already looped the same month countless times trying to save Madoka Kaname from a grisly fate.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) In Mitakihara, Madoka Kaname and Sayaka Miki meet Kyubey, who offers any wish in exchange for fighting witches. Transfer student Homura Akemi tries to stop Madoka from contracting. After mentor Mami Tomoe is killed and the girls learn Soul Gems turn into Grief Seeds when despair overwhelms them—transforming magical girls into witches—Sayaka contracts to heal her crush, then falls into despair and becomes a witch. Kyubey reveals the Incubators harvest magical-girl emotions to counteract entropy. Madoka learns Homura is a magical girl from a different timeline who has repeated the same month countless times via time travel to save Madoka. When Walpurgisnacht approaches and Homura fails, Madoka contracts anyway: she wishes to erase witches from past, present, and future. The paradoxical wish remakes the universe into one governed by the Law of Cycles, purifying magical girls before they become witches; only Homura remembers Madoka.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Homura's shield enables personal month-scale resets; each loop is a full rewrite attempt converging on Madoka's fate.
- Final wish is a universe-scale reality rewrite rather than a simple loop exit.
- Rebellion (2013 film) and later Walpurgisnacht: Rising continue Homura/Madoka cosmology.
