# Doraemon — Time Machine & notable TT movie arcs (1979–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Gadget encyclopedias and film-by-film era maps (Birth of Japan, Wan-Nyan Spacetime Odyssey, Steam-Powered Time Machine 2027) are endlessly charted by fans.

## Short blurb
22nd-century robot cat Doraemon aids Nobita with a pocket arsenal whose **Time Machine** (desk drawer / hill tunnel), Anywhere Door, and time accessories enable gag-scale history trips—and several feature films built entirely on spacetime tourism.

## Detailed plot summary / franchise notes
(Source: EN Doraemon film list + character pages.) Doraemon is sent from 2112 by descendant Sewashi to fix Nobita’s ruined future. Recurring TT tools: **Time Machine**, **Anywhere Door** (spatial), time TV/cloth, etc. TV episodes casually visit Edo, prehistoric eras, Nobita’s future children. Feature films with strong TT/alt-history cores include:
- *Nobita and the Birth of Japan* (1989) / *2016* remake — prehistoric Japan.
- *The Record of Nobita’s Parallel Visit to the West* (1988) — parallel Journey to the West.
- *Nobita’s Diary on the Creation of the World* (1995) — world-building diary.
- *Nobita in the Wan-Nyan Spacetime Odyssey* (2004) — explicit spacetime odyssey.
- Upcoming *Nobita’s Steam-Powered Time Machine* (announced for 2027).
Plus *Stand by Me Doraemon* films’ future-marriage / destiny beats. One archive entry covers the **franchise TT cluster** rather than 45 separate film pages.
