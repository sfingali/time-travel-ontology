# Devs (2020)

## Short blurb
Amaya’s Devs division simulates past and future under Forest’s grief-driven determinism; Lily’s investigation collides with a golden vault’s block-universe theology.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Sergei’s death after joining Devs; Lily and Jamie probe. Forest (Offerman) uses the machine to revisit dead daughter Amaya; Katie and team debate many-worlds vs determinism. Climax projections of Lily’s choices; ending tableau in a simulated garden after real deaths—Garland’s thesis on free will vs prediction. FX/Hulu limited series.

## Analysis
- Kin to Arrival’s block universe without alien language.
- Magenta/gold visual code; vacuum-sealed Devs cube.
- Explicit philosophy-of-physics dialogue.
