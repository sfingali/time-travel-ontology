# A Step into the Past (尋秦記, 2001)

## Short blurb
HK VIPPU inspector Hong Siu-lung is sent to film Qin coronation, overshoots to 250 BC Zhao, and accidentally midwifes Ying Zheng’s rise—until the First Emperor erases “Hong Siu-lung” from history.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 40-ep TVB Jade classic (Oct–Dec 2001); Louis Koo, Raymond Lam, Jessica Hsuan, Sonija Kwok. After museum hostage heroics and a breakup, Hong joins Lee Siu-chiu’s time experiment; error drops him three years early in Zhao. Martial skill + future knowledge make him kingmaker: he maneuvers Chiu Poon into becoming Ying Ching / Qin Shi Huang, romances Sin-yau, Wu Ting-fong, Kam Ching (lookalike of modern ex), Princess Chiu Sin. Realizing he enabled tyranny, he refuses court; Ying decrees his name purged—motivating the historical book-burning. Finale: pastoral exile; son wants the name Hong Yu (Xiang Yu)—Hong’s English “Shit!” punchline. Film sequel *Back to the Past* (2025). Landmark chuanyue drama.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Machine mishap + fixed return coordinates.
- Predestination: traveler causes the history he studied.
