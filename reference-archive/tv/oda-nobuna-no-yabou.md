# The Ambition of Oda Nobuna (織田信奈の野望, 2012)

## Short blurb
Modern student Yoshiharu wakes in a Sengoku Japan where famous warlords are women—chiefly Oda Nobuna—and his future knowledge becomes the campaign’s cheat code.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN Wikipedia.) Light-novel isekai: protagonist slips into an alternate Warring States where gender-flipped historical figures wage unification. Aligning with **Oda Nobuna**, he applies modern strategy/knowledge, constantly risking butterfly effects on “history” he half-remembers. 2012 TV anime; tone is fanservice-historical hybrid. Candidate #22—file as JP TT/isekai adjacent to *Nobunaga Concerto* and *Samurai Commando*.
