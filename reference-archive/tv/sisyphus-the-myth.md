# Sisyphus: The Myth (시지프스: the myth, 2021)

## Short blurb
Genius engineer Han Tae-sul meets Kang Seo-hae, a warrior from a nuclear-war future who traveled back via the Uploader to stop him inventing that very time machine—and together they try to break the loop.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis/cast.) After Tae-sul (Cho Seung-woo) saves a flight from crashing he is pulled into conspiracies around Quantum and Time, the company he co-founded. Seo-hae (Park Shin-hye) arrives from a bunker-scarred future Korea to prevent Tae-sul's death and the Uploader's creation. Brother Tae-san faked death to hunt Sigma (Seo Won-ju / Seo Gil-bok), a classmate-turned-investor who becomes Advance Team leader and war instigator. Control Bureau Team 7 polices illegal immigrants from the future; Asia Mart fronts time-traveler logistics. 16 episodes, JTBC/Netflix, Feb–Apr 2021. Critics noted inconsistent time-travel rules late in the run (SCMP).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Uploader sends people/objects to the past; Control Bureau vs Advance Team faction war.
- Classic bootstrap: Seo-hae fights the machine that sent her.
