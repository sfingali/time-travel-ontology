# Occult Academy (世紀末オカルト学院, 2010)

## Short blurb
Time agent Fumiaki Uchida is sent from alien-invaded 2012 to Waldstein Academy in 1999 to destroy the Nostradamus Key with occult-hating new headmistress Maya—before July 21 brings the end.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 13-ep A-1 Pictures (Jul–Sep 2010), third Anime no Chikara project. Maya Kumashiro inherits the Occult Academy she vowed to destroy after her occult-obsessed father’s death. Fumiaki (#6, identity “Abe Minoru”) arrives with specialized phones to investigate occult cases leading to the Key. Childhood celebrity psychic burnout; Maya’s Christmas-gift academy revelation softens her hate. Episode cases (demons, near-death, etc.) advance the Key hunt toward preventing the 1999 alien invasion that created 2012. NIS America Blu-ray; Crunchyroll simulcast.
