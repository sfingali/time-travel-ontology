# Babylon 5 (temporal arcs) (1993–1998)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Valen/Sinclair bootstrap and B4 displacement timelines are among TV’s most diagrammed closed loops.

## Short blurb
In *Babylon Squared* and the two-part *War Without End*, station Babylon 4 is yanked through time; Jeffrey Sinclair fulfills destiny as Minbari holy figure Valen—a textbook identity bootstrap powering the Shadow War’s ancient history.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**“Babylon Squared” (S1):** Four years after vanishing, Babylon 4 reappears. Sinclair and Garibaldi evacuate survivors; mysterious Zathras speaks of “The One.” Temporal stresses age some crew; the station slips away again. Parallel: Delenn is summoned by the Grey Council.

**“War Without End” I–II (S3):** Sinclair returns from Minbar with a mission: steal B4 and send it 1,000 years into the past to serve as a base in the previous Shadow War. Using Epsilon 3’s Great Machine (Draal) and tachyon/time tech, Sheridan, Delenn, Ivanova, Sinclair, and Zathras board the shifting station. Time flashes show Sheridan’s possible Centauri-Prime doom. Sinclair remains in the past, using a chrysalis device (akin to Delenn’s) to become **Valen**—the Minbari leader who reorganized their caste society. Thus Sinclair *always was* the historical Valen whose legend shaped Delenn’s people and the war’s first round. Zathras’s “The One” remarks braid Sinclair/Sheridan/Delenn across eras.

Other temporal notes: *Deconstruction of Falling Stars* flashes far futures; *Sleeping in Light* closes Sheridan’s timed lifespan after Lorien; 2023 animated *The Road Home* adds multiverse sliding. Core TT thesis remains the Valen loop.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **B4 disappearance (backstory) / Squared reappearance.**
2. **Partial evacuation; Zathras lore.**
3. **S3 heist:** take B4 into the past on purpose.
4. **Identity reveal:** Sinclair → Valen.
5. **Stable history:** Minbari past always contained the bootstrap.

## Paradox analysis

- **Identity bootstrap:** Valen exists because Sinclair becomes him—information/person closed loop.
- **Station as object bootstrap:** B4’s past presence depends on future theft.
- **Multiple “The One”:** Layered destiny across characters without breaking the Valen core.
- **Serialized payoff:** Seeds in S1 bloom in S3—arc TV’s TT masterclass.
- **Not branching wish-fulfillment:** The loop *is* history; success preserves continuity.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

PTEN/TNT; Straczynski’s planned five-year novel. Fan wikis (Lurker’s Guide) annotate continuity obsessively. Often cited beside *Dark* and *Primer* in bootstrap pedagogy.
