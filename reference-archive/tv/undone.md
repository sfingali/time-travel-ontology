# Undone (2019-2022)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
After a car crash, Alma discovers she can time-travel—or is experiencing mental illness—guided by her dead father.

## Detailed plot summary
Amazon rotoscope. S1: Alma tries to prevent father’s death; reality softens. S2: expands family history and consequences of changing events. Show deliberately sustains ambiguity between psychic TT and psychosis while depicting vivid travel. Formal experimentation matches theme.
