# Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World (Re:ゼロから始める異世界生活, 2016–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Per-arc Return by Death checkpoint maps (Capital, Mansion/Rem, White Whale/Witch Cult, Sanctuary, Priestella, etc.) are among the most-charted loop structures in anime fandom—pair with Steins;Gate worldline charts.

## Short blurb
Convenience-store NEET Subaru Natsuki is dumped into Lugunica with one broken gift: **Return by Death**—when he dies, he rewinds to an invisible checkpoint, alone with the memories, under a witch’s secrecy curse that punishes spoilers with shadow hands and madness.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers; anime-forward)
(Source: EN Wikipedia premise + widely documented anime arc beats; LN ongoing.)

**Authority:** Return by Death (RbD) is involuntary, death-triggered, and checkpointed by an unseen hand (Satella / Witch of Envy association). Only Subaru retains memories. Telling others invites the Witch’s punishment. Checkpoints advance or shift mid-arc, stranding him with irreversible losses until the next death.

**Arc 1 / S1 early (Capital):** Subaru helps silver-haired half-elf **Emilia** (Puck) recover an insignia from thief Felt and old man Rom; he is murdered repeatedly by Elsa Granhiert (“Bowel Hunter”) until he coordinates a raid that saves Emilia’s candidacy path and earns him a place at Margrave **Roswaal**’s mansion.

**Arc 2 / Mansion:** Demon-maid twins **Rem** and **Ram**; librarian **Beatrice**. Loop cluster: curse/Demon Beast attacks, Rem killing “suspicious” Subaru in some loops, trust collapse, then alliance. Subaru’s psychological break and Rem’s confession are series landmarks. Ends with mansion household alive and Rem’s devotion locked in.

**Arc 3 / Royal Selection & White Whale:** Candidates (Emilia, Priscilla, Anastasia, Crusch, Felt). Subaru’s arrogance at the royal selection humiliates Emilia. Subsequent loops include the White Whale battle, Witch Cult Sin Archbishop **Petelgeuse** possession arcs, and Rem’s memory erasure from the world via Gluttony—Subaru alone remembers her. One of the most diagrammed checkpoint ladders in the show.

**Arc 4 / Sanctuary (S2):** Emilia’s trial in the Witch’s Sanctuary; Echidna’s tea parties; Garfiel; Ryuzu clones; Roswaal’s gospel-driven looping agenda vs Subaru’s refusal to treat lives as resets. Rabbits (Great Rabbit) death is author-cited as the most gruesome. Subaru vows to win *without* relying on RbD as a plan-A.

**Later anime (S3+):** Priestella city arc / other Sin Archbishops; continuing Emilia camp vs Cult. Season 3 split-cours 2024–25; S4 announced 2026. OVAs (*Memory Snow*, *The Frozen Bond*) side-story.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure
1. Death → blackout → wake at last checkpoint (minutes to days earlier).
2. Information advantage only Subaru holds; allies experience first-pass timeline.
3. Checkpoint moves after major “saved” states—old save points become unreachable.
4. Secrecy curse blocks coordination that would make loops trivial.
5. Psychological damage accumulates across subjective years of dying.

## Paradox analysis
- Not branching multiverse TV: world appears single; resets overwrite.
- Memory-only traveler = classic loop epistemology (*Groundhog Day*, *Maanaadu*, *Edge of Tomorrow*) with horror cost.
- Witch/Authority metaphysics (compatible authorities, Satella’s love) complicate “just a save-scum skill.”
- Roswaal’s gospel creates antagonistic competing loop planner.

## Endings / production notes
Web novel on Narō (2012–); MF Bunko J LNs illustrated by Otsuka; Yen Press EN. Anime: White Fox; S1 2016 (25 eps + cour); S2 2020–21; S3 2024–25. Massive LN sales; Sugoi Japan awards. Author cites *Familiar of Zero* fanfic culture and *Lost World: Jurassic Park* for rabbit death inspiration.
