# Terminator: The Sarah Connor Chronicles (2008–2009)

## Short blurb
After *T2*, Sarah and John jump from 1999 to 2007 with protector Cameron (Summer Glau) to forestall Skynet. Two seasons of FBI heat, rival terminators, Cromartie, and Catherine Weaver’s liquid mystery end on a cliffhanger cancellation.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Series premiere: Sarah (Lena Headey), John (Thomas Dekker), and newly arrived Cameron escape 1999 via bank-vault time displacement to 2007, skipping a lethal FBI trap. They war against Skynet’s building blocks—Cyberdyne successors, AI researchers—while John attends school under aliases. Cromartie hunts John; Derek Reese arrives from the future; FBI agent Ellison seeks truth.

S2 introduces **Catherine Weaver** (Shirley Manson), a liquid-metal shape-shifter running a tech corporation with ambiguous anti-Skynet aims, and her “daughter” Savannah. John Henry, an AI in a terminator chassis, becomes a moral mirror. Finale: John jumps forward alone seeking answers; Sarah’s fate cliffhangs—Fox canceled the show.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **1999→2007 vault jump** — skip into new status quo.
2. **Protector procedural** vs emerging Skynet infrastructure.
3. **Future soldiers** (Derek) and enemy infiltrators.
4. **Weaver / John Henry** myth arc.
5. **Cancellation cliff** — John forward again.

## Paradox analysis

- **Franchise branching:** Explicitly ignores *T3*’s Judgment Day date; keeps *T2* “no fate” thesis.
- **Multiple displacements:** War is fought with repeated one-way jumps.
- **AI ethics:** Cameron/John Henry blur tool vs person—time war’s moral center.
- **Bootstrap war:** Future sends agents to create the past that creates the future.

## Endings / production notes

Fox; critically liked, ratings-canceled. Often cited as the best post-*T2* Terminator media. Headey between fantasy epics.
