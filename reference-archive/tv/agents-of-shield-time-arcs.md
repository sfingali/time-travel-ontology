Monolith planets, Framework VR, and Chronicom time war culminate in season 7’s history-hopping finale.

## Detailed plot summary
Key arcs: S5 future Lighthouse dystopia; S4 Framework virtual rewrite; S7 team leap through decades battling Chronicoms trying to erase S.H.I.E.L.D. from history. Daisy, Coulson android, and time-drive Zephyr are central. Dense fan timelines.
