# Dark (2017-2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Family trees, 33-year triquetra maps, and Adam/Eva world charts are mandatory; EN-facing explainers abound despite German production.

## Short blurb
In Winden, missing children expose a 33-year time knot binding the Kahnwald, Nielsen, Doppler, and Tiedemann families—bootstrap paradoxes, Sic Mundus, a parallel world, and an origin world beyond both.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers; seasons 1–3)

**S1:** 2019 disappearances; Jonas Kahnwald learns caves link 2019↔1986↔1953. Mikkel Nielsen becomes Michael Kahnwald (Jonas’s father)—bootstrap parenthood. Ulrich’s revenge path; Noah’s experiments; nuclear plant secrets.

**S2:** Apocalypse approaches 2020; Sic Mundus (Adam = elderly Jonas) vs Claudia’s faction. God particle enables freer travel beyond 33-year gate. Jonas/Adam dual strategies to end the knot by destroying or preserving loops.

**S3:** Parallel world (Eva’s world, alternate Martha) mirrors and wars with Adam’s. The Unknown (tri-fold son of Jonas×Martha across worlds) is the knot’s genetic engine. Claudia reveals a third **Origin World** where H.G. Tannhaus built a time machine after a family car crash—splitting reality into Adam/Eva worlds. Jonas and Martha prevent the crash in 1971; machine never built; knot worlds cease; survivors in origin toast the dead who “never were.”

## Temporal structure
- **33-year cycle** as cave gate rhythm (1953–86–2019–2052…).
- Bootstrap family links: Charlotte↔Elisabeth mother/daughter loop; Mikkel/Michael; Agnes/The Unknown lines.
- Devices: chair, portable machine, God particle sphere, Sic Mundus HQ.
- Dual worlds + origin = exit from closed knot rather than endless Novikov.

## Paradox analysis
- Pure bootstrap genetics: several characters exist only because of time travel.
- Adam’s “destroy both worlds” vs Eva’s “preserve loop” vs Claudia’s origin fix.
- Ending is **reality rewrite / uncreation** of the knot universes—closer to destiny manipulation than a stable closed loop.
