# Dark (2017–2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Family trees, 33-year triquetra maps, and Adam/Eva world charts are mandatory; EN explainers abound despite German production.

## Short blurb

In Winden, missing children expose a 33-year time knot binding the Kahnwald, Nielsen, Doppler, and Tiedemann families — bootstrap paradoxes, Sic Mundus, a parallel world, and an origin world beyond both.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

## Overview Overview Children start vanishing from the German town of Winden, bringing to light the fractured relationships, double lives, and dark pasts of four families living there and unfurling a mystery that spans four generations. The first season begins in 2019, but later grows to include 1986 and 1953 via time travel , when members of the show's central families become aware of a wormhole in the cave system beneath the local nuclear power plant.

During the first season, secrets are revealed concerning the Kahnwald, Nielsen, Doppler, and Tiedemann families, and their lives begin crumbling as their ties are exposed. The conspiracy involves the missing children and the history of the town and its citizens. The second season continues the families' attempts to reunite with their missing loved ones several months after the first-season finale, in 2020, 1987 and 1954, respectively.

Additional storylines are set in 2053 and 1921. The second season introduces Sic Mundus Creatus Est , a major faction in the ongoing battle for the ultimate fate of the people of Winden and the world. The season counts down to the apocalypse . The third season follows the four families across time in the wake of the apocalypse in 2020. It introduces a parallel world tethered to the first.

The third season continues the 1954, 1987, 2020, and 2053 storylines in the first world, while also adding a new 1888 storyline and two 2019 and 2052 storylines in the second world, as the factions further their own desires for each world. The season also showcases the main events across all of these years, continuing the events of the season while also serving as backstory for the events of the first two seasons.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

**Season 1 (2017):** 2019 disappearances (incl. Erik, Mikkel). Jonas Kahnwald discovers cave passages linking 2019 ↔ 1986 ↔ 1953. Mikkel Nielsen becomes Michael Kahnwald — Jonas’s father — parental bootstrap. Ulrich’s revenge path across decades; Noah’s chair experiments; nuclear plant / barrels secrets; Claudia’s emerging long game.

**Season 2 (2019):** Months later toward 2020 apocalypse. Sic Mundus (Adam = elderly Jonas) vs Claudia’s faction. God particle enables freer travel beyond the 33-year cave gate. Dual Jonas/Adam strategies to end or preserve the knot. 1921 strand added; family tree densifies (Charlotte ↔ Elisabeth mother/daughter loop begins to surface).

**Season 3 (2020):** Apocalypse aftermath; **Eva’s parallel world** (alt-Martha) mirrors and wars with Adam’s world. The Unknown (tri-fold son of Jonas × Martha across worlds) is the genetic engine of the knot. Claudia reveals a third **Origin World** where H.G. Tannhaus built a time machine after a family car crash — splitting reality into Adam/Eva worlds. Jonas and alt-Martha prevent the 1971 crash; machine never built; knot worlds cease; survivors in origin toast those who “never were.”

**Cycle math:** 33-year rhythm (1953–86–2019–2052…) structures cave travel; God particle breaks some limits; bootstrap genetics bind family trees into closed knots until origin repair.

## Paradox analysis

- **Bootstrap genetics:** Several characters exist only because of time travel (Mikkel/Michael; Charlotte/Elisabeth loop; The Unknown).
- **Closed knot vs exit:** Seasons 1–2 feel Novikov-stable; Season 3 offers **uncreation** of the knot universes via origin-world intervention.
- **Adam vs Eva vs Claudia:** Destroy both worlds / preserve the loop forever / fix the origin accident — three strategies.
- **Dual-world war:** Parallel Winden is not escape until the third world is known.
- **Diagram necessity:** Family trees are functionally required to follow identity — Vulture/ScreenRant/Fandom charts are part of reception.
- **Destiny rhetoric:** Characters quote/live “Sic mundus creatus est”; ending reframes destiny as engineered split trauma.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Netflix German original; three seasons planned as a closed story. English dub/subs drove global diagram culture. Ending is origin-world dinner — knot worlds erased. No extra season retconning the finale as of public record.
