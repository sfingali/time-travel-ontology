# Early Edition (1996–2000)

## Short blurb
Chicagoan Gary Hobson mysteriously receives tomorrow’s *Sun-Times* each dawn—delivered with a ginger cat—and spends four CBS seasons preventing tragedies, wrestling moral triage, and slowly mapping a lineage of paper-bearers.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

After wife Marcia throws him out, stockbroker Gary moves into the Blackstone Hotel and begins finding next-day papers at 6:30 a.m. wherever he is. Friends Chuck Fishman (cynical ex-colleague) and blind former receptionist Marissa Clark help him parse headlines. Gary quits brokerage to prevent accidents, crimes, and disasters; Chuck initially schemes sports bets and tips; Marissa is conscience.

Detective Zeke Crumb becomes a recurring ally who never quite learns the paper’s secret. Gary confirms predecessor Lucius Snow once held the gift. Season 2 adds McGinty’s bar shifts; Chuck exits as series regular after S2 (guest returns). Later foils: Patrick Quinn, Erica & Henry Paget. Occasional episodes wake Gary in other eras to fix past wrongs.

Mythology stays soft: a shadowy group can grant the paper; supernatural logistics unexplained. Series finale-adjacent episode “Time” (S4E20, aired early): Lucius once saved child-Gary and passed a pocketknife whose initials morph to the next bearer. Gary eventually passes the knife to young Lindsey Romick after her grandfather’s death—implying she will inherit when Gary cannot continue. 90 episodes; canceled May 2000.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Gift onset** post-divorce; cat + paper ritual.
2. **Trio dynamic** Gary / Chuck / Marissa.
3. **Case-of-the-week prevention** rewriting headlines in-camera.
4. **Lineage breadcrumbs** (Snow; knife initials).
5. **Cast churn** after Stevens leaves; romantic subplots.
6. **Inheritance handoff** to Lindsey.

Creators pitched via a mock next-day O.J. headline newspaper (Aug 1995). Not based on 1944 film *It Happened Tomorrow* despite resemblance (creators’ claim).

## Paradox analysis

- **Information-only TT:** No body travel in the default mode—causality edits via foreknowledge.
- **Headline as ripple meter:** Text updates when Gary succeeds—friendly on-screen ontology.
- **Moral triage paradox:** Whom to save when multiple tragedies print same day.
- **Gift chain:** Knife/initials as soft bootstrap of responsibility, not physics.
- **Kin to Frequency / Deja Vu:** Communication with adjacent time without machines.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

CBS Saturday staple; Chicago location shooting (McGinty’s = real Franklin/Illinois corner building). Snuffy Walden theme. Fox Family syndication; complete DVD 2018. 2022 CBS reboot pilot (Alice Eve) not picked up.
