# The OA (2016-2019)

## Short blurb
Prairie claims she traveled to other dimensions via near-death experiences and seeks to open a path with five movements; Season 2 reframes cast as other-universe versions in a San Francisco mystery.

## Detailed plot summary
Ambitious metafiction: captives Hap studies, the Invisible Self, and a jump into a dimension where The OA is a TV show. Canceled before full resolution; fan diagrams of dimension hops abound.
