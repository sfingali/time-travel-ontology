# Nobunaga Concerto (信長協奏曲, 2014)

## Short blurb
High-schooler Saburō slips to Sengoku Japan, is asked by lookalike Oda Nobunaga to take his place, and steers history with a modern textbook while the real Nobunaga reinvents himself as Akechi Mitsuhide—and other time travelers (Dōsan, Yasuke, Hisahide) litter the era.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Manga by Ayumi Ishii (*Monthly Shōnen Sunday*); 10-ep anime (Jul–Sep 2014), 11-ep Fuji TV drama + 2016 film (Shun Oguri dual role). Burned-out Nobunaga drafts Saburō as double; Saburō uses school notes to hit Okehazama, Mino, shogun politics. Tokichirō (future Hideyoshi) begins as Imagawa spy; Matsudaira/Ieyasu gets a modern porn mag and lifelong debt. Real Nobunaga returns masked as Mitsuhide. Fellow travelers: Saitō Dōsan (ex-cop, 30 years stuck), baseball-player Yasuke, yakuza-turned-Matsunaga Hisahide. Anime ends mid-campaign (Mount Hiei choice). Shogakukan Manga Award; 3.5M+ copies by 2016.
