# Stargate SG-1 (temporal episodes) (1997–2007)

## Short blurb
Across ten seasons and the film *Continuum*, SG-1 breaks time via solar-flare Stargate misjumps (1969/2010), Ancient loop altars (*Window of Opportunity*), Aschen sterilization futures, and Ba’al’s timeline purge—mixing beloved comedy loops with apocalypse prevention.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**“1969” (S2):** A solar flare sends SG-1 to 1969; young Hammond helps; they hitch forward via another flare. Fish-out-of-water Cold War comedy with closed return.

**“2010” (S4):** Alternate future: Earth allies with the bland advanced Aschen, defeats the Goa’uld, then learns Aschen drugs sterilize humanity. Aging SG-1 members sacrifice to fire a note backward through the Gate warning present-SGC never to dial P4C-970. Sequel episode “2001” plays the warning’s payoff.

**“Window of Opportunity” (S4):** On P4X-639, geomagnetic storms + Ancient altar trap O’Neill and Teal’c in a ~10-hour loop only they remember. Archaeologist Malikai seeks to revisit his dead wife. After subjective months, O’Neill/Teal’c learn Ancient enough to finish Daniel’s translation; golf through the Gate, pottery, and a mid-loop Carter kiss fill boredom. O’Neill’s grief over his son persuades Malikai to shut the device down. Widely voted among SG-1’s best episodes (Sci-Fi Channel / MSN polls).

**Other notables:** “Brief Candle”-adjacent aging; various solar-flare and crystal misadventures; *Stargate: Continuum* (2008 film)—Ba’al alters the past so SG-1 and the SGC never exist; survivors must restore history from a submarine/Arctic scrap.

Tone oscillates: *Window* is pure Groundhog comedy; *2010*/Continuum are sterilization/erase horror.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Flare tourism** (1969) — accidental past, assisted return.
2. **Aschen branch** (2010→2001) — future warning note as closed-loop message.
3. **Ancient loop** (Window) — two-person memory island inside base Groundhog Day.
4. **Continuum wipe** — villainous past edit erasing the team’s ontology.
5. **Franchise glue:** Gate + stellar mechanics as recurring TT engine.

Mallozzi/Mullie wrote *Window* after Wright steered them from dark apocalypse-loop toward *Groundhog Day*; DeLuise padded runtime with improvised bits when the shoot ran short.

## Paradox analysis

- **Solar flare + wormhole:** Franchise’s “natural” TT—navigation error, not TEC.
- **Partial memory loops:** Only some characters retain state (*Window*)—forces comedy from asymmetric knowledge.
- **Note-from-future:** *2010*’s warning is a classic information closed loop that successfully prevents the branch.
- **Continuum:** Hard branching erase; restoration mission akin to *First Contact*.
- **Malikai’s grief machine:** Ancient tech that never worked as intended—failed plague escape becomes personal time addiction.

## Endings / cuts / production notes
