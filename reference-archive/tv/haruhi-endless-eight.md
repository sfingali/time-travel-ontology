# Endless Eight (Haruhi Suzumiya arc, 2009 broadcast)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Famous eight-episode televised loop; iteration counts and variation charts are a fandom staple.

## Short blurb
Haruhi unconsciously loops the last two weeks of August ~15,532 times because summer still feels incomplete; only after Kyon gathers everyone to finish homework together does September 1 arrive.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode list for the 2009 re-airing.) Across eight nearly identical TV episodes (*Endless Eight I–VIII*), the SOS Brigade repeats Haruhi's checklist of summer activities (pool, Bon festival, fireworks, part-time job, stargazing, beach, karaoke, etc.). Kyon suffers déjà vu; Mikuru cannot return to the future; Itsuki theorizes Haruhi has locked mid-to-late August into an infinite loop. Yuki reports iteration counts rising from ~15,498 into the 15,500s. Slight variations appear each episode (fully re-animated). On the 15,532nd loop (*VIII*), as Haruhi leaves on 30 August, Kyon calls the Brigade to finish summer homework together—the one ordinary shared-schoolwork experience her intellect had skipped. Satisfied she "used" summer fully, Haruhi lets time proceed; Kyon wakes on 1 September. Itsuki suggests superior intellect meant she had never needed to study with friends before.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Closed time loop powered by Haruhi's godlike unconscious will; exit requires fulfilling her unspoken desire.
- Formal experiment in televised repetition; novel/manga treat the loop more compactly.
- Arc sits chronologically after "Bamboo Leaf Rhapsody" and before "The Sigh of Haruhi Suzumiya" film-making arc.
