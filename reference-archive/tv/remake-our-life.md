# Remake Our Life! (ぼくたちのリメイク, 2021)

## Short blurb
Failed 28-year-old game creator Kyoya Hashiba wakes in 2006 just after arts-college admission, roommates with future “Platinum Generation” stars, and tries to remake his career—only to leap eleven years *forward* into a marriage timeline he must abandon.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode list.) 12-ep Feel adaptation (Jul–Sep 2021) of Nachi Kio’s MF Bunko J novels. Laid-off Kyoya regrets not attending art school; wakes pre-college, joins Share House Kitayama with Aki Shino (future illustrator Shino Akishima), singer Nanako, and writer Tsurayuki. Using future knowledge he pushes doujin game *HaruSora*, burns out the team, and Tsurayuki drops out. Keiko (time agent / alum) sends him to 2018 where he is married to Aki with daughter Maki, working free-to-play under Eiko—while friends’ dreams failed. He fixes a buggy launch, confesses burdens to Eiko, then chooses to return to the day after Tsurayuki quit, this time respecting autonomy. Anime ends mid-remake; novels concluded at vol. 12 (2023). Spin *Ver.β* explores never-leaping timeline.

## Timeline/structure notes
