# Someday or One Day (想見你, 2019–2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Dual eras, dual identities, soul crossings; Wikipedia hosts a multi-axis timeline table; fan charts prolific.

## Short blurb
Grieving Huang Yu-hsuan (2019) falls asleep to a Wu Bai cassette and awakens in 1998 inside high-schooler Chen Yun-ru—beside a boy who looks exactly like her dead boyfriend.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia premise + cast/plot outline.) Yu-hsuan texts her late boyfriend Wang Chuan-sheng; a Walkman and *The End of Love* tape arrive. On a bus she slips into Yun-ru's body after Yun-ru's car accident. The bedside boy is Li Zi-wei (same face as Chuan-sheng). Dual casting (Ko Chia-yen as Yu-hsuan/Yun-ru; Greg Hsu as Chuan-sheng/Zi-wei) unfolds romance, identity, and a traffic-accident mystery across 1998 and 2019. Souls cross among leads including Mo Jun-jie and the Hsieh brothers; rewriting fate risks paradox. Golden Bell Awards: Best Series, Best Actress (Ko), Best Writing. Korean Netflix remake *A Time Called You* (2023). Theatrical semi-continuation 2022.
