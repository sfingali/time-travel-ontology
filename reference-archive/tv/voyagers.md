# Voyagers! (1982–1983)

## Short blurb
Phineas Bogg—a Guide from a future corps of history-fixers—teams with orphan Jeffrey Jones; their Omni’s light glows red when history is wrong and green when back on track as they hop famous eras.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Family adventure: Bogg (Hexum) crashes into 1980s orphan Jeffrey’s life; together they use the handheld **Omni** to jump through time. Red light = divergence to correct (missing inventions, wrong battle outcomes, endangered historical children); green = success. Educational cameos (Wright brothers, Cleopatra-adjacent plots, American Revolution, etc.) sell NBC Saturday/family slots. Bogg’s Guide manual is incomplete; Jeffrey’s schoolbook knowledge often saves missions—kids’-empowerment twist. Short run (one season) but durable template for “fix the timeline” kids’ TT and a clear ancestor to *Timeless*’s history-of-the-week grammar.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Omni alarm** (red).
2. **Jump to broken historical node.**
3. **Identify divergence** via guidebook + Jeffrey’s facts.
4. **Adventure correction.**
5. **Green light → next call.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Guide corps ethic:** Institutional set-right, not personal revenge.
- **UI as ontology:** Traffic-light Omni makes status kid-readable.
- **Textbook bootstrap lite:** Jeffrey’s memory of “how it should be” guides repairs.
- **Soft stakes:** Educational optimism over dark butterfly horror.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

NBC; Hexum’s charismatic lead (later tragic off-show death). Merchandising and lunchbox footprint larger than episode count. Revival pitches recur.
