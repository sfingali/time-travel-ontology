# The Tatami Time Machine Blues (四畳半タイムマシンブルース, 2022)

## Short blurb
On Kyoto's hottest day, Ozu spills cola on the dorm AC remote; "I" and friends use a storage-room time machine to grab yesterday's remote—and nearly wreck the universe in a Morimi/Science SARU farce sequel to *The Tatami Galaxy*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: MAL synopsis / Disney+ episode synopses.) Narrator "Watashi" dreams of cooling his 4.5-tatami room until prankster Ozu destroys the only AC remote. A visitor from 25 years ahead brings (or reveals) a time machine; the group travels to the previous day to retrieve the remote before it breaks. Meddling multiplies timelines; Akashi and Watashi repeatedly leap to restore order and stop universal collapse. Episode 6 is an original prequel about Watashi joining a club that fantasizes about a fictional railway. Loosely adapts Morimi's novel (itself riffing Makoto Ueda's *Summer Time Machine Blues* play/film). Six-episode ONA (Science SARU, 2022) on Disney+/Hulu.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Small-stakes comedy time travel (AC remote) escalating to universe-threat loops.
- Spiritual sequel to *Tatami Galaxy*'s parallel campus years; mechanism shifts from year-loops to machine jumps.
