# Lost (time-travel arcs) (2004-2010)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Lostpedia skip maps, Orchid/wheel schematics, and Season 5 chronological reorderings are iconic; academic essays map Faraday’s rules vs Jughead.

## Short blurb
Island electromagnetic weirdness moves people through time. Season 4’s Desmond consciousness flashes introduce “the Constant”; Season 5’s wheel skips drop survivors into DHARMA-era 1970s. Faraday’s mantra—“Whatever happened, happened”—collides with the Jughead plan to change history. Season 6’s flash-sideways is afterlife, not a successful branch.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

### Mechanisms
- **Consciousness travel:** Desmond (and later others) unstick in time between eras; need a **Constant** (Penny) to survive (“The Constant”).
- **Physical skips:** Ben turns the frozen Wheel; later accidental wheel instability flings remaining survivors through island eras (1950s army, DHARMA 1970s, etc.).
- **Orchid Station:** DHARMA time research; donkey wheel linked to pocket of exotic energy.

### Season 5 — living in 1977
Sawyer, Juliet, Jin, et al. integrate into DHARMA. Faraday returns from Ann Arbor with a plan: the Swan drilling will hit energy → Incident → hatch button → Desmond failure → Oceanic 815 crash. He revises his immutable-time teaching in **“The Variable”**: people are variables with free will; detonating the buried **Jughead** hydrogen bomb might negate the energy and prevent the crash.

Faraday is shot by his mother Eloise (young) before completing the plan. Jack’s group still detonates Jughead at the Swan site (“The Incident”). White flash → Season 6 opens with apparent “Oceanic never crashed” LA timeline.

### Bootstraps (examples)
- Richard’s compass: Locke gives Richard a compass; later Locke receives it from Richard—object loop.
- Faraday’s journal / mother’s guidance.
- DHARMA and Others’ histories intertwined with future castaways’ actions.

### Season 6 resolution
Flash-sideways is a constructed afterlife meeting place (**“The End”**, Christian Shephard), not proof Jughead branched reality. Public consensus (Lostpedia; SIC Journal): Jughead was part of the original Incident; timeline preserved—**whatever happened, happened**.

## Paradox analysis

- **Primary model:** Novikov-like self-consistency with emotional tragedy (you become the past you tried to fix).
- **False branch bait:** S6 marketing and underwater Island tease a successful change; finale pulls rug.
- **Consciousness vs body:** Two TT modes with different rules (Constants vs wheel coordinates).
- **Memory paradox patch:** Faraday’s radiation-damaged memory explains why young Faraday forgets Desmond’s Oxford visit.
