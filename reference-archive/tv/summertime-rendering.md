# Summertime Rendering (サマータイムレンダ, 2022)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Hitogashima loop calendar / Shadow hierarchy widely charted by fans.

## Short blurb
Shinpei Ajiro returns to Hitogashima for childhood friend Ushio's funeral and discovers Shadows that copy humans—and that dying resets him through a time loop marked by his changed eye.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis + character notes.) After his parents' deaths Shinpei grew up with Ushio and Mio Kofune, then left for Tokyo. On 22 July 2018 he returns for Ushio's drowning funeral but finds strangle marks implying murder. Mio reports seeing Ushio's doppelgänger—island lore calls them Shadows, creatures that copy appearance and memories then kill and replace humans, led by Hiruko/Haine at Hito Shrine. Shinpei's right eye has turned light blue, marking the start of his ability to loop by dying and retain knowledge across resets. Allied with Mio, Shadow-Ushio (a "defective" copy), writer/fighter Hizuru Minakata (who hosts her dead twin Ryūnosuke's Shadow data), and friends on the island, he iterates strategies against Haine and hybrid priest Karikiri across festival nights. 25-episode anime (OLM, 2022) adapts the full manga; live-action film announced for 2027.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Death → reset to a prior checkpoint on the island; memory retention for Shinpei.
- Shadows create identity-theft paradoxes; copying limits and "defective" Ushio drive plot.
- Summer festival / Hiruko rites act as fixed temporal pressure.
