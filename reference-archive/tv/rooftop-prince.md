# Rooftop Prince (옥탑방 왕세자, 2012)

## Short blurb
Joseon Crown Prince Lee Gak and three retainers fall onto Park-ha’s Seoul rooftop in 2012; mistaking a modern secretary for his drowned wife’s reincarnation, he impersonates missing chaebol heir Yong Tae-yong to solve an 18th-century murder echo.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 20-episode SBS Wed–Thu drama (Mar–May 2012) starring Park Yoo-chun and Han Ji-min. After Crown Princess Hwa-yong’s mysterious drowning, Lee Gak, scholar Song Man-bo, bodyguard Woo Yong-sool, and eunuch Do Chi-san slip ~300 years forward onto the rooftop of resilient Park-ha—whose Joseon counterpart Bu-yong was scarred and displaced as bride by sister Hwa-yong. Chairman Yeo mistakes Lee Gak for lost grandson Tae-yong (pushed into the sea in New York by cousin Yong Tae-mu). Lee Gak plays along to approach secretary Hong Se-na (Hwa-yong’s double / Park-ha’s stepsister) believing marriage in 2012 will unlock the Joseon murder. Corporate intrigue, fish-out-of-water comedy (tracksuit “four amigos”), and Park-ha’s growing love collide with Tae-mu’s guilt. Doubles map: Se-na/Hwa-yong, Park-ha/Bu-yong, Tae-mu/Prince Muchang (revealed late). Strong overseas fandom (China/Japan); SBS Drama Awards haul for leads.

## Timeline/structure notes
- One-way-seeming Joseon→2012 slip with mirrored identities across eras.
- Murder mystery as temporal hinge rather than machine rules.
- Reincarnation/lookalike logic, not hard sci-fi.
