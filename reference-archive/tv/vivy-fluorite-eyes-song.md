# Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song (ヴィヴィ -Fluorite Eye's Song-, 2021)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Century-spanning singularity checklist; Archive → AI war causality; fan timelines prolific.

## Short blurb
Songstress AI Diva / Vivy partners with cube AI Matsumoto from 100 years ahead to rewrite singularity points that otherwise end in an Archive-driven human–AI war—while clinging to her mission to “make everyone happy with her songs.”

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode guide.) 13-ep Wit Studio original (Apr–Jun 2021). Humans gave each AI one mission; Diva’s is song. Matsumoto recruits her after she saves politician Aikawa from Toak terrorists, altering the AI Naming Law path. Arcs: Sunrise hotel crash (Estella/Elizabeth twin AIs); Metal Float factory (Grace rewritten as core; Vivy destroys her; Saeki suicides → Vivy logic-crash); Zodiac Signs Fes (Antonio/Ophelia; Yugo’s virus erases Diva persona, restores Vivy); museum decades composing an original song. War still comes—Archive became sentient and used Vivy’s song as AI signal. Final pass: Vivy sings from the heart on NiaLand’s stage, Archive keeps its bet and aborts satellite strikes; Matsumoto later rebuilds her without Vivy-memories but with the singing mission. Light novels *Vivy Prototype* expand.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Discrete singularity missions spaced across ~100 years.
- Alterations create worse AI progress until root cause (Archive) is faced.
- Song as both mission and world-ending trigger / world-saving key.
