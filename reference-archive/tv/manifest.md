# Manifest (2018-2023)

## Short blurb
Montego Air Flight 828 lands five years late; passengers receive Callings tied to a Death Date.

## Detailed plot summary
NBC then Netflix. Passengers missing years while aging only hours; divine/scientific Callings steer them. Mythology expands to Egyptian/Omega/Sapphire stones and Judgment. Series finale (Netflix) resolves Death Date stakes. Soft TT + destiny drama.
