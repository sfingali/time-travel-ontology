# Love Weaves Through a Millennium (相爱穿梭千年, 2015)

## Short blurb
Chinese remake of *Queen In-hyun’s Man*: Western Han scholar Gong Ming’s jade pendant hurls him 2,000 years to modern China, where he loves actress Lin Xiangxiang—cast as the empress he once served—until duty demands a near-impossible return.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: EN Wikipedia + DramaWiki.) **Gong Ming** (Jing Boran), imperial scholar supporting deposed Empress Xu against Zhao Feiyan intrigue / Wang Mang-era peril, activates a mystical jade pendant in a life-threatening ambush and arrives in 2015. He meets **Lin Xiangxiang** (Zheng Shuang), landing the Empress Xu role in a historical drama. Romance + culture clash; pendant allows limited peril-triggered travel. Gong insists on returning to save the empress; complications strand him from Xiangxiang’s present. ~20–24 eps, Hunan TV Feb–Mar 2015. Direct remake lineage from KR 2012 original.
