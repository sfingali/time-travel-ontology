# Kindred (2022)

## Short blurb
Dana Franklin is forcibly pulled from 2016 LA to antebellum Maryland plantations tied to her ancestry.

## Detailed plot summary
FX/Hulu adaptation of Butler’s novel. Involuntary time travel triggered by ancestors’ peril; slavery horror made present. Modern framing device expands Kevin relationship and family mystery. Essential Black TT canon on screen.
