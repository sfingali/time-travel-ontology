# Go Back Couple (고백부부, 2017)

## Short blurb
Exhausted 38-year-old spouses Choi Ban-do and Ma Jin-joo wake as their 20-year-old university selves and deliberately choose differently—impacting friends and family across the rewrite.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) Ban-do (pharma salesman bullied by clients) and Jin-joo (burned-out homemaker) hit a divorce breaking point and wish they had never met—then awaken as Hankook University students with adult memories. Unsure how long the slip lasts, they enjoy campus life and alter past choices (shy crush Min Seo-young; ROTC Nam-gil; friends Jae-woo, Dok-jae, Bo-reum, Seol). Decisions ripple through families and the cruel senior Park Hyun-suk's trajectory. 12 episodes, KBS2, Oct–Nov 2017; based on Naver webtoon *Do it one more time*.
