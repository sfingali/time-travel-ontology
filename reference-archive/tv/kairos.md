# Kairos (카이로스, 2020)

## Short blurb
Workaholic director Kim Seo-jin, living one month ahead, phones hard-working Han Ae-ri in the "past" month: he needs her to stop the disasters that took his daughter and wife; she needs him to find her missing mother.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis.) Kim Seo-jin's daughter vanishes; soon his wife Kang Hyun-chae dies by suicide from the shock. Han Ae-ri's mother disappears from a hospital. Seo-jin—existing roughly one month ahead of Ae-ri—calls her. They trade tasks across the offset: she tries to prevent his family's destruction; he searches for her mother. Thriller expands through Yujung Construction intrigue, detectives, and suspects around the kidnapping. 16 episodes, MBC, Oct–Dec 2020; themes billed as opportunity and choice (*kairos*). Shin Sung-rok and Lee Se-young lead.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Fixed ~one-month phone offset (not freely tunable travel); Signal/The Call family of linked-era communication.
- Actions in Ae-ri's "present" rewrite Seo-jin's nearer future.
