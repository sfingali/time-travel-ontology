# Faith / The Great Doctor (신의, 2012)

## Short blurb
Goryeo royal-guard captain Choi Young crosses “Heaven’s Gate” to 2012 Seoul, kidnaps plastic surgeon Yoo Eun-soo to save Queen Noguk—then politics, Yuan intrigue, and romance race the portal’s closing.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) King Gongmin and Princess Noguk are attacked; the queen is dying. Advisor urges Captain **Choi Young** (Lee Min-ho) through a mystical portal to fetch the “heaven’s doctor.” He abducts **Yoo Eun-soo** (Kim Hee-sun), a 2012 Seoul plastic surgeon (ex-general surgeon). She saves Noguk; the court forces her to stay as a political asset. Eun-soo and Choi fall in love while nobleman **Ki-chul** and Prince **Deok-heung** hunt the “Heaven’s doctor.” Woodalchi warriors protect the pair through 24 episodes (SBS, Aug–Oct 2012). Stakes: return through Heaven’s Door before it closes forever. Production was famously troubled (lead recasts, budget, unpaid crew; director Kim Jong-hak’s later tragedy)—but the portal + medical fish-out-of-water hook remains a landmark KR TT sageuk.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Portal is mission-gated (open/close), not a free commuter well.
- Modern → Goryeo one primary displacement; return is the season goal.
- Title 신의 / 神醫 puns faith vs divine doctor.
