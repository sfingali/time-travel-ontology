# Mr. Queen (철인왕후, 2020–2021)

## Short blurb
Blue House chef Jang Bong-hwan dies mid-scandal, wakes in 1851 inside Queen Cheorin’s body on the eve of her wedding to King Cheoljong, and must cook, scheme, and lake-dive his way home while Joseon court factions collide.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode guide.) 20-episode tvN hit based on Chinese web series *Go Princess Go*. Playboy chef Bong-hwan (Choi Jin-hyuk), framed over a fishhook in envoy food, falls from a high-rise into a pool and slips into Kim So-yong / Queen Cheorin (Shin Hye-sun) after her pre-wedding lake accident. King Cheoljong (Kim Jung-hyun) is a figurehead under Grand Queen Dowager Sunwon (Andong Kim) while pretending to be idle; he raids Kim warehouses by night. Bong-hwan-as-queen weaponizes modern cooking (ramyeon from scratch, food palanquin) to win Sunwon’s favor and refill the lake for return attempts; jumps fail or are interrupted. Parallel modern plot: Bong-hwan’s vegetative body is nearly unplugged, briefly yanking his soul home before he falls back. Court intrigue—Pungyang Jo vs Andong Kim, concubine Jo Hwa-jin, cousin Kim Byeong-in—mixes with identity leakage as So-yong’s memories bleed into Bong-hwan. Finale: near-death battle returns Bong-hwan to the present; original So-yong awakens; Cheoljong reclaims power; couple begins a new ruling era. Spin-off *Mr. Queen: The Bamboo Forest* (TVING). Peak cable rating ~17.4%; later Netflix global Top 10. Controversies: “tabloid” joke about the Veritable Records; Chinese-source-author comments.
