# Torchwood (2006-2011)

## Short blurb
Captain Jack’s Torchwood team polices Cardiff’s Rift—alien and temporal bleed from Doctor Who mythos.

## Detailed plot summary
Adult Who spinoff. Rift opens time/space anomalies; Jack’s immortality is a temporal wound. Children of Earth and Miracle Day shift focus, but S1–2 are rift-centric. Bridges Who continuity to standalone thrillers.

## Timeline/structure notes
