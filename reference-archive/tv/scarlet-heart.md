# Scarlet Heart / Bu Bu Jing Xin (步步惊心, 2011)

## Short blurb
Modern office worker Zhang Xiao slips into the Kangxi-era Qing court as Ma’ertai Ruoxi, loves rival princes amid the Nine Sons’ succession struggle, and learns history will not bend for her heart.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: EN Wikipedia.) **Zhang Xiao** (Liu Shishi) timeslips from contemporary China into early 18th-century Qing as **Ruoxi**. She navigates palace intrigue among Kangxi’s sons—especially the future Yongzheng (**Yinzhen**, Nicky Wu) and tragic Fourth/Eighth prince romantic quadrangle variants across cuts. Knowing fragments of “future” history, she tries to save loves and friends; the succession war still claims them. Emotional climax stresses the cruelty of fixed history for a traveler who remembers modern values. 35 eps landmark chuanyue (穿越) drama; spawned *Scarlet Heart 2* (Republic era, weaker TT), Korean remake *Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo*, and a whole wave of palace time-slip soaps.
