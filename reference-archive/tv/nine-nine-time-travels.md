# Nine: Nine Time Travels (나인: 아홉 번의 시간여행, 2013)

## Short blurb
News anchor Park Sun-woo finds nine magical incense sticks that each send him twenty years into the past—finite trips to avenge family tragedy and rewrite love, with every past change reshaping the present.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia concept/cast synopsis.) Sun-woo (Lee Jin-wook), a broadcast news anchor, suffers a family tragedy and obtains nine incense items enabling travel ~20 years back. He tries to pinpoint the right moment to protect his family and reclaim love with colleague/reporter Joo Min-young (Jo Yoon-hee), while brother Park Jung-woo holds keys to a crime mystery. Each past action ripples into altered presents affecting many lives including his own. Dual-era casting (adults vs 1992/93 younger selves, including Park Hyung-sik as young Sun-woo). 20 episodes, tvN, March–May 2013. ABC developed an unproduced U.S. remake. Ending left open to interpretation per contemporary coverage.

## Timeline/structure notes

- Strong butterfly emphasis; present updates after each stick.
