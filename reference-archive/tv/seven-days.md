# Seven Days (1998–2001)

## Short blurb
Ex-SEAL/CIA chrononaut Frank Parker pilots NSA Project Backstep’s Roswell-derived Chronosphere—limited to a seven-day “backstep”—to avert national-security disasters, while Element-115 malfunctions spawn loops, splits, and parallel flukes.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Secret Nevada base “Never Never Land.” The Chronosphere, reverse-engineered from Roswell tech and fueled by scarce **Element-115**, can send one human seven days into the past. Past instances of the Sphere vanish on arrival (“two instances cannot occupy the same space”). Transit requires “flying the needles”—agonizing joystick centering of gravitational axes; failure strands or kills.

Frank Parker—photographic memory, extreme pain tolerance, emotional volatility from Somalia torture—is chosen over stabler backup Craig Donovan. Team: Russian émigré Dr. Olga Vukavitch (mutual almost-romance continually erased by backsteps), security hardliner Nathan Ramsey (Frank’s foil), director Bradley Talmadge, scientist Mentnor (Roswell ties), and Sphere techs Ballard then “Hooter” Owsley.

Episode engine: catastrophe hits → Sphere charged → Frank backsteps → uses future knowledge under political constraints → often must also wrangle Sphere glitches (time loops including *Run Lola Run*-ish variants, mind-to-child, soul separation, parallel Earths, split selves, etc.). Running gags: sports bets ruined by timeline drift; Olga affection reset after each jump.

Three seasons / 66 episodes on UPN; canceled amid ratings and cast tension (Vail exits before finale; Whipple left with cancer). Justina Vail won a Saturn Award (2000).

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Backstep brief** — seven-day fuel hard cap.
2. **Disaster present** → charge → transit ordeal.
3. **Past intervention** under Ramsey/NSA politics.
4. **Malfunction subplot** as weekly wild card.
5. **Return to altered (hopefully saved) present.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Hard window constraint:** Title is the physics—cannot fix older mistakes.
- **Replacement rule:** Incoming Sphere deletes prior copy—limits stacking.
- **Personal romance paradox:** Olga arc dramatizes erased intimacies.
- **Malfunction anthology:** Treats experimental alien tech as unreliable narrator of causality.
- **Quantum Leap kinship:** Troubled operative + government project + case-of-week saves.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Paramount / Crowe Entertainment; mixed critic reception (premise called flimsy). Complete DVD (VEI) 2018. Filmed US/Canada.
