# Queen In-hyun's Man / Queen and I (인현왕후의 남자, 2012)

## Short blurb
Joseon scholar Kim Bung-do, fighting to reinstate deposed Queen Inhyeon, slips via talisman to 2012 and falls for struggling actress Choi Hee-jin—who has just been cast as Queen Inhyeon in a TV sageuk.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 1694: noble scholar **Kim Bung-do** (Ji Hyun-woo) survives his family’s massacre and supports Queen Inhyeon’s reinstatement against Lady Jang’s faction. 2012: obscure actress **Choi Hee-jin** (Yoo In-na) lands the Inhyeon role in *New Jang Heebin*. A mysterious talisman throws Bung-do into Seoul; culture-clash romance ensues (famous Gwanghwamun kiss). Meta gag: he coaches the woman playing the queen he served. 16 eps, tvN Apr–Jun 2012. Remade in China as *Love Weaves Through a Millennium* (2015).
