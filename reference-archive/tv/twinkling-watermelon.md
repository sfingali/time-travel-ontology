# Twinkling Watermelon (반짝이는 워터멜론, 2023)

## Short blurb
CODA guitarist Ha Eun-gyeol fights with his deaf father, sells a guitar at a mysterious shop, and wakes in 1995—joining band Watermelon Sugar with young Yi-chan while another traveler (Se-kyung's daughter Eun-yoo) also rewrites the past.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) Eun-gyeol, sole hearing child in a deaf family, secretly bands until father Yi-chan forbids a music career. Quitting, he tries to sell his guitar at La Vida Music and timeslips to 1995. Young Yi-chan can still hear; Eun-gyeol joins his band to stop the accident that will deafen him and helps reserved Yoon Chung-ah (his future mother) learn sign language despite an abusive stepmother. Popular cellist Choi Se-kyung suddenly changes—revealed as her future daughter On Eun-yoo, who slipped back hoping to erase her own birth by ending Se-kyung's first love. Romance pairs form (Eun-gyeol/Eun-yoo; Yi-chan/Chung-ah). Despite interventions Yi-chan is still deafened saving Eun-gyeol; back in the present Eun-gyeol finds the band stayed together, Chung-ah's father funded them, and Yi-chan still plays music. 16 episodes, tvN, Sep–Nov 2023.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Dual independent travelers from related families; shop as portal.
- Partial success: disability not erased, but family/music bonds improved.
