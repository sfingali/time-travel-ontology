# Doctor Who — landmark time-travel arcs

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Fixed-point / timey-wimey explainers; Blink angel maps; Day of the Doctor multi-Doctor charts.

## Short blurb
The TARDIS makes Who the longest-running TT franchise; selected arcs define closed loops (Blink), bootstrap identities, and timeline wars (Time War, Flux).

## Selected arcs (spoilers condensed)
- **Genesis of the Daleks / Time War lore:** Dalek origin interference ethics; later Last Great Time War backstory.
- **Blink (2007):** Weeping Angels; DVD messages create bootstrap—pure closed loop pedagogy.
- **The Pandorica Opens / Big Bang:** Nested resets of universe.
- **The Day of the Doctor (2013):** Multi-Doctor; Moment; Gallifrey frozen not burned—retcon Time War ending.
- **Heaven Sent / Hell Bent:** Confession dial loop castle—personal bootstrap grief engine.
- **Spyfall / Timeless Children:** Origin rewrite debates.
- **Flux (2021):** Universe-destroying temporal event; Division.

## Mechanism tags
TARDIS (relative dimensional travel), fixed points in time, parallel Englands (Rise of the Cybermen), River Song diary bootstrap.
