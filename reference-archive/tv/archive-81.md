# Archive 81 (2022)

## Short blurb
Archivist Dan Turner restores fire-damaged 1990s tapes of Melody Pendras’s documentary on the occult Visser apartment building—and is pulled into the tapes’ reality. Analog ritual, a missing corporation, and a demonic broadcast collapse time between 1994 and now.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Based on the podcast. Dan (Mamoudou Athie), grieving his family’s fire, takes a remote job for LMG restoring Melody’s (Dina Shihabi) Hi8 footage of the Visser’s cultish tenants and Baldung witchcraft conflicts. As he watches, the boundary thins: he converses across media; corporate handler Virgil is complicit; the Vos family / Otherworld entity seeks an opening via ritual architecture and recorded sacrifice.

Melody’s 1994 investigation and Dan’s present braid until Dan physically enters the tape-world. Season climax involves interrupting a broadcast ritual that would fully merge worlds, with survivors’ fates split across eras and a cliffhanger setup (series canceled after one season on Netflix). Time travel here is **media-as-portal**: playback is transportation; composition of the archive determines which past can be reached or altered.
