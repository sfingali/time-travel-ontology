# W: Two Worlds (더블유, 2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Real world ↔ webtoon *W* crossings; “To Be Continued” / “The End” status flags; tablet Enter/Delete physics.

## Short blurb
Cardiothoracic resident Oh Yeon-joo is yanked through her missing father’s monitor into the finale of his webtoon *W*, saves doomed hero Kang Cheol, and discovers crossings depend on his feelings—while the killer, the author, and the comic itself fight for narrative control.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode guide.) 16-episode MBC hit (Jul–Sep 2016), Lee Jong-suk & Han Hyo-joo. Writer Song Jae-jung (*Nine*). Author Oh Seong-moo vanishes mid-final chapter showing Cheol dying; Yeon-joo is pulled in, saves him, and returns when “To Be Continued” appears—her actions auto-upload as new pages. Seong-moo tries to kill his “monster” protagonist; the webtoon rejects truck-kill endings. Cheol learns he is a character, steps into reality, confronts his creator, and shoots him when refused a drawn killer’s face. Cheol’s suicide is uploaded as ending until “The End” reverts; assistant Soo-bong restarts the story. Marriage gets Yeon-joo out of in-comic prison; becoming a drawn character means she can bleed. Killer Han Sang-hoon (given Seong-moo’s face) hijacks the faceless author and tablet physics (Enter = instantiate, Delete = erase). Final arcs: Cheol’s death sentence, Seong-moo’s villain personality, security-footage redraw, two-year prison, reunion in a hospital away from the comic. MBC Daesang for Lee; Drama of the Year; remakes in Thai, Malay, Chinese; US adaptation developed.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Portal trigger: Cheol’s emotional state / narrative necessity.
- Metafictional causality: drawings become events; events become drawings.
- Parallel to *A Writer’s Odyssey* / *Inkheart* logic in K-drama form.
