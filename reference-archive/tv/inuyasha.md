# Inuyasha (犬夜叉, 2000–04 / Final Act 2009–10)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Bone-Eater’s Well transit charts, Shikon Jewel shard maps, and Naraku incarnation trees are staple fan diagrams.

## Short blurb
Modern Tokyo teen Kagome falls through her shrine’s **Bone-Eater’s Well** into the Sengoku period, meets half-demon Inuyasha, shatters the Shikon Jewel, and spends hundreds of episodes commuting between eras to gather shards before Naraku remakes the jewel.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) In 1496 Kikyo seals Inuyasha and is cremated with the Shikon Jewel. In present-day Tokyo, **Kagome Higurashi**—Kikyo’s reincarnation—is dragged into the well by a centipede demon (~1546 Sengoku). The Jewel emerges from her body; she frees Inuyasha; an arrow shatters the Jewel into shards. Party forms: fox-demon **Shippō**, monk **Miroku**, demon-slayer **Sango** (+ Kirara). Antagonist **Naraku** (spider half-demon who framed Inuyasha/Kikyo) manipulates shards and incarnations. Subplots: Sesshomaru/Tessaiga, Kōga, resurrected Kikyo. Climax: Naraku completes the Jewel, traps himself and Kagome inside; Jewel wants a selfish wish for eternal conflict; Kagome wishes it gone. Well seals; three years pass; Kagome graduates, reopens the well, marries Inuyasha, trains as priestess.

Anime: 167 eps (2000–04) + *Final Act* 26 eps (2009–10). Films are mostly original side stories (well still the franchise TT hinge). Spin-off *Yashahime* (2020–22) follows the next generation.

## Timeline/structure notes
- **Bidirectional portal**: Bone-Eater’s Well links Higurashi shrine ↔ Sengoku; seals/opens with plot.
- Kagome routinely returns to modern school life between arcs—commuter TT.
- Reincarnation (Kagome/Kikyo) layers identity across 500 years without machine rules.
