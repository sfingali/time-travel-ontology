# Splash Splash Love (퐁당퐁당 LOVE, 2015)

## Short blurb
CSAT-fleeing teen Danbi jumps into a playground puddle and lands in Joseon, is mistaken for a eunuch, becomes King Yi Do (Sejong)’s confidante, and modern high-school math makes her the realm’s top scientist—until homesickness sends her home.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) **Jang Dan-bi** (Kim Seul-gi) bolts from the college exam, hears a drum in a puddle, and timeslips to Joseon. Mistaken for a eunuch, she advises **King Yi Do** (Yoon Doo-joon / Sejong). Her school-level STEM outclasses the court; traditionalists target her. Queen Soheon (doppelgangers abound, including Dowager = Danbi’s mother) even endorses consort status, but Danbi chooses return. Beach goodbye; Yi vows to find her again. Web 10 shorts → MBC 2×60-min (Dec 2015). International Emmy-nominated TV movie/mini.
