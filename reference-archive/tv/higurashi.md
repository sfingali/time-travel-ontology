# Higurashi When They Cry (ひぐらしのなく頃に, 2006–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Fragment/"world" loops of June 1983; question→answer arc charts; Rika memory carryover graphs.

## Short blurb
Hinamizawa’s curse repeats summer 1983 across “fragments”; Rika Furude retains pieces of memory—and later, with Hanyuu’s help, seeks a fragment where friends survive Oyashiro-sama’s mystery and the village’s conspiracy.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia; deepen pass on loop arcs.) Sound-novel adaptations (2006 Deen anime; 2020 Passione reboot, etc.). **Question arcs** (*Onikakushi*, *Watanagashi*, *Tatarigoroshi*, *Himatsubushi*): each fragment replays festival season with different murders/outcomes; audience assembles clues. **Answer arcs** (*Meakashi*, *Tsumihoroboshi*, *Minagoroshi*, *Matsuribayashi*): reveal Takano Miyo / Tokyo conspiracy, Hinamizawa Syndrome, and Rika’s centuries of failed loops. Loops are horror-mystery fragments with partial memory carryover rather than sci-fi boxes; Rika’s growing awareness is the TT spine. Borderline for pure SF shelf but core of anime loop discourse beside *Madoka* / *Steins;Gate*.
