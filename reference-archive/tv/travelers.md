# Travelers (2016-2018)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Protocol lists and team TELL/mission trees on Travelers Wiki; finale Version One vs Two flowchart in recaps.

## Short blurb
From a post-collapse future, operatives’ consciousnesses overwrite 21st-century people at their recorded deaths (GPS TELL coordinates). Team 3468 serves the Director AI’s Grand Plan—until Faction war, Traveler 001, and Protocol Omega force a reset that erases their timeline for a better Version Two.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**Method:** No bodies move. A Traveler mind is shot into a host about to die, minimizing historical missing-persons noise. Smartphones made precise elevation/lat/long/time archives possible. Rule: no transfer safely to a time *before* one already performed (from the future’s frame). Teams of five keep host lives as cover. The **Director** (quantum AI) messages via children (adult hosts risk death if briefly possessed).

### Team 3468
- **Grant MacLaren** (FBI) — team lead, host marriage strain with Kat
- **Marcy** — medic; host disability / Faction trauma arcs
- **Trevor** — high-schooler host; historian
- **Carly** — young mother cover; tactical
- **Philip** — addict host; data historian who “sees” timeline probabilities

Missions avert cascading disasters (helping the Grand Plan). Series expands: other teams; **Faction** travelers rejecting the Director; **Archivists**; morality of murdering hosts’ remaining lifespans; MacLaren’s FBI partner and family entanglement.

### Traveler 001 / Vincent
Earliest traveler gone off-mission; builds power, hostages loved ones, forces reveal videos. Season 3 crisis: nuclear launch paths; 001 distributes consciousness into Ilsa supercomputer / internet to await and corrupt the future Director.

### Finale — Protocol Omega
Director abandons the timeline (Omega = no more missions; either optimal path or unsavable). Team uses 001’s transfer tech plus Director’s pre-loaded program to send **MacLaren** further back to warn/prevent Version One’s initiation—emailing the future so the program never starts as it did. Original team’s worldline is sacrificed; Director begins **Version Two**. A host-Marcy who was never overwritten meets David on a bus—grace note that some human threads persist.

## Paradox analysis

- **Consciousness ≠ body TT:** Social and ethical drama over physics spectacle; still full changewar stakes.
- **Grand Plan bootstraps:** Missions create the archives the future uses to aim TELLs.
- **Version sacrifice:** Finale is explicit timeline abandonment—rarer than “heal the one true line.”
- **001 as anti-Director:** Distributed mind waiting in the net = long-game grandfather attack on the AI.
