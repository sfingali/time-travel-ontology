# Lovely Runner (선재 업고 튀어, 2024)

## Short blurb
Fan Im Sol, devastated by idol Ryu Sun-jae's suicide, is thrown by a magical watch fifteen years to 2008 high school—and iterates timelines to save them both while time freezes if she blurts future spoilers.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis/episode summaries.) Paralyzed after a 2008/09 accident, Sol finds solace in Eclipse frontman Sun-jae. After his 2022/23 death she chases his old watch into a river and wakes in June 2008 as teens. She works to prevent his swimming injury path to despair and her own kidnapping/paralysis; failed reveals freeze time. Multiple returns to altered 2023 presents (walking again; Sun-jae alive but rendezvous missed; later stabbing; then a timeline where they never met and he is an actor). Watch enables further jumps (e.g., March 2009). 16 episodes, tvN, Apr–May 2024; web novel/webtoon *Tomorrow's Best*.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Watch-triggered slips with anti-spoiler freeze rule.
- Iterative personal rewrites; friend/family fates swap as collateral (Hyun-joo/Geum marriage, etc.).
