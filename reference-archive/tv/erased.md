# Erased / Boku dake ga Inai Machi (僕だけがいない街, 2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** 2006↔1988 Revival maps, victim-timeline trees, and Yashiro confrontation charts are widely circulated.

## Short blurb
Manga artist Satoru Fujinuma’s involuntary **Revival** snaps him minutes back from fatal accidents—until his mother’s murder hurls his adult mind into his 1988 childhood to stop a serial kidnapping before it claims classmates and, decades later, Sachiko.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers; 12-ep anime)
(Source: English Wikipedia episode guide.)

**2006 frame:** 29-year-old Satoru (struggling mangaka / pizza delivery) experiences Revival: a glowing blue butterfly precedes a snap back minutes to avert deaths (e.g., runaway truck). Mother **Sachiko** visits, references the 18-years-ago Hokkaido child murders blamed on kind delivery man **Jun Shiratori (“Yuki”)**. Sachiko deduces the true killer is still active; she is murdered in Satoru’s apartment. Accused, Satoru’s Revival overshoots—**adult mind in 11-year-old body, February 1988**.

**1988 first pass:** Satoru befriends lonely, abused **Kayo Hinazuki** (future victim), invites her to a shared birthday (Mar 2), shows her a frost “Christmas” tree. He thinks he saved her after Mar 1 passes—then she vanishes anyway; another girl disappears. Snap back to 2006 as fugitive; coworker **Airi** hides him; journalist Sawada; Airi trapped in arson. Satoru spots the man from the murder night.

**1988 second pass (willpower Revival to Feb 28):** With sharp friend **Kenya**, Satoru fakes events to give Yuki an alibi, “abducts” willing Kayo to an abandoned bus, finds the killer’s toolkit meant to frame Yuki. Sachiko helps confront abusive mother Akemi; Kayo goes to grandmother. Satoru watches remaining targets **Hiromi** and **Aya**. Homeroom teacher **Gaku Yashiro**—father figure—reveals himself as the killer, rolls a truck into a frozen lake with Satoru inside.

**2003 wake:** Satoru exits a 15-year coma; friends visit; Kayo married Hiromi, baby **Mirai**. Memories return. Yashiro, now politician “Nishizono,” tries rooftop murder/frame involving patient Kumi. Satoru’s bluff and friends’ catch enable arrest (attempted murder—old crimes timed out). **2010:** successful mangaka Satoru meets Airi again; Revival’s butterfly stirs.

Anime compresses/alters manga ending; Netflix 2017 live-action drama follows manga more closely; 2016 live-action film (Tatsuya Fujiwara) is separate (`films/erased-2016.md`).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Short Revivals (minutes) vs long Revival (18 years) = two scales of the same ability.
- Butterfly = omen of unpredictable side effects.
- Killer adapts across loops—adversarial looping, not static puzzle.

## Paradox analysis
- Branching personal timeline; future 2006 is mutable (Airi meeting, career).
- Statute of limitations twist: justice comes for the new crime.
- Coma bridge = cost of lake death without a clean third childhood pass in the anime ending.
