# A Time Called You (너의 시간 속으로, 2023)

## Short blurb
Netflix remake of Taiwan's *Someday or One Day*: grieving Jun-hee wakes in 1998 inside Kwon Min-ju's body and meets Nam Si-heon, who looks exactly like her dead boyfriend Yeon-jun.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis.) In 2023 Han Jun-hee still mourns boyfriend Koo Yeon-jun a year after his death. She somehow travels to 1998 and inhabits 18-year-old Kwon Min-ju. There she meets Si-heon (Ahn Hyo-seop, also Yeon-jun), whose resemblance deepens the mystery, alongside Si-heon's friend Jung In-gyu (Kang Hoon), who likes Min-ju. 12 episodes released on Netflix 8 Sep 2023. Direct remake of Huang Tien-jen's *想見你*; archive already holds the Taiwanese original.
