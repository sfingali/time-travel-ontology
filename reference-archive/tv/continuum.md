# Continuum (2012-2015)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Fan season timeline charts tracking Alec jumps, dual Kieras, Freelancer/Guardian custody, and 2077 endpoint variants.

## Short blurb
CPS officer Kiera Cameron is pulled from corporate-dystopia 2077 Vancouver into 2012 with escaping Liber8 terrorists. She allies with detective Carlos Fonnegra and teen genius **Alec Sadler**—future architect of the surveillance state—while Freelancers/Guardians fight a shadow time war.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**2077:** North American Union / Corporate Congress police state. Liber8, led by Edouard Kagame, faces execution; a time device flings them—and Kiera—to **2012**. Liber8 decides to wage war early against the corporations that will replace democracy. Kiera hides her origin, joins VPD via Carlos, and phones future tech through her CMR implants—answered by young Alec, whose later SadTech designs those implants.

### Escalating factions
- **Liber8** internal splits (Kagame’s martyrdom plans; Travis/Sonya violence vs reformists).
- **Alec** as pivot: protect him to preserve Kiera’s home timeline vs stop him becoming oligarch.
- **Freelancers / Guardians of the Continuum:** millennia-spanning time cults policing “glitches,” imprisoning travelers in pods, forging dead-infant IDs. Escher (Alec’s future-linked father figure) murks the line between Guardian and freelancing power broker.
- **Kellog:** fellow traveler playing markets and politics for personal empire.
- Multiple time jumps produce **duplicate Alecs** and eventually **duplicate Kieras**; timelines diverge when Alec uses the device to save Emily (S2/S3), aborting one future.

### Series finale (“Final Hour”)
Kiera works to stop a darker path involving Kellog and invading future soldiers through a portal. Alec sabotages keys; invasion collapses. Kiera reaches a **better 2077**—but a variant Kiera already exists there as Sam’s mother. She cannot reclaim her exact family without erasing that life; bittersweet acceptance that she changed the future she can never fully rejoin.

## Paradox analysis

- **Branching changewar:** Protecting vs preventing Alec is the central grandfather-adjacent dilemma—her son’s world depends on the dystopia she fights.
- **Multiverse-lite:** Duplicate travelers coexist after jumps; killing a duplicate doesn’t auto-erase the older self (Den of Geek finale notes).
- **Bootstrap tech:** Alec reverse-engineers future CMR/SadTech from Kiera’s gear—he invents what he already invented.
- **Institutional TT:** Freelancers as TVA-cousins years before *Loki*.
