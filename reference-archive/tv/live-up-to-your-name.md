# Live Up to Your Name / Dr. Heo (명불허전, 2017)

## Short blurb
Joseon acupuncturist Heo Im, shot with arrows and drowning after a royal-treatment disaster, awakens in modern Seoul and partners with cardiothoracic fellow Choi Yeon-kyung—while namesakes and hospital politics echo 400-year-old intrigues.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis.) **Heo Im** (Kim Nam-gil) treats the poor by day and nobles by night; summoned for the king’s migraine, anxiety leads to treason charges. Cornered at a river, arrowed, he “dies”—and wakes in present Seoul. He meets **Choi Yeon-kyung** (Kim Ah-joong), a driven surgeon at Shinhae Hospital. Bidirectional time-slip medical/romance comedy: Heo’s acupuncture miracles clash with evidence-based surgery; Yeon-kyung’s career and family oriental-clinic ties (grandfather Choi Chun-sool) braid the eras. Supporting doubles map Joseon figures onto modern hospital/power players (e.g., director Shin Myung-hoon ↔ corrupt war minister; Yoo Jae-ha ↔ Joseon noble feud). Legendary physician **Heo Jun** appears in period threads. 16 eps, Aug–Oct 2017; finale Nielsen paid-platform ~6.9% (cable hit). Writer Kim Eun-hee (*Signal*, *Kingdom*).
