# Day Break (2006)

## Short blurb
Detective Brett Hopper is framed for murder and relives the same day trying to clear his name.

## Detailed plot summary
ABC series cancelled early then completed on cable/streaming arcs in public memory. Conspiracy unfolds across loops; Hopper retains memory.
