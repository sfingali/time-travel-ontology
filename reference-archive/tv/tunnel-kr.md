# Tunnel (터널, 2017)

## Short blurb
1986 detective Park Gwang-ho chases a Hwaseong-inspired serial killer into a tunnel and emerges in 2016, partnering with elite detective Kim Seon-jae and criminal psychologist Shin Jae-yi—who is his own daughter—to stop killings that resume with the same M.O.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis/cast.) In 1986, passionate Hwayang detective Park Gwang-ho leads a women's serial-homicide case; pursuing the culprit through a tunnel, he timeslips thirty years to 2016. There he partners with Kim Seon-jae while working with Professor Shin Jae-yi (his and Shin Yeon-sook's daughter, raised after his disappearance). The killer's pattern reappears; the duo plus Jae-yi chase cold-case truth across eras. Supporting timeline cast includes Jeon Sung-sik (young detective in 1986, team leader in 2016). Culprit identity and parental revelations drive the back half (per series cast notes: forensic doctor Mok Jin-woo as the 1986 killer). 16 episodes, OCN, March–May 2017; inspired by Hwaseong murders; Thai and Indonesian remakes followed.

## Timeline/structure notes
- One-way (initially) tunnel portal 1986↔2016; Signal-adjacent walkie/era-partner structure without the radio.
- Father–daughter recognition across the slip is the emotional spine.
