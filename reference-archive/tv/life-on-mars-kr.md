# Life on Mars (KR) (라이프 온 마스, 2018)

## Short blurb
Modern profiler Han Tae-joo wakes in 1988 as a small-city detective after a near-death accident while chasing a serial killer—Korean remake of the UK *Life on Mars*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Tae-joo trusts data over people; after an accident he is appointed to a 1988 precinct and must solve murders with outdated tools and quirky partners (Park Sung-woong, Go Ah-sung et al.) to return to 2018. 16 episodes on OCN.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Ambiguity of coma vs true slip inherited from UK original (also archived as UK *Life on Mars*).
