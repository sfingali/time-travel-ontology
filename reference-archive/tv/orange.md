# Orange (オレンジ, 2016)

## Short blurb
Naho Takamiya receives letters from her future self warning how to prevent transfer student Kakeru Naruse's suicide—and learns her friends got matching letters from a grieving future where he is already dead.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) In Matsumoto, second-year Naho receives letters from herself ten years ahead asking her to avoid regrets tied to new classmate Kakeru. Events match the letters; she learns future-Kakeru died by suicide after his mother's suicide (which he blames on ditching her hospital trip for friends). Hiroto Suwa—Naho's future husband in the letter-world—also received letters telling him to support Naho and Kakeru even if it costs his own future with her. Takako, Hagita, and Azusa reveal matching letters. Chemistry class references many-worlds; the group concludes they may not overwrite their own future but can create a parallel world where Kakeru lives. They stop him from walking into a truck on the predicted night; letters and a time capsule close the arc. 13-episode anime (2016); theatrical *Orange: Future* retells from Suwa's view.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Information-only time travel (letters), not bodily leaps.
- Explicit many-worlds / parallel-rescue framing in late episodes.
- Live-action film (2015) is a separate adaptation of the same manga.
