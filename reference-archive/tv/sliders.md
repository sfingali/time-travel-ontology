# Sliders (1995-2000)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
Quinn Mallory’s timer opens vortices between parallel Earths; the group slides seeking home.

## Detailed plot summary
Pilot: Quinn invents sliding; he, professor Arturo, singer Rembrandt, and later Wade (and replacements) hop worlds where history branched (Soviet America, gender-reversed norms, dinosaur Earths, etc.). Early seasons emphasize alternate-history anthropology; later seasons add Kromagg empire war and more serialized mythology. Timer countdown stranding is the weekly engine. Foundational US multiverse TV.
