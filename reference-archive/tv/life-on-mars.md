# Life on Mars (UK) (2006–2007)

## Short blurb
After a 2006 car accident, DCI Sam Tyler wakes as a DI in 1973 Manchester under loudmouthed Gene Hunt. Is he in a coma, mad, dead, or truly time-traveling? Radio voices from 2006 and period policing collide for two series before a rooftop choice.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Sam (John Simm), modern procedure-minded copper, is hit by a car while investigating a killer; he awakens in 1973 with period clothes, badge, and assignment to CID under DCI Gene Hunt (Philip Glenister)—sexist, violent, oddly effective. Sam hears hospital monitors and 2006 voices through radios; Test Card Girl hauntings intensify. He adopts hybrid policing, bonds with Annie Cartwright, clashes with Hunt, Chris, and Ray, and chases both 1973 cases and clues to “wake up.”

Ontology stays deliberately plural: coma dream, neurological misfire, actual timeslip, purgatory. Finale path (spoiler): Sam appears to wake in 2006, finds modern policing empty without Hunt’s tribe, and suicides-by-jump—returning to 1973 as chosen home. Sequels (*Ashes to Ashes*) reframe the world as a limbo for dead officers with Hunt as psychopomp—retcon that deepens rather than voids *Mars*’s emotional ending.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Accident → 1973 drop-in.**
2. **Culture clash procedural** of the week.
3. **Radio/Test Card** messages as “present” tethers.
4. **Identity crisis peaks** (am I inventing them?).
5. **Wake / reject / return** finale choice.

US remake (2008) ends with Mars-mission VR—different ontology entirely.

## Paradox analysis

- **Ambiguity as design:** Show refuses single mechanism until sequel mythology.
- **No tech travel:** Slip is trauma-triggered, not machine.
- **Affective closed loop:** Choosing 1973 assigns meaning regardless of physics.
- **Sequel retcon:** *Ashes*’s limbo reading makes Sam dead-all-along—purgatory TT adjacent.

## Endings / production notes

BBC One; BAFTA winner; cultural landmark for 1970s aesthetics + Bowie title track. Glenister’s Hunt became iconic. Direct sequel *Ashes to Ashes* (2008–10).
