# The Flash (CW) (2014–2023)

## Short blurb
Barry Allen becomes the Flash and repeatedly time-travels via the Speed Force—saving mothers, creating Flashpoint, fighting Reverse-Flash, time wraiths, and Savitar—across nine CW seasons of reset buttons and Arrowverse crossovers.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

S1: Particle accelerator accident; Barry vs Eobard Thawne / Reverse-Flash, who killed Nora Allen. Season end allows a run back that Thawne manipulates. Later seasons canonize **Flashpoint** (Barry saves Nora; timeline warps; he partially restores a damaged “Point of Divergence” present). Time remnants, aberrant clones, and Savitar (future Barry remnant) literalize paradox cost.

Myth devices: Speed Force as afterlife/energy plane; Negative Speed Force; Still Force, etc., in late seasons. Crisis on Infinite Earths remaps the multiverse; Barry’s final seasons wrestle with hanging up the boots vs endless crisis. Tone: earnest found family at STAR Labs amid increasingly baroque temporal jargon.

## Paradox analysis

- **Flashpoint template:** Wish-save → worse world → imperfect restore (comics + film echo).
- **Time remnants:** Duplicates from aborted timelines as people—ethical horror.
- **Soft fixed points:** Newspaper headlines from the future as mutable prophecy props.
- **Speed Force glue:** Hand-waves energy accounting for travel.

## Endings / production notes

The CW flagship; nine seasons. Crossovers with Arrow, Legends, Supergirl. Grant Gustin lead throughout.

## Season-by-season temporal map (public synopsis level)

**S1:** Accelerator origin; Wells/Thawne long con; Nora’s murder as motive; first deliberate past run teased.
**S2:** Zoom/Earth-2; timeline already scarred; speedster arms race.
**S3:** Flashpoint—Barry saves Nora; world warps (Diggle’s child swap etc.); incomplete restore leaves “post-Flashpoint” residue; Savitar as time-remnant future Barry.
**S4–5:** Thinker; Elseworlds; Crisis buildup; Nora West-Allen from future as bootstrap daughter complications.
**S6–9:** Crisis reboot of multiverse; Mirror; Speed Force wars; Armageddon; final legacy/retirement vs endless crisis.

Arrowverse crossovers (*Legends of Tomorrow*, *Supergirl*, *Batwoman*) treat Barry’s timeline edits as shared weather. Newspaper from the future (S1) remains the franchise’s soft prophecy prop—headlines rewrite when Barry changes fate.

Teaching use: Flashpoint as TV’s longest engagement with “wish-save → worse world → imperfect restore.” Time remnants literalize aborted timelines as people—ethical horror under soap lighting. Compare *Loans* of *Legends*’ fixed-point language and *Endgame*’s branch rules.

## Paradox devices glossary (series usage)

- **Speed Force / Negative Speed Force:** Energy planes enabling travel and housing speedster afterlives.
- **Flashpoint:** Wish-save of Nora → cascading personal/world changes; incomplete restore.
- **Time remnant:** Duplicate created by leaving a version of oneself in an aborted timeline—Savitar’s origin horror.
- **Fixed points (soft):** Events treated as resistant but not absolute (newspaper headlines as mutable prophecy).
- **Crisis reboot:** Multiverse remap that soft-resets prior continuity weather.

Nine-season CW run with Grant Gustin throughout; Berlanti/Kreisberg/Johns development. Teaching archive note: longest mainstream TV engagement with Flashpoint grammar.
