# Tidsrejsen (2014–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** 24-episode advent calendar with 1984↔2014↔2044 crossings; fan charts of Birk-erasure / Ragnar-sect branches.

## Short blurb
Thirteen-year-old Sofie finishes grandfather’s “gyro” time machine, meets future boy Dixie fleeing Tempus agents, and rewrites 1984 so her divorced parents stay together—erasing brother Birk and nearly handing the town to a prophecy-faking teacher.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia episode guide.) DR Christmas calendar (24 eps, Dec 2014), *Back to the Future* × rom-com. Sofie’s gyro + Dixie’s future battery freeze time and jump to 1984 Dragør. Dixie is from 2044, where Sofie invents the machines Tempus police outlaw; Agent Sort is future Sofie. Butterfly: fixing parents’ meet-cute deletes Birk (replaced by a dog), then a stolen local-history book lets teacher Ragnar fake prophecies and run a Christian sect. Sofie/Dixie iterate 1984 fixes, destroy the original gyro to prevent abusive futures, and still end together with a daughter in 2044. S2 (2024): adult Sofie + student Romeo across medieval/90s/future. S3 *Arven efter Drago* (2025). ~1.02M avg viewers; Robert Award best short-format series.
