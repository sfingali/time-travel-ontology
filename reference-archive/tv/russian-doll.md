# Russian Doll (2019-2022)

## Short blurb
Nadia dies on her 36th birthday and resets to the party—until she and Alan share a death loop; S2 boards a multiverse train through mother/lineage timelines.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
**S1:** Kraken hardware store resets; Nadia and Alan must help each other exit by facing trauma/guilt. **S2:** Subway multiverse; Nadia as her mother; time/identity nested dolls metaphor made literal. Tonally witty, formally ambitious Netflix comedy-drama.

## Analysis
- S1 = Groundhog Day + suicide-ideation ethics.
- S2 = parallel_worlds / lineage time.
- Lyonne as auteur performer.
