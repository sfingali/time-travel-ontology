# Russian Doll (2019–2022)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Party-death loop maps (S1) and time-subway diagrams (S2) are the dual-season explainer set.

## Short blurb

Nadia Vulvokov dies at her 36th birthday party and resets to the bathroom mirror — until she and fellow looper Alan fix the pattern; Season 2 sends Nadia through decades via a surreal subway to confront family history.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

## Premise Premise Russian Doll follows a woman named Nadia who is caught in a time loop at her 36th birthday party one night in New York City. She dies repeatedly, always restarting at the same moment at the party, as she tries to figure out what is happening to her. She meets a man who is experiencing the same thing in a different event. In season 2, Nadia is 10 days away from celebrating her 40th birthday when the 6 train sends her back in time to 1982.

She soon discovers she is trapped inside the body of her mother, Nora, who is pregnant with her. Nadia decides to pursue the gold Krugerrands her mother lost that same year, in order to change the course of her family's history.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

**Season 1 (2019):** Nadia’s party loop; escalating deaths; meets Alan who shares a linked reset pattern. Investigation into their connection, guilt, and care for others; breaking the loop requires genuine change and mutual recognition — Groundhog grammar with NYC indie-comedy tone. Exit restores linear time with lessons kept.

**Season 2 (2022):** Different mechanism — Nadia boards a mysterious train/subway that deposits her in other decades (1980s etc.), inhabiting family-adjacent timelines; deals with mother/grandmother trauma, capitalism/kratom side plots, and returning objects/people across eras. Less pure death-loop; more portal-history therapy.

**Shared DNA:** Mortality, addiction/recovery metaphors, immigrant family pain, multiverse-adjacent NYC weirdness.
