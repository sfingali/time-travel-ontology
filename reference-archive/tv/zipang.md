# Zipang (ジパング, 2004–2005)

## Short blurb
JMSDF Aegis helicopter destroyer *Mirai* slips from a 21st-century exercise to the eve of Midway (June 1942); its crew debates whether to change history while militarist Kusaka pursues an undefeated Japan he names "Zipang."

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot — anime covers early manga arcs.) En route to exercises off Ecuador, *Mirai* hits an anomaly and faces the battleship *Yamato* and Nagato-class ships: it is 4 June 1942. Crew resolve to return home without altering history, but XO Yosuke Kadomatsu rescues IJN Lt. Cdr. Takumi Kusaka, who would have died—already a divergence. Kusaka studies *Mirai*'s records, rejects both Imperial militarism and postwar defeat, and schemes for a new nation Zipang (oil in Manchuria, early peace on his terms). *Mirai* is drawn into Guadalcanal, intercepts *Yamato* shells with Sea Sparrows, sinks USS *Wasp* in self-defense, and docks at wartime Yokosuka. Kadomatsu learns his own father died as a child in this altered line (car accident), erasing his future birth. Anime ends on a cliffhanger as Kadomatsu leaves for China to stop Kusaka; manga continues through nuclear brinkmanship, *Mirai*'s sinking, and an alternate postwar where only Kadomatsu can persist because no "original" self was born.
