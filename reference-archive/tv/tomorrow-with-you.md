# Tomorrow With You (내일 그대와, 2017)

## Short blurb
Real-estate CEO Yoo So-joon can ride a subway into other times; after glimpsing his own future death he hastily marries photographer Song Ma-rin—and only afterward learns to love her for real.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia synopsis.) tvN fantasy romance, 16 episodes (Feb–Mar 2017), starring Lee Je-hoon and Shin Min-a. So-joon, CEO of Myreits, travels through time via a subway line taught/shared by fellow traveler Doo-sik (Jo Han-chul). Foreseeing his future self die, he engineers a marriage to Ma-rin (ex-child star “Bap-soon,” now an amateur photographer) as insurance against that fate. As they share a life, he shifts from instrumental self-preservation to selfless love. Supporting arcs involve Myreits colleagues, Ma-rin’s mother and friends, and Happiness Construction / childhood-friend Se-young. Public English-language plot detail beyond the premise is thin; episode-level temporal rules are not fully documented on EN Wikipedia.
