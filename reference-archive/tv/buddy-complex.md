# Buddy Complex (バディ・コンプレックス, 2014)

## Short blurb
High-schooler Aoba Watase is attacked by a mecha, saved by classmate Hina, and flung to 2088’s Free Pact Alliance vs Zogilia war—where he must Coupling-pilot with Dio and save a memory-wiped Hina from becoming the enemy who once saved him.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 13-ep Sunrise mecha (Jan–Mar 2014) + 2-part *Final Chapter* (Sep 2014). Hina from the future protects Aoba in 2014 then sends him forward; in 2088 she is Zogilia pilot Hina Ryazan with no memory of him. Coupling System links Luxon/Bradyon pilots’ minds. Aoba refuses to kill her, island stranding softens her, childhood-obsessive Bizon interferes. Revelation that she *is* the same Hina drives the rescue. Final Chapter closes the loop of her 2014 mission. Soft closed loop: future Hina creates the past rescue that creates the future.

## Timeline/structure notes
- ~74-year jump 2014→2088.
- Coupler mind-link as interpersonal time-bond metaphor.
- Enemy-ally identity = classic TT recognition delay.
