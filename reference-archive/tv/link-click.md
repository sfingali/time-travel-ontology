# Link Click (时光代理人, 2021–)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Photo-entry missions with 12-hour caps, partner clairvoyance, and accumulating timeline consequences across seasons — fan timelines common.

## Short blurb
Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang run Time Photo Studio: Xiaoshi enters photos to possess the photographer for twelve hours; Lu Guang reads the photo’s twelve-hour window and coaches him telepathically—supposedly without changing the past.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Clients hire them to resolve regrets. Cheng Xiaoshi travels into a supplied photo, assuming the photographer’s identity (or appearing as himself for surveillance stills) and absorbing memories/emotions. Lu Guang reconstructs events in the photo’s vicinity for twelve hours after capture and communicates with Xiaoshi during the dive. Rules: one trip, twelve hours, leave the past unchanged—yet Xiaoshi’s actions can affect the timeline. Recurring cases include police captain Xiao Li’s murder investigation involving influencer Liu Min, overworked secretary Emma/Wu Lihua, and other clients. Seasons: S1 (2021, Bilibili/Funimation), S2 (2023), Bridon Arc prequel (2024–25), S3 from 2026. Live-action and musical adaptations announced/aired per Wikipedia.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Pair of complementary powers (dive vs. photo-clairvoyance); soft “don’t change past” rule under narrative pressure.
- Case-of-the-week → serialized conspiracy structure.
