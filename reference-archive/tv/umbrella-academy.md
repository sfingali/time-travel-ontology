# The Umbrella Academy (2019-2024)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Commission org charts, season apocalypse maps, and S4 “145,412 resets” diner variants explained across Radio Times / Mashable / CinemaHolic.

## Short blurb
Forty-three children born to random women the same day; seven adopted by Reginald Hargreeves as the Umbrella Academy. Number Five’s time jumps and Temps Commission briefcases hurl the siblings through apocalypses—until they learn *they* are the anomaly and erase themselves to restore a clean timeline.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

### Season 1 — 2019 apocalypse
Adult siblings reunite after Reginald’s death. Five returns from a future wasteland (and Commission assassin years) warning of imminent end. Viktor’s repressed power + Commission interference → Moon knocked into Earth. Five time-jumps them out.

### Season 2 — 1960s Dallas
Scattered across 1960s; JFK week; Commission civil war (Handler vs Herb). They avert (their) apocalypse and briefcase home—arriving in a rewritten present.

### Season 3 — Sparrow Academy / Kugelblitz
Home timeline replaced by **Sparrow Academy** (different adopted set). Umbrellas are paradoxes (mothers died before births). Paradox → **Kugelblitz** eating the universe. Five & Lila find Commission ruined; its Founder is an elderly **alternate Five** in a life-support chamber. Reset via Hotel Oblivion / new universe deal with Reginald strips powers.

### Season 4 — marigold, Cleanse, uncreation
Years later, marigold particles restore powers; Jennifer/durango threat. Five and Lila lost seven years in a multiverse subway; fall in love. In Max’s Deli, Five meets countless variant Fives: every world with Umbrellas ends in apocalypse (~145,412 tries). The Commission was one Five’s failed project to prune broken timelines back to an original. **Conclusion:** the siblings’ existence shattered the perfect timeline. Cure: let marigold and durango cancel—**erasing the Umbrellas (and Lila’s relevant tie) from history**. World continues without them; post-credits shows strangers living peaceful lives once occupied by Commission/family motifs.

## Paradox analysis

- **Family as grandfather paradox:** Their births are the event that breaks time; saving the world requires never having been.
- **Commission bootstrap:** Founded by a Five to fix problems Fives keep causing—institutional loop.
- **Not Novikov:** History *does* rewrite (Sparrow world); final move deletes the agents of rewrite.
- **Briefcases vs innate jumps:** Bureaucracy vs personal power—Five’s arc from runaway to Founder to self-erasure.
