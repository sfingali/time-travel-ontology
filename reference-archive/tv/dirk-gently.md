# Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency (2016–2017)

## Short blurb
Holistic detective Dirk Gently drags reluctant Todd into cases where “everything is connected”—including body-swapping, soul-splitting, time travel cults, and emotional animals. Two seasons of chaotic causality before cancellation.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

BBC America/Netflix adaptation (loose on Adams). S1: Dirk (Samuel Barnett) and Todd (Elijah Wood) unravel the Wendimoor / Rowdy 3 / Project Blackwing conspiracy tying murders to emotional psychic vampires and a fantasy realm next door. Time travel appears as looped causality and agency black projects rather than DeLorean tourism.

S2: Time-traveling “fun” escalates—characters bounce across eras; Amanda’s powers; Francis / Blackwing fallout; a more explicit temporal war over connected fates. The holistic thesis: apparent coincidence is unseen structure; fighting it only ties the knot tighter (Adams’s fundamental joke).

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **S1 murder mystery** → Blackwing reveal.
2. **Fantasy-adjacent Wendimoor** as parallel pressure.
3. **S2 temporal reshuffle** — eras as case rooms.
4. **Cancellation mid-myth.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Holistic determinism:** Soft predestination via connection, not Novikov lectures.
- **Blackwing science:** Government attempts to weaponize anomaly people—including temporal sensitivities.
- **Comedy first:** Paradoxes serve jokes and character growth.
- **Adams DNA:** Infinite improbability energy without Hitchhiker branding.

## Endings / production notes

Canceled after two seasons. Distinct from 2010–12 UK Dirk Gently (Stephen Mangan). Landis exited; showrunners adjusted S2 tone.
