# Signal (시그널, 2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Present-day records rewrite after each past intervention; case-by-case ripple charts common in fan explainers.

## Short blurb
Profiler Park Hae-young (2015) finds a walkie-talkie that links to Detective Lee Jae-han (1989–2000); solving cold cases across eras changes—and endangers—the present.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Hae-young connects with Jae-han while revisiting a classmate's kidnapping; with Detective Cha Soo-hyun (who has searched for missing mentor Jae-han for 15 years) they form a cold-case squad. Cases inspired by real Korean crimes (incl. Hwaseong-adjacent serial murders) are cracked via cross-time tips, but each save creates new victims or altered records only Hae-young fully remembers. Investigation of the Inju high-school rape case (framing Hae-young's brother Sun-woo) exposes corrupt superintendent Kim Bum-joo. Jae-han is nearly murdered in 2000 by Chi-soo; Soo-hyun's warning from 2015 lets him survive with backup. Reality resets: Hae-young briefly dies then wakes in a world where Sun-woo was exonerated and the squad never formed; Jae-han is missing again. Finale: Hae-young and Soo-hyun (who retains memory) drive toward a nursing home where an active walkie suggests Jae-han lives. Remakes: Japan (2018), China, Thai, Indian *Gyaarah Gyaarah*; S2 announced 2024.
