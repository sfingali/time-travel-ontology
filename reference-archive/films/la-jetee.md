# La Jetée (1962)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
Photo-roman short: a prisoner of a post-apocalyptic underground is sent through time via memory, obsessed with a pier image that is his own death.

## Detailed plot summary
Scientists use the man's fixation on a woman's face at Orly to anchor trips to past and future. He meets the woman repeatedly; the future offers escape; he returns to the pier and is killed — the childhood memory was his death. Direct ancestor of 12 Monkeys.
