# Frequently Asked Questions About Time Travel (2009)

## Short blurb
Trivia-loving misfits Ray, Toby, and Pete meet Cassie—a woman from the future—in a British pub and tumble through editors who prune timelines, doomed selves, and toilet portals in a meta comedy about TT tropes.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Ray obsesses over genre rules; a night at the pub introduces Cassie (Faris), who needs help with future complications. The trio encounters “editors” who delete failed histories, versions of themselves from worse branches, and the discovery that pub toilets can act as temporal junctions. Jokes systematically name-check bootstrap paradoxes, grandfather issues, and fandom pedantry while the plot spirals. Cult UK/indie comedy: light on hard consistency, heavy on affectionate satire of the genre the archive catalogues.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Pub meet** — Cassie rupture.
2. **Rule-lawyering** as character comedy.
3. **Editor threat** — timeline pruning personified.
4. **Overlapping selves / toilet portals.**
5. **Improvised save** leaning on Ray’s trivia.

## Paradox analysis

- **Meta-TT:** Tropes become diegetic hazards.
- **Editors as antagonists:** Institutional Novikov enforcers played for laughs.
- **Bootstrap gags:** Objects/knowledge without origin as running bits.
- **Pub as nexus:** Mundane space = chronotope (kin to *Hot Tub*).

## Endings / cuts / production notes

UK indie; modest theatrical, durable cult DVD life. Carrivick’s feature. Pair with *Bill & Ted* and *Hot Tub* as comedy pedagogy.
