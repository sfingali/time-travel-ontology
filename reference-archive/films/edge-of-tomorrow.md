# Edge of Tomorrow (2014)

## Short blurb
PR officer Cage dies on a Mimic-invasion beach and wakes at base dawn repeatedly—bonding with Rita “Full Metal Bitch” Vrataski to kill the Omega.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Europe occupied by Mimics. Major Cage (Cruise) is forced into the doomed Operation Downfall at Trafalgar/French beach. Killed after contact with an Alpha’s blood, he resets to Heathrow base morning with memories intact. Rita previously had the power (lost after transfusion). Together they iterate tactics, find the Omega (central reset control) under a dam, then in Paris after visions. Cage loses looping after a transfusion, stages a final assault, dies in Omega blood, and wakes to a pre-invasion morning where Mimics are already collapsing—soft reset victory. Nickname marketing: Live Die Repeat.

## Temporal structure
- Death-triggered reset to a fixed morning (Groundhog Day + military campaign).
- Alphas linked to Omega; human blood hijack of alien system.
- Within-loop branching of tactics; final post-Omega wake may be a deeper reset.

## Paradox analysis
- Learning-across-death as power fantasy of iteration.
- Ending debated: new timeline vs continued loop with destroyed Omega—public explainers differ slightly; film presents victorious altered morning.
- Adaptation softens novel’s darker tone.
