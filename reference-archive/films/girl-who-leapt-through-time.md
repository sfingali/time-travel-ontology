# The Girl Who Leapt Through Time (2006) (2006)

## Short blurb
Schoolgirl Makoto gains the ability to leap back and casually fixes mishaps until she depletes leaps needed for a greater purpose.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia, 2006 film.) Makoto Konno gains time-leaping after contacting a walnut-shaped device and surviving a train near-miss. She frivolously fixes grades, karaoke, and avoids Chiaki Mamiya's confession. Arm tattoos count remaining leaps. After using her last leap to dodge Chiaki's call about leaping, friends borrow her faulty bike toward the same crossing. Chiaki freezes time: he is from the future, the device is his, he spent his last leap to save them and must leave. His leap restores one leap to Makoto; she returns to just after gaining powers so Chiaki keeps a leap, shows him the spent device, and vows to preserve the painting he came to see. Loose sequel to Tsutsui's novel with new characters.
