# Steins;Gate: The Movie − Load Region of Déjà Vu (2013)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** R-worldline / Steins Gate fluctuation explainers common in franchise charts.

## Short blurb
One year after reaching Steins Gate, Okabe's overtaxed Reading Steiner makes him flicker out of existence; only Kurisu retains faint memories and must time-leap—and travel to 2005—to implant the Hououin Kyouma persona that anchors him.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) August 2011: lab members reunite when Kurisu visits Japan. Okabe suffers visions of other worldlines, then vanishes; nobody but Kurisu faintly remembers him. A mysterious visitor (future Suzuha) urges her to recall a phone, a microwave, and SERN. Kurisu has Daru hack SERN and rebuilds a Time Leap machine, leaping to 3 August to watch Okabe phase in and out. Suzuha explains Okabe fluctuates between Steins Gate and the "R" worldline (only other line where neither Mayuri nor Kurisu die) because accumulated worldline memories overload Reading Steiner. Okabe refuses further machines, kisses Kurisu, and asks to be forgotten. After he vanishes again, Suzuha convinces Kurisu to use a time machine: they go to 30 June 2005. First attempt causes young Okabe's car-accident death; second succeeds—Kurisu implants the mad-scientist Hououin Kyouma identity and gives him his first kiss, the powerful distinguishing memory that keeps him on Steins Gate. Present Okabe returns; film ends with him asking if she will return the kiss.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Sequel epilogue to the TV series True End; introduces R worldline.
- Bootstrap: Kurisu originates Okabe's chuunibyou persona in 2005.
