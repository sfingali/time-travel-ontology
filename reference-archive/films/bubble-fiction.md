# Bubble Fiction: Boom or Bust (バブルへGO!!, 2007)

## Short blurb
Finance Ministry drafts reluctant daughter Mayumi into a drum-type washing-machine time machine to 1990 Tokyo—mission: stop collateralized-loan law that helped burst Japan’s bubble—after inventor mom Mariko vanishes mid-jump.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Yasuo Baba comedy; Ryōko Hirosue, Hiroko Yakushimaru, Hiroshi Abe. Hitachi-linked engineer Mariko’s drum washer is a time machine; officials testing it retrieve only faded socks until Mayumi, estranged from Mariko, is coerced into the 1990 jump. Culture-clash gags (phones, manners, boom-era excess) frame an economic-history butterfly: prevent legislation tied to the asset-price collapse that shaped 2007 Japan. Contact lost / mother-daughter repair as emotional spine. Rare JP TT film whose MacGuffin is macroeconomic policy.
