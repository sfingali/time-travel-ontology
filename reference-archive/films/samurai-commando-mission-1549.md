# Samurai Commando: Mission 1549 (戦国自衛隊1549, 2005)

## Short blurb
A JGSDF EMP-shield test dumps a modern unit into 1549; years later black holes eat the present because their colonel has murdered Oda Nobunaga and remade Sengoku Japan—so a recovery team must undo him before Mount Fuji’s kit-bashed nuke erupts.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 10 March 2003: JGSDF EMP/solar-flare shield test fails; soldiers appear under samurai attack in **1549**. After 74 hours a reverse effect dumps wounded samurai **Iinuma Shichibei** into the 21st century. Two years later black holes threaten Japan. Maj. **Mori** and Lt. **Rei Kanzaki** recruit weary ex-Lt. **Yusuke Kashima** to recover stranded Col. **Tsuyoshi Matoba**.

The recovery team arrives, rescues youth **Tosuke** (actually young **Toyotomi Hideyoshi**), and is captured. At Mount Anmo’s anachronistic castle—“Nobunaga” is Matoba, who killed the real Nobunaga and plans to erupt Fuji with a shield-battery nuclear device to become shōgun. Mori dies covering an escape; Kanzaki is recaptured. With ~21 hours left, survivors ally with Shichibei and Saitō Dōsan, draw Matoba’s army out with a Kyoto feint, sabotage the castle, and Kashima kills Matoba. The armed bomb is flown to the return point; electromagnetic timeshift stops the timer and restores the present.
