# Freejack (1992)

## Short blurb
Future elites snatch people from the past milliseconds before death to inhabit their bodies—racing driver Alex is “freejacked.”

## Detailed plot summary
Cyberpunk-ish 2009/future: Alex Furlong (Estevez) is taken before a crash; becomes valuable meat for a dying rich man. Chase thriller with Mick Jagger’s bounty hunter. Loose Takeover adaptation vibes.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Time travel as resource extraction.
