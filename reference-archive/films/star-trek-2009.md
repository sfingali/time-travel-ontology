# Star Trek (2009) (2009)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
Nero’s Narada falls from the future, creating the Kelvin Timeline alternate Trek continuity.

## Detailed plot summary
Romulan Nero, displaced from Prime future after Hobus supernova, attacks Kelvin and later Vulcan. Young Kirk/Spock form in a branched timeline. Explicitly justifies reboot continuity via time-travel branching—rare franchise honesty about soft reboot mechanics.
