# Tomorrow I'll Wake Up and Scald Myself with Tea (Zítra vstanu a opařím se čajem, 1977)

## Short blurb
Czech SF comedy: corrupt time-ship pilot Karel chokes on a roll; identical twin Jan impersonates him, is shanghaied by unaging Nazis seeking to give Hitler an H-bomb, and must hop timelines to stop them.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) Near-future commercial time travel exists; anti-aging pills keep a Nazi cell young. They bribe pilot Karel to take them back with a hydrogen bomb. Karel dies choking on a breadroll; twin Jan (also a rocket designer) impersonates him for fiancée Eva and is mistaken by the Nazis for Karel. Jan can fly the ship; once he grasps their plan he triggers paradoxes by shuttling through time until he defeats them and cleans up consequences of Karel's death. Adaptation of Josef Nesvadba's story; Petr Kostka plays both twins.
