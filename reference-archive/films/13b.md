# 13B: Fear Has a New Address / Yavarum Nalam (2009)

## Short blurb
Manohar’s new flat 13B plays a private TV serial that predicts his family’s next hours—echoing a 1977 massacre in a house with the same number—until he realizes the “killer face” in the finale is him saving them.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) R. Madhavan bilingual horror. Serial *Sab Khairiyat* / *Yavarum Nalam* appears only on their TV at 13:00, mirroring pregnancies, exams, miscarriage. Dig reveals 1977 Doordarshan anchor Chitra’s family murdered; blamed brother Ashok; cop “suicide.” Twist: family doctor Balu/Shinde was the hammer killer (avenging fan-brother Sairam); spirits used the lookalike modern family and prophetic show to force justice. Manu kills Balu with the sledgehammer matching his nightmare. Coda: Balu now “haunts” Manu’s phone. **Borderline:** precognitive broadcast / ghost revenge, not traveler TT—Wave B listed for “time/parallel elements.” Cult following; Collider best-Indian-horror lists.
