# Coherence (2013)

## Short blurb
During a comet’s passage, a dinner party decoheres into overlapping houses—each with slightly different friends. Glow sticks and notes try to map which reality is whose.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Micro-budget; largely improvised within structure. Power fails; another house identical appears. Schrödinger’s cat / decoherence banter becomes literal: groups split across dark streets into alternate party-copies. Colored glowsticks label “origin” groups; violence and betrayal escalate as people replace their doubles. Emily’s ending choice—attacking another Emily to steal a happier reality—lands on a phone call that may expose the swap.

- Not time travel; **interacting parallel worlds** (archive scope).
