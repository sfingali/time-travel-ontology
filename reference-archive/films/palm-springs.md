# Palm Springs (2020)

## Short blurb
Nyles has been looping a wedding forever; maid-of-honor Sarah gets pulled in. Together they test science, suicide, and intimacy as exits—while Roy hunts Nyles for dragging him in.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Honeymoon-period comedy shifts to existential. Dinosaur-day gags; cave glowing pool is the mechanism entry. Sarah’s research leads to a controlled explosion idea in the cave to break entanglement. Ending: they trigger it and wake to November 10?—Ambiguity remains whether escape worked, but emotional arc completes. Roy subplot shows loops can contain others unwillingly.

## Analysis
- Self-aware Groundhog Day descendant (characters discuss the trope).
- Consent and shared vs solitary looping.
