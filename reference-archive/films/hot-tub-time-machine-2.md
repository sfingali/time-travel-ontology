# Hot Tub Time Machine 2 (2015)

## Short blurb
Sequel sends the gang to a raunchy future night to fix a shooting aftermath.

## Detailed plot summary
Lou is shot; hot tub sends survivors to a future party night to identify the shooter and repair the timeline. Critically poorly received; diminishes first film’s 1986 nostalgia engine.
