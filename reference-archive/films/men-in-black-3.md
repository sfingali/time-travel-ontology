# Men in Black 3 (2012)

## Short blurb
Agent J goes to 1969 to prevent the murder of young Agent K and an alien invasion enabled by that death.

## Detailed plot summary
Boris the Animal escapes lunar prison and changes the past. J partners with young K and Griffin (a fifth-dimensional being who sees timelines). Restoring K's path restores the present.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Multiverse glimpses via Griffin.
