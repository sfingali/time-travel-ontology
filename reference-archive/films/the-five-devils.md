# The Five Devils (Les Cinq Diables, 2022)

## Short blurb
Child Vicky can smell and recreate any scent in jars; capturing aunt Julia's scent after prison release hurls her into dark magical memories of mother Joanne's past affair—and the secret of her own existence.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia premise.) Vicky Soler has a solitary magical gift: reproducing scents in labelled jars, including her mother Joanne's (Adèle Exarchopoulos). When father's sister Julia is released from prison and enters their Alpine village life, Vicky captures Julia's scent and is transported into past memories revealing Joanne and Julia's love affair, village secrets, and how Vicky came to be. Léa Mysius's second feature; Directors' Fortnight Cannes 2022; French release 31 Aug 2022.
