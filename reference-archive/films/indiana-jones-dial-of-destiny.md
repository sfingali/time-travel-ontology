# Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny (2023)

## Short blurb
Indy and Helena pursue Archimedes’ Dial; Nazis seek to weaponize a portal to 212 BC—and 1969 intervenes.

## Detailed plot summary
1969-set adventure: Jürgen Voller wants the Antikythera/Archimedes dial to rewrite WWII via ancient mathematics opening a time fissure. Climax unexpectedly delivers characters to the Siege of Syracuse era. Indy briefly contemplates remaining in the past; Helena brings him home. Franchise’s first explicit TT MacGuffin.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Mixed critical reception; clear TT mechanics late-film.
- Ties archaeology myth to SF portal.
