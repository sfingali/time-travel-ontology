# Sliding Doors (1998)

## Short blurb
Helen splits into two lives at a London Underground door: one catches the train and discovers her boyfriend’s affair; one misses it and stays deceived. Parallel cutting compares love, jobs, and fate until the timelines rhyme on a hospital revelation.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

PR executive Helen (Paltrow) is fired; rushing home, she either **catches** or **misses** a Tube train. Catch-Helen finds Gerry with mistress Lydia, leaves him, cuts her hair, falls for James (Hannah), builds a new career. Miss-Helen is delayed, remains with cheating Gerry, suffers mounting humiliations and a pregnancy complication.

The film intercuts both without a portal—pure counterfactual drama. Climactic convergence: both Helens end in hospital contexts; a final twist suggests the “surviving” continuity absorbs a memory flash of the other, or simply that chance will offer James again—interpretations vary, but the thematic point is that character persists across forks.

- **No mechanism:** Philosophical branching, not SF travel.
- **Parallel editing as ontology:** Audience holds both worlds.
- **Soft convergence:** Final beat flirts with single timeline without hard merge rules.
- **Romance ethics:** Knowledge of affair as the critical informational fork.

## Endings / production notes
