# The Tomorrow War (2021)

## Short blurb
Present-day civilians are drafted to 2051 to fight alien “Whites”; father-daughter science may stop the war early.

## Detailed plot summary
Future soldiers recruit from the past because humanity is losing. Dan Forester meets adult daughter Muri; alien biology and toxin research become the strategic lever. Ending emphasizes preventing the future war by acting in the present/near future—soft branching hope after closed-loop wartime structure.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Amazon blockbuster; military TT logistics foregrounded.
- Alien twist reframes “invasion from future” expectation.
