# 13 Going on 30 (2004)

## Short blurb
Thirteen-year-old Jenna wishes to be “thirty, flirty and thriving” and wakes in her 30-year-old magazine-editor life.

## Detailed plot summary
Wish-fulfillment body/age jump: teen Jenna becomes adult Jenna with teen mind, navigating career, friendship betrayals, and lost childhood friend Matt. Soft magical mechanism; romance-comedy structure.
