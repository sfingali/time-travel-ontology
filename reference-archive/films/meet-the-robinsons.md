# Meet the Robinsons (2007)

## Short blurb
Orphan inventor Lewis is taken to a bright 2037 by future son Wilbur Robinson to stop Bowler Hat Guy—and the bowler AI Doris—from stealing Lewis’s memory scanner and creating a dystopian branch. Theme: “Keep Moving Forward.”

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Lewis fails adoption interviews while inventing a memory scanner to find his birth mother. At the science fair, mysterious Wilbur appears; sabotaged demo and a bowler-hatted villain set a chase into the future. 2037’s Robinson family (flying houses, frogs, jazz) is Lewis’s own eventual family—Wilbur is his son. Antagonist Goob (childhood almost-friend twisted by bitterness) + Doris (hat AI) steal the scanner to sell/control, producing a gray industrial tyranny when they succeed. Lewis must repair his confidence, fix the past science-fair moment, and refuse obsessive backward-looking grief. Walt Disney’s “Keep Moving Forward” quote closes the ethic: healthy relationship to time is invention, not rumination. Clear on-screen branch: dystopia vs Robinson utopia.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Orphan present / scanner motive.**
2. **Wilbur abduction to 2037.**
3. **Family reveal & Doris/Goob threat.**
4. **Dystopian alternate** shown as warning.
5. **Science-fair repair + emotional acceptance.**
6. **Forward-looking coda.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Branching kids’ TT:** One invention’s custody decides civilizational tone.
- **Villain as failed self-kin:** Goob = bitterness timeline personified.
- **Anti-rumination theme:** Explicitly frames TT ethics as psychological.
- **Bootstrap family:** Lewis meets descendants before becoming their ancestor.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Walt Disney Animation; mixed initial reviews, warmer reappraisal. Joyce book loose inspiration. Theme song / score push optimism. Staple of family TT lists.
