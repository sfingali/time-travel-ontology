# Haunter (2013)

## Short blurb
Dead teen Lisa relives the same day in a haunted house, trying to warn the next living family from the Pale Man.

## Detailed plot summary
Natali (Cube) horror: Lisa realizes she’s a ghost looping the day of her family’s murder. Serial killer’s pattern spans generations. Loop as purgatorial warning system.
