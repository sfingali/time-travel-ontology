# Oke Oka Jeevitham / Kanam (2022)

## Short blurb
Three struggling friends ride scientist Paul’s machine to March 1998 to save Adhi’s mother and rewrite their youth—until their child selves steal the machine to 2019 and the mother’s death proves fixed.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Sharwanand, Amala Akkineni, Ritu Varma; bilingual Telugu/Tamil (*Kanam*). 1998: Paul’s experiment kills partner Michael. 2019: Paul recruits Adhi (musician), Chaitu, Seenu to stop that experiment and let Adhi save mom (car accident). Rules briefed; jump to 1 Mar 1998. Adults infiltrate school lives (guitar teacher, auto driver, peon). Kids discover machine, iris-scan to 2019; adults stranded. Parallel chases; Michael still dies; Adhi confesses identity to mother before truck hits. Acceptance: cherish the borrowed month. Kids returned with advice to live fully; adults restart present. Emotional TT over hard physics.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Machine + date targeting; iris as biometric key.
- Kid/adult co-presence; stolen machine = mid-plot crisis.
- Novikov-ish mother’s death vs changeable personal attitudes.
