# The Lake House (2006)

## Short blurb
Hollywood remake of *Il Mare*: doctor Kate and architect Alex fall in love through a lakeside mailbox linking 2004 and 2006—until a deferred meeting and a traffic death force a warning letter that rewrites the opening.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN Wikipedia; deepen vs *Il Mare* entry.) Sandra Bullock & Keanu Reeves. Kate (2006) leaves a note for the next tenant; Alex (2004) answers from two years earlier. Correspondence, shared dog, and near-misses; Alex’s architect father subplot. Kate asks Alex to intercept her ex; he dies in the attempt in one branch. Finale mirrors Korean source: Kate’s warning letter reaches living Alex at the house as she prepares to leave the first note—nested opening/closing. Magical-realist mailbox; less explicit object-passing than *Il Mare* in some beats. Kannada remake lineage via Korean original.
