# Will You Be There? (당신, 거기 있어줄래요, 2016)

## Short blurb
Terminally ill surgeon Soo-hyun receives ten mysterious pills that send him thirty years into the past, where he meets his younger self and tries to see—and save—lost love Yeon-ah.

## Detailed plot summary
(Sources: English Wikipedia; AsianWiki.) Based on Guillaume Musso’s novel *Seras-tu là?*. **Present (2015):** On a medical mission, Soo-hyun saves a girl; her grandfather gives him ten pills. Taking one, he sleeps and awakens face-to-face with his past self from thirty years earlier. **Past (1985):** Young Soo-hyun, living happily with Yeon-ah, finds a collapsed man who claims to be Soo-hyun from the future and offers proof. Present Soo-hyun confesses he wants to see Yeon-ah once more; the younger self grows uneasy as he learns the unbelievable future ahead. AsianWiki/IMDb summaries add: older Soo-hyun is seriously ill; the pills enable short past visits; he and his younger self try to alter events around Yeon-ah’s fatal accident, with timeline consequences.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Finite resource (10 pills); dual Soo-hyun interaction across ~30 years.
- Romance / regret frame rather than hard closed-loop SF.
