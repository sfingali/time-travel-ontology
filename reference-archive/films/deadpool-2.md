# Deadpool 2 (2018)

## Short blurb
Cable arrives from a ruined future to kill Firefist; Deadpool’s X-Force comedy collides with parental trauma.

## Detailed plot summary
Cable’s chrono-strategic mission to prevent his family’s murder by mutant Russell/Firefist. Deadpool forms X-Force; Vanessa’s death drives Wade. Ending uses Cable’s time-travel device for a joke-repair montage. Sets up later TVA jokes in Deadpool & Wolverine.
