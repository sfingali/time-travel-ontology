# The Call (콜, 2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Multiple cascading present-day rewrites invite timeline charts.

## Short blurb
Seo-yeon (2019) and Young-sook (1999) share a house phone across twenty years; helping each other mutates into a serial-killer duel that keeps rewriting the present.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia; based on 2011 film *The Caller*.) Seo-yeon finds a cordless phone linking to Young-sook being abused by her shaman adoptive mother. Seo-yeon's tips let Young-sook prevent the fire that killed Seo-yeon's father—present becomes affluent. Young-sook kills her mother to avoid a predicted death and becomes a serial killer; victims vanish from 2019. After Seo-yeon accidentally reveals Young-sook's future arrest, Young-sook kills Seo-yeon's father in 1999 and kidnaps child Seo-yeon. Adult Seo-yeon steals a 1999 police notebook, feeds then corrects information under threat to her mother Eun-ae. Cross-time chases culminate with Eun-ae seemingly sacrificing herself to stop Young-sook; 2019 resets with Eun-ae alive (scarred). Mid-credits: older Young-sook warns her past self, erasing Eun-ae again; adult Seo-yeon is captive once more.
