# Run Lola Run (1998)

## Short blurb
Lola has 20 minutes to replace her boyfriend Manni’s lost 100,000 DM and save him from mob death. Three consecutive runs—same start, different butterfly collisions—yield three endings, with flash-forward polaroids of bystanders’ altered lives.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Berlin: Manni calls Lola—he left the cash on a train; boss Ronnie will kill him at noon. Lola runs. **Run 1:** She fails to get money from bank-manager father; joins Manni’s supermarket robbery; is shot by police. **Run 2:** Slightly different stair/dog timing; father spat; she robs the bank with a gun; Manni is hit by an ambulance. **Run 3:** She leaps the dog, wins casino cash with screamed luck, meets Manni who has recovered the money from the homeless man—both survive, walking off with surplus.

Between runs, red-tinted bed talk and cartoon interstitial; passerby lives flash-forward differently each time (lottery, accident, faith). Techno score drives the tempo of possibility.

## Paradox analysis

- **Branching presentation:** Not memory-loop; audience sees alternate worldlines.
- **Butterfly portraits:** Extrapolated futures for extras = chaos theory made montage.
- **Agency + chance:** Screaming at roulette = will vs randomness theme.
- **No ontology lecture:** Form *is* the theory.

## Endings / production notes

German indie smash; influenced a generation of “restart” cinema and games. Potente breakout. Runtime 80 min of pure momentum.
