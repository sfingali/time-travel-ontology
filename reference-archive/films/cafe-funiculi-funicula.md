# Cafe Funiculi Funicula / Before the Coffee Gets Cold (コーヒーが冷めないうちに, 2018)

## Short blurb
Tokyo café Funiculi Funicula’s haunted seat sends patrons to meet café regulars in other times—but only until the coffee cools, and nothing you do can change the present.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: Kawaguchi novel wiki + film credits.) Adaptation of *Kohi ga Samenai Uchi ni* starring Kasumi Arimura as barista Kazu. Rules (novel→film): only one seat; ghost must leave for toilet; cannot leave the seat in the past; can only meet prior café visitors; present cannot be altered; return before coffee goes cold (~1 hour). Four vignettes: Fumiko confronting boyfriend’s US departure; nurse Kohtake seeking Alzheimer’s husband’s letter; bar owner Hirai facing estranged sister; co-owner Kei visiting her unborn daughter in the future. Theme: past fixed, future open. Screenplay Satoko Okudera (*Girl Who Leapt*, *Summer Wars*). Novel franchise sequels (*Tales from the Cafe*, etc.); Fox/SK Global TV adaptation developed.
