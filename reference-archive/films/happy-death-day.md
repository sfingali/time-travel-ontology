# Happy Death Day (2017)

## Short blurb
Mean sorority girl Tree Gelbman is murdered on her birthday by a baby-masked killer and wakes up to relive the day. She loops through suspects—serial killer John Tombs, professor Gregory Butler, roommate Lori—until poisoned cupcake and character growth close the loop.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

After a drunken night, Tree (Jessica Rothe) wakes in classmate Carter Davis’s dorm on her birthday. She is rude to Carter, ignores roommate Lori’s cupcake, continues an affair with married professor Gregory Butler, and that night is lured into a tunnel and stabbed by a figure in a baby mask.

She wakes again in Carter’s bed. Events repeat; she avoids the tunnel, reaches a party, and is still killed. Third loop: she barricades her room—the killer is already inside. Convinced she is looping, she uses iterations to trail suspects and dies repeatedly (including a hospital escape in Gregory’s car).

Carter helps her map the day. News names **John Tombs**, a serial killer held at the campus hospital. Tree races to stop his escape; Carter saves her but Tombs kills Carter. At the bell tower Tree nearly finishes Tombs, then realizes killing him while Carter is dead would lock that outcome—so she hangs herself to reset with Carter alive.

Confident loop: she ends the affair, reconciles with her estranged father over lunch, traps and kills Tombs, celebrates with Carter, and eats Lori’s cupcake. She still wakes in the loop—because the cupcake was poisoned; Lori is the true killer, framing Tombs via her nurse access after Tree kept discarding the cupcake. Lori (also sleeping with Gregory) attacks; Tree force-feeds her the cupcake and kicks her out a window.

Restaurant coda with Carter; next morning Tree panics at another “wake-up,” but Carter reveals a prank—it is truly the next day. They kiss.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Ignorant deaths** — tunnel / party / room: establish loop + mask threat.
2. **Investigation loops** — suspect trail; hospital chase.
3. **Tombs false solution** — Carter dies; Tree suicides to restore him (moral reset rule).
4. **“Perfect day” fail** — kill Tombs + eat cupcake → still loops.
5. **True killer reveal** — Lori / poison; final confrontation.
6. **Exit** — next calendar day confirmed by prank beat.

Influences Landon cites: *Groundhog Day*, *Scream*, *Halloween*, 1980s teen comedies. Original ending (Stephanie Butler killing Tree in hospital) test-screened badly and was cut.

## Paradox analysis

- **Death-triggered Groundhog loop:** Unexplained origin in film 1 (reactor explained in 2U).
- **Knowledge carry / body damage:** Injuries accumulate across loops until she faints—soft continuity.
- **Moral constraint:** Tree will not accept a “win” that leaves Carter dead—agency over timeline preference.
- **Misdirection killer:** Classic slasher red herrings (Tombs, Butler) before intimate betrayal.
- **Exit condition:** Killing the true murderer *and* surviving the night ends the loop—not merely surviving once.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Blumhouse / Universal; $4.8M → ~$125.5M worldwide. Shot Loyola University New Orleans. Mask designer Tony Gardner; King Cake Baby / Pelicans mascot lawsuit settled 2021. Novelization paired with 2U. Sequel *Happy Death Day 2U* (2019); third film long in development.
