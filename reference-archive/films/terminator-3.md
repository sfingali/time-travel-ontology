# Terminator 3: Rise of the Machines (2003)

## Short blurb
TX targets John Connor’s future officers; Judgment Day is delayed, not prevented—Skynet still rises.

## Detailed plot summary
A decade after T2, John Connor lives off-grid. A T-X and a reprogrammed T-850 arrive; the mission expands beyond killing John to eliminating his future command staff, including Kate Brewster. Despite destroying Cyber Research Systems assets, the film ends with Judgment Day occurring—reframing T2’s victory as a delay. Contested among fans for undermining T2’s ending.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Shifts series toward inevitability vs T2’s “no fate.”
- Kate Brewster becomes co-lead of future Resistance.
