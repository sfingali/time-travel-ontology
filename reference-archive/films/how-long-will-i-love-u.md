# How Long Will I Love U (超时空同居, 2018)

## Short blurb
2018 woman Gu Xiaojiao and 1999 man Lu Ming suddenly share the same Shanghai apartment across nineteen years—rom-com after a spacetime merge.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Fantasy romantic comedy from Xu Zheng's idea; Lei Jiayin and Tong Liya as mismatched roommates whose eras overlap inside one flat. Homages noted to *The Lake House* and *The Time Traveler's Wife*. ~¥900M China gross on modest budget; fine lead chemistry (pair previously in *Weaning*).
