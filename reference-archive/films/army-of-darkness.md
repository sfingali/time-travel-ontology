# Army of Darkness (1992)

## Short blurb
Ash is hurled to 1300 AD to retrieve the Necronomicon and fight Deadites.

## Detailed plot summary
Evil Dead 3: slapstick/horror Ash in medieval England. Time travel is portal/book magic more than SF machine. Cult comedy-horror classic.
