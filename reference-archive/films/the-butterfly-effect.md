# The Butterfly Effect (2004)

## Short blurb
Evan blackouts let him jump into childhood via journals/drawings—each fix cascades into worse adult timelines until a drastic origin choice.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Evan suffers childhood trauma blackouts (molestation, prank killing, dynamite, etc.). College: rereading journals inserts adult mind into past body; returns to altered present (prison, loss of limbs, Kayleigh’s fates). Climactic theatrical cut: Evan as fetus strangling his cord to never be born—erasing damage. **Director’s cut** ending: he merely abandons Kayleigh in childhood; they pass as strangers. Alternate endings circulated on home video.

## Temporal analysis
- Explicitly named for Bradbury’s butterfly metaphor.
- Body-jump rewrite with memory retention of prior timelines (painful).
- Trauma ethics: exploitation debates in criticism.
- Sequels (2, 3) unrelated direct-to-video.
