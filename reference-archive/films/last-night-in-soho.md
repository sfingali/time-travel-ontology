# Last Night in Soho (2021)

## Short blurb
Fashion student Eloise night-slips into 1960s Soho as Sandie—glamour curdles into horror.

## Detailed plot summary
Eloise’s psychic sensitivity lets her experience 1960s London nightlife through aspiring singer Sandie. Past and present bleed; male violence and exploitation themes drive the thriller. Time travel is experiential/psychic rather than machinic; climax binds identities across decades.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Wright’s neon-noir; soundtrack-forward.
- Debate: pure TT vs haunting/possession hybrid.
