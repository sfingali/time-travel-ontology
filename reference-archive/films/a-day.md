# A Day (하루, 2017)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Multi-POV day loops (doctor, paramedic, vengeful father); fan loop counts (~15) and Maanaadu inspiration notes.

## Short blurb
Surgeon Kim Joon-young’s daughter dies in a taxi crash on her birthday; the day resets until he and paramedic Min-chul confront the grieving father whose child’s heart was taken without consent—and whose death is the loop’s reset key.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia + widely cited explainers paraphrasing the same beats.) Returning from abroad, renowned doctor Joon-young (Kim Myung-min) races to meet daughter Eun-jung, finds a taxi wreck, and discovers she was the pedestrian killed—alongside Min-chul’s pregnant partner Mi-kyung in the cab. He wakes back on the plane; the day repeats. Min-chul (Byun Yo-han) is also looping. Interventions fail. They learn taxi driver Kang-sik is deliberately targeting their loved ones: three years earlier Joon-young performed an unethical heart transplant on Eun-jung using Kang-sik’s son Ha-roo without paternal consent after a prior accident involving Min-chul’s delayed ambulance. Kang-sik’s own death in the crash resets the loop; saving him (and seeking forgiveness / public confession of the transplant) breaks it. Fantasia Audience Award (Bronze); inspired Tamil *Maanaadu* (archive already has that entry).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Loop anchored to Kang-sik’s death, not the daughters’—explains failed “just save her” attempts.
- Shared awareness between Joon-young and Min-chul; Kang-sik as antagonistic looper.
- Moral exit: confession/press reckoning rather than pure physics.
