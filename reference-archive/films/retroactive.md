# Retroactive (1997)

## Short blurb
Former criminal psychologist Karen Warren hitchhikes into a desert murder spiral with abusive Frank Lloyd. A scientist’s experimental time machine lets her rewind the same violent stretch—each pass raising the body count—until she overloads the system sixty minutes and refuses the ride that started it.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Karen’s car dies on a lonely Texas road. She rides with Frank and his battered wife Rayanne, who are moving stolen computer chips. A trooper tickets Frank; a tow-truck driver (Jesse) passes them. At friend Sam’s gas station, evidence of Rayanne’s affair with Jesse surfaces. Frank shoots Rayanne and tries to kill Karen; she flees into the desert and finds Brian’s lab, where a mouse-tested time device accidentally hurls her **~20 minutes** earlier—back into Frank’s passenger seat.

**Pass 2:** Karen asks the trooper for help; Frank kills Rayanne, the trooper, and Jesse. She reaches Brian, explains the loop, and they agree to rewind together so Brian can call police.

**Pass 3:** Karen grabs Frank’s gun; Rayanne misses with her own; Frank still massacres Rayanne, Jesse, the trooper, and vacationing parents of boy Paul. Brian fails to bring police in time. Frank takes Paul hostage, forces Brian to send them back.

**Pass 4:** Frank and Paul arrive mid-fight; Paul helps Karen but dies with his parents. Karen and Brian flee to the lab under fire and **overload the system for a ~60-minute jump**.

**Stabilizing pass:** Karen wakes in her own car **before** Frank picks her up and refuses the lift. She warns the trooper; Brian collects her. At the station aftermath: Frank has killed Sam and Jesse and been shot dead by Rayanne, who is arrested. Karen’s non-participation collapses the hitchhike cascade.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Inciting hitchhike → first murder.**
2. **Accidental 20-min rewind** (lab mice device).
3. **Escalating interventions** that add victims (trooper, Jesse, family).
4. **Hostage forced rewind** (Frank learns the machine exists).
5. **System overload → 60-min pre-pickup reset.**
6. **Refusal of the ride** as the winning branch.

Tone Out / Time Out notes compare it to 1980s low-budget exploiters (*Tremors*, Band) with black-comic energy; Belushi’s Frank is overbearing on purpose.

## Paradox analysis

- **Short-window branching:** Not a full Groundhog Day day—surgical rewinds of the same hour.
- **Worsening butterfly:** Knowledge + intervention inflate collateral deaths until refusal strategy.
- **Villain learning:** Frank’s awareness of the machine becomes a meta-threat (forces travel).
- **Pre-inciting fix:** Winning move is upstream of the hitchhike, not better marksmanship inside it—prefigures *Source Code* / *Edge of Tomorrow* “find the right pass” logic.
- **Single traveler persistence:** Karen (and later Brian) retain memory across rewinds; others reset.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Orion release; ~91 minutes; RT ~57% from limited reviews. First Morneau–Belushi pairing (later *Made Men*). Roger Clinton cameo as truck driver. Modest indie SF-thriller rather than studio tentpole.
