# Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Multiverse villain-origin charts and “spell revision” flowcharts are common fan explainers; less classical TT diagram culture than Endgame but heavy crossover mapping.

## Short blurb
After Mysterio outs Peter Parker, Strange’s botched forgetfulness spell tears the multiverse. Villains (and Spider-Men) from other universes arrive. Peter tries to cure them; to seal the rift he has Strange erase **everyone’s memory of Peter Parker**.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Post-*Far From Home*, Peter’s identity is public; MIT rejects him, MJ, and Ned. They visit **Doctor Strange**, who prepares a spell so the world forgets Peter is Spider-Man. Peter interrupts with exceptions (MJ, Ned, May, Happy…); the unstable spell cracks the barrier between universes.

Pulled in (at moments near their deaths in home films): **Norman Osborn / Green Goblin**, **Otto Octavius**, **Flint Marko / Sandman**, **Max Dillon / Electro**, **Curt Connors / Lizard**. Strange boxes the spell; Peter learns the arrivals die fighting Spider-Man at home. Against Strange’s wish to send them back immediately, Peter steals the spell artifact, traps Strange in the Mirror Dimension, and tries to **cure** the villains at Happy’s apartment (Stark tech). Octavius is cured; Goblin reasserts, kills **Aunt May** (“with great power…”).

Grief-broken Peter-One meets **Peter-Two (Tobey)** and **Peter-Three (Andrew)**—drawn by the same spell. They share loss (Ben; Gwen), build cures, and lure villains to the Statue of Liberty. Cures largely succeed; Goblin is cured after a brutal fight. A new multiversal rift opens as more variants try to enter. Peter realizes the only seal is for Strange to cast a spell making the world forget **Peter Parker entirely** (not merely that he is Spider-Man). He says goodbye to MJ and Ned; Strange casts it; visitors return home (Eddie Brock briefly appears; leaves a symbiote sample).

Two weeks later Peter reintroduces himself to no one—he watches MJ and Ned happy without him, visits May’s grave, and resumes street-level heroism alone in a new suit.

## Temporal / multiverse structure

| Beat | Effect |
|---|---|
| Interrupted forget-identity spell | Opens multiversal “hunt” for anyone who knows Peter is Spider-Man |
| Contained spell | Villains held; Mirror Dimension fight |
| Cure arc | Attempts to change other-universe fates upon return |
| Final memory wipe | Closes rift; MCU loses all personal memory/records of Peter (writers: “magical redaction”) |

Not classical time travel—**interacting parallel universes** via magic—but archive-in-scope as multiverse contact with reality rewrite.

## Paradox / continuity analysis

- **Fate editing:** Cured villains may survive on return—branching their home continuities (Octavius clearly reformed).
- **Memory vs history:** Writers (McKenna/Sommers) describe emotional redaction, not hard BTTF photo-erasure math; Spider-Man’s public deeds can remain while Peter the person does not.
- **MCU bridge:** Lands Tobey/Andrew canon as alternate universes; sets *Brand New Day* isolation; Venom sample leftover.
