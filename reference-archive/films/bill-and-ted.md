# Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure (1989)

## Short blurb
Slackers Bill Preston and Ted Logan must pass a history presentation or be split up—dooming a utopian future built on their music. Future agent Rufus lends a phone-booth time machine; they collect historical figures for a report and learn “San Dimas time” waits for no one.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

San Dimas, 1988: Bill (Winter) and Ted (Reeves) face a history oratory or military school (Ted). Future utopia depends on their Wyld Stallyns music; Rufus (Carlin) arrives with a booth that goes anywhere/anywhen. They grab Napoleon, Billy the Kid, Socrates, Freud, Beethoven, Joan of Arc, Lincoln, etc., stash them in the present, lose them around the mall/park, reassemble for the report, and ace it. Rufus confirms the future is saved; sequels extend the bootstrap (*Bogus Journey*, *Face the Music*).

Running gags: “Has it been done to you?” predestination pranks; clock ticking in home-present while they travel (San Dimas time).

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Utopia stake / Rufus brief.**
2. **Historical kidnapping tour.**
3. **Present-day babysitting chaos.**
4. **Report triumph / future secured.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Bootstrap utopia:** Future that sends Rufus exists because the report succeeds because Rufus helped.
- **San Dimas time:** Present clock runs during travel—deadline comedy physics.
- **Harmless abduction:** Historical figures returned none the worse (mostly)—soft touch.
- **Character catchphrases as philosophy:** “Be excellent” as temporal ethic.

## Endings / production notes

Orion/De Laurentiis path to hit status; launched Reeves. Sequels 1991 and 2020. Phone booth ≠ TARDIS legally distinct.
