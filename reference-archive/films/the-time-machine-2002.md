# The Time Machine (2002) (2002)

## Short blurb
Wells’s great-grandson directs: Alexander Hartdegen cannot save his fiancée, then journeys to Eloi/Morlock future.

## Detailed plot summary
Personal tragedy motivates the machine; past proves resistant to change (predestination on Emma’s death). Far-future lunar disaster backstory reframes Morlocks. Mixed reception vs 1960 Pal film.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Director Simon Wells is H. G. Wells’s great-grandson.
