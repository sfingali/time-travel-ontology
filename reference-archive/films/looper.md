# Looper (2012)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Straw diagrams and three-timeline maps are a meme the film itself predicts (“drawing diagrams with straws”).

## Short blurb
In 2044, hitmen called loopers kill targets sent from 2074. When Joe’s older self arrives unbound and escapes, young Joe must choose between gold payday mythology and stopping a future tyrant’s origin.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Kansas City 2044: Loopers execute hooded future victims for silver; closing one’s own loop pays gold. Abe (from the future) runs the local syndicate. Joe’s friend Seth fails to kill Old Seth; Abe’s Gat Men mutilate young Seth so injuries appear on Old Seth—**causality bleed** as teaching horror—then kill him at a carved address.

Joe’s next target is Old Joe, unbound. In the timeline where young Joe succeeds, he retires to China, spirals, finds love, then is seized to close his loop; his wife dies in the raid. Old Joe overpowers captors and returns to 2044 to kill the child who will become the Rainmaker (a telekinetic crime boss who mass-closes loops).

Diner confrontation: Old Joe won’t debate mechanics (“we’ll be here all day drawing diagrams with straws”). Maps of three children born same hospital/day; farm path leads to Sara and son **Cid**, whose TK dwarfs ordinary “TK” parlor tricks. Gat Men attacks escalate; Cid kills Jesse telekinetically. Joe realizes: if Old Joe kills Sara, traumatized orphan Cid becomes the Rainmaker. Joe shoots himself; Old Joe vanishes; Sara may raise Cid differently.

Chinese release restores more Shanghai material (DMG request per Wikipedia).

## Temporal structure & paradox analysis

Public reconstructions (Stack Exchange, Slate straw diagram, Cinemablography) usually posit:

1. **Timeline A:** Loop closed “successfully”; Rainmaker rises; Old Joe fails escape or never alters 2044 enough.
2. **Timeline B (film body):** Old Joe escapes; hunts child Rainmaker; memories blur as young Joe’s present rewrites Old Joe’s past.
3. **Timeline C:** Young Joe’s suicide erases Old Joe; Sara lives; Rainmaker path possibly averted.

Johnson publicly prioritized character over Primer-style rigor; bleeding scars (Seth) show present→future linkage even when branching is allowed. Influences cited by Johnson include Terminator, 12 Monkeys, Timecrimes, Akira.

## Endings / cuts
