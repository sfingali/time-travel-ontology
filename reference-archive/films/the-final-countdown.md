# The Final Countdown (1980)

## Short blurb
USS *Nimitz* passes through a weird Pacific storm from 1980 into December 6, 1941—hours before Pearl Harbor. Captain Yelland must decide whether to smash the Japanese fleet or preserve history; a second storm resolves the temptation.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Systems analyst Lasky (Sheen) boards *Nimitz* for observation. A mysterious electric storm drops the carrier into 1941 waters. After verifying via radio and a rescued 1941 senator’s party (including Laurel and secret-agent suspicions), the crew confronts Japanese scout planes and the approaching strike force. Yelland (Douglas) prepares to intercept—modern jets vs Zeroes would rewrite the Pacific War—then the storm returns, yanking *Nimitz* back to 1980 before weapons release. Coda: Lasky meets older Laurel and a surviving companion who somehow “waited,” implying personal closed threads; the war remains as known.

## Paradox analysis

- **Temptation plot:** Power to prevent Pearl = ultimate grandfather-adjacent military wish.
- **Storm as unexplained gate:** No ownership of the mechanism.
- **Nonintervention ending:** History preserved by external force, not solely ethics—debated by fans.
- **Lasky–Laurel coda:** Soft personal loop without macro rewrite.
