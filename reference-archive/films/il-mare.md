# Il Mare (시월애, 2000)

## Short blurb
Eun-joo (1999) and Sung-hyun (1997) occupy seaside house “Il Mare” two years apart and fall in love through a mailbox that crosses time—source text for Hollywood’s *The Lake House*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia; deepen pass.) Eun-joo leaves a Christmas card asking the next tenant to forward mail; Sung-hyun, first resident in 1997, receives a card dated two years ahead. Experiments prove the mailbox links 1997↔1999; objects (tape player, books) can pass. She tries to bring a book about his dying architect father and is delayed by an accident. Solo “dates” in separate times; planned beach meeting fails when he dies in traffic the day he tries (at her request) to stop her fiancé leaving. She warns him via mailbox not to go. Finale returns to opening: Sung-hyun arrives alive with her warning letter as she prepares to leave the card—nested causal loop. Remakes: *The Lake House* (2006); Kannada *Minchagi Nee Baralu* (2015). Title from Italian “the sea”; house name diegetic.
