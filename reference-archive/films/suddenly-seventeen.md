# Suddenly Seventeen (28岁未成年, 2016)

## Short blurb
Career-sidelined 28-year-old Liang Xia eats “magic chocolates” and swaps for five-hour stretches with her 17-year-old self—together they reclaim art, dump a lukewarm boyfriend, and almost OD on youth.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Zhang Mo debut; Ni Ni, Wallace Huo, Darren Wang. Big Liang’s proposal-pressure plan fails; chocolates + crash trigger Little Liang. Toggle rule: one chocolate → five hours of 17-year-old mind in 28-year-old body; they coordinate via video/WeChat. Little Liang dates free-spirited Yan; Big Liang uses her drawings for agency work and confronts partner Mao’s coldness. Gallery climax: Big Liang paints without a chocolate. Meteor destroys the only factory (10 left); Little Liang downs nine chasing Yan, flatlines; mental meet-cute where Little Liang dissolves proud of grown-up Liang. Soft ending: Yan sees her billboard; Mao runs naked with a “take me back?” sign. Age/body swap adjacent to *13 Going on 30* with dual-persona TT flavor.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Shared body, alternating consciousness—not calendar travel.
- Finite MacGuffin chocolates create deadline pressure.
- Archive inclusion: body-era displacement romance cluster.
