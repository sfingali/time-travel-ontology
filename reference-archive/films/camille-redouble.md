# Camille redouble (Camille Rewinds, 2012)

## Short blurb
On her 40th birthday Camille collapses and awakens as her teenage self in the 1980s—with adult memory—facing whether to rewrite love and life.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: French TT surveys / EN wiki *Camille Rewinds*.) Noémie Lvovsky writes/directs/stars; party collapse sends Camille into her lycée years. She navigates parents, friends, and future husband with foreknowledge. César-nominated comedy-drama in the personal-rewind lineage of *Peggy Sue Got Married*.
