# The 100th Love with You (君と100回目の恋, 2017)

## Short blurb
Riku’s secret “record of life” rewinds time so he can keep saving bandmate Aoi from a fatal truck crash—until she snaps the record rather than watch him die for her again and again.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) Miwa & Kentaro Sakaguchi. University summer: Aoi’s band prepares a July 31 concert; she dies chasing lyrics into traffic. She wakes looping; Riku reveals he has cheated time since childhood via a vinyl that rewinds days—he practiced guitar for years of loops to serenade young Aoi after she broke her father’s record. They date through a “perfect” summer montage; she still dies. Riku loops her death countless times; only trading his life for hers once let her remember dying. Uncle Shuntaro confesses the same cancer-wife loops. Aoi breaks the record; they choose one last lived day—fireworks concert as her “best of 100 birthdays.” She dies; her farewell record urges him to live. PIA audience #1; Chinese remake rights sold.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Vinyl grooves = discrete rewind control.
- Sacrifice creates the memory-bleed that recruits Aoi into awareness.
- Cap is emotional (“100 birthdays”), not a hard counter UI.
