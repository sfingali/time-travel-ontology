# We Are from the Future (Мы из будущего, 2008)

## Short blurb
Russian blockbuster: cynical modern treasure-hunters digging WWII relics slip into the Great Patriotic War itself and must survive—and rethink patriotism—beside historical Red Army fights.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: franchise listings / IMDb / EN coverage of *My iz budushchego*.) Four young men scavenging war relics near St. Petersburg experience a timeslip into 1942 combat. Modern attitudes collide with frontline reality; survival and moral conversion drive the arc. Hugely popular domestically; sequel *We Are from the Future 2* (2010) extends the formula. Soft displacement mechanism—emphasis on war film immersion over paradox math.
