# Click (2006)

## Short blurb
Architect Michael Newman gets a remote that pauses/fast-forwards life—then learns it rewrites automatically.

## Detailed plot summary
Michael (Sandler) receives a Bed Bath & Beyond “universal remote” from Morty (Walken) that controls his reality. Fast-forwarding past chores and fights skips years; the remote learns and auto-skips. Moral fable about presence vs career obsession.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Not classical TT machine; remote as destiny/reality control.
- Tonal shift from comedy to melodrama.
