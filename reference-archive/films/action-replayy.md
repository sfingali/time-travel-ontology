# Action Replayy (2010)

## Short blurb
Bunty travels to 1975 via his girlfriend's grandfather's time machine to make his divorcing parents fall in love—Hindi comedy often compared to *Back to the Future*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Young Bunty Chopra, facing parents Kishen and Mala's divorce, uses a scientist's machine to visit 1975 when they were unmarried and engineer their romance. Akshay Kumar and Aishwarya Rai Bachchan lead; director cites a Gujarati play and Wells alongside BTTF comparisons. Mixed reception; notable Hindi mainstream TT vehicle.
