# Omni Loop (2024)

## Short blurb
Dying physicist Joan relives a week via experimental time effects, seeking meaning and a finished equation.

## Detailed plot summary
Indie SF: Joan discovers she can reset roughly a week while facing terminal illness. She mentors student Zula, revisits relationships, and wrestles with whether looping avoids death or life. Quieter, melancholic loop cinema.
