# The Adam Project (2022)

## Short blurb
In 2050, fighter pilot Adam Reed crash-lands in 2022 and teams with his twelve-year-old self to recover lost time-travel tech tied to his wife Laura—and to heal their relationship with inventor father Louis.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Netflix/Levy/Reynolds vehicle (same duo as *Free Guy*). Future Adam, fleeing pursuers after a mission gone wrong, drops into the bedroom of young Adam shortly after their mother’s death. Zoe Saldana’s Laura is missing/presumed lost with key chrono-jet knowledge; villainous Maya Sorian (or corporate/military rivals in the future apparatus) wants monopoly control of time travel. The two Adams recruit younger versions of allies, confront grief over mom, and reconnect with Louis—whose research is the secret root of the future tech. Action-comedy set pieces (jets, school, lab) serve a closed-loop recovery: ensuring the right people hold the invention and that Adam’s personal timeline can still include love and family. Light *Flight of the Navigator* energy in the kid/adult buddy pairing.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Future heist fallout → 2022 crash.**
2. **Dual-Adam odd-couple** training/bonding.
3. **Laura mystery / Sorian threat.**
4. **Louis reconciliation** as emotional & technical key.
5. **Recover/secure chrono tech; restored futures.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Closed recovery:** Goal is possession/ethics of the machine, not endless tourism.
- **Family as causality:** Father’s invention + sons’ choices braid personal and civilizational stakes.
- **Soft bootstrap:** Future depends on past healing without Primer cruelty.
- **Kids’-adventure grammar:** Empower young Adam as co-equal agent.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Netflix global hit metrics; Reynolds banter tone. Compared widely to *Navigator* and *BTTF*. Standalone; sequel talk intermittent.
