# Maanaadu (மாநாடு, 2021)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Same-day political assassination iterations; dual POV (hero then villain) after shared blood.

## Short blurb
NRI Abdul Khaliq is forced to shoot a chief minister, dies, and wakes on the same flight—trapped in a day loop against corrupt DCP Dhanushkodi until he stops a communal-riot plot.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Khaliq helps friends elope; cops frame him into assassinating CM Arivazhagan. Shot dead, he loops mid-flight over Ujjain (Kaal Bhairav time-god motif). Iterations: prevent sniper then IED; Dhanushkodi enters the loop via accidental transfusion and races to cause a religious riot for powerbroker Paranthaman. Khaliq must also save Hindu leader Tamizhvanan earlier that day to convince Arivazhagan. Final iteration: expose Paranthaman, take a bullet for the CM, wake in hospital—then post-credits suggests the loop continues when Mansoor attacks. Hits (~₹117 cr); sequel confirmed.
