# Doctor Strange (2016)

## Short blurb
Strange traps Dormammu in an endless death loop in the Dark Dimension to bargain for Earth's safety.

## Detailed plot summary
After mystic training, Strange uses the Time Stone to rewind battles and then to loop his own death against a timeless entity until Dormammu relents.
