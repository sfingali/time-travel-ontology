# Abracadabra (2017)

## Short blurb
Carmen’s abusive husband Carlos is accidentally possessed—via botched wedding hypnosis—by the soul of 1980s schizophrenic dancer Tito; she must choose whose mind keeps the body.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Pablo Berger; Maribel Verdú, Antonio de la Torre, José Mota. Carlos crashes cousin Pepe’s hypnosis act, then alternates into charming, brilliant, psychotic Tito Cantero (1983 murder-suicide). Séance via dying vessel; club dance to Steve Miller Band’s “Abracadabra.” Pepe hypnotizes both into Carlos’s mindscape; Carmen seemingly picks Tito then stabs his mental form, waking to leave Carlos. Shortlisted for Spain’s Oscar submission. **Borderline for archive:** possession/mediumship more than calendar TT—kept because Wave B listed it in EU gap set and it shares body-displacement DNA with *Mr. Queen* / *Suddenly Seventeen*.
