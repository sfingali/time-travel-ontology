# 24 (2016, Tamil)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Watch rules (30s freeze; ≤24h reverse) + 1990/2016 dual; National Award–level craft.

## Short blurb
Scientist Sethuraman invents a time watch; twin Athreya murders for it. Decades later watchmaker son Mani inherits the device and must stop the waking paraplegic Athreya—Suriya in three roles.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) 1990: Sethuraman finishes Project 24; Athreya kills Priya for the watch. Sethuraman leaves infant Mani and locked watch with Sathyabhama on a train then dies tricking Athreya. 2016: Mani opens the box, learns freeze/reverse limits. Athreya wakes from 26-year coma, kills Mani for the watch, reverses ≤24h, discovers he needs Mani to upgrade range. Disguised as Sethuraman he tricks Mani into upgrading; Mani learns the truth and jumps to 1990—Athreya hitchhikes the jump. Toddler Mani can't warn, but adult Sethuraman finds the upgraded watch in the crib, freezes time, kills Athreya, and discards the watch for a peaceful life. Mid-credits: young Mani and Sathya. Music A. R. Rahman; National Awards for cinematography and production design.
