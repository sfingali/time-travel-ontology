# Orange (live-action film, 2015)

## Short blurb
Theatrical live-action of Ichigo Takano's manga (Tao Tsuchiya, Kento Yamazaki): future letters guide Naho to keep Kakeru alive—released before the 2016 anime.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia media notes.) Directed by Kojiro Hashimoto; Japanese release 12 December 2015. Same core: Naho's future letters, Kakeru's depression and predicted death, friends' coordinated intervention. Distinct from the Telecom/TMS anime and from *Orange: Future*.
