# Indru Netru Naalai (இன்று நேற்று நாளை, 2015)

## Short blurb
A 2065 time machine stranded in 2015 lets unemployed Elango and fake astrologer Arumugam game stocks and "predictions"—until they accidentally save a gangster and must fix the mess.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Scientist's machine fails to auto-return from 2015. Elango, Arumugam, and inventor Parthasarathy find it; the duo steal it after Parthasarathy's coma. They fake astrology success and impress Anu's father with future stock tips. Thwarting a police encounter keeps gangster Kuzhandaivelu alive; he threatens them. Damaged machine can only go one-way to the damage moment. Kuzhandaivelu kills Anu; Elango jumps back, tricks past/present Kuzhandaivelu into mutual death, and prevents their earlier accident so the machine is never found—returning it to 2065. Leads wake into a peaceful wedding timeline. Tamil SF comedy landmark; sequel long in development.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Abuse → butterfly → sacrificial one-way fix → machine never discovered.
