# Aditya 369 (ఆదిత్య 369, 1991)

## Short blurb
India’s landmark time-travel film: Krishna Kumar and friends ride Professor Ramdas’s machine to Krishnadevaraya’s 1526 Vijayanagara court and a post–WWIII 2504 underground world while chasing a thrice-stolen diamond.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia; deepen pass.) Art thief Raja Varma swaps a museum diamond; schoolboy Kishore witnesses. Activating Aditya 369, Krishna (Nandamuri Balakrishna), Hema, and a constable arrive in 1526; Krishna impresses the emperor (also Balakrishna dual role) by reciting future Tenali verse and hears a prophecy of two thefts 500 years apart. Framed by dancer Simhanandini; escape with imperial trust. Malfunction dumps them in 2504 underground survivors on nuclear power; Krishna sees a news report of his own 1991 death. Returning, he foils Varma; machine explodes; he survives a cliff leap; Ramdas dismantles the project. Inspired by Wells more than *BTTF*. Dubbed Hindi *Mission 369*, Tamil *Apoorva Sakthi 369*. Three cinematographers for past/present/future looks. Long-discussed sequel *Aditya 999 Max*.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Past tourism + bleak future vignette sandwich present heist.
- Prophecy as soft closed loop on the diamond thefts.
- Foundational Telugu/Indian TT reference point for later *24*, *Maanaadu*, *Oke Oka Jeevitham*.
