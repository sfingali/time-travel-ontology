# Minchagi Nee Baralu (ಮಿಂಚಾಗಿ ನೀ ಬರಲು, 2015)

## Short blurb
Kannada remake of Korea’s *Il Mare*: lovers in different years exchange letters through a seaside house mailbox—South Indian node in the global mailbox-TT chain that also produced *The Lake House*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN wiki / remake notes.) Transplants the *Il Mare* (2000) premise into Kannada cinema: two tenants separated by years communicate via the same magical mailbox, fall in love, and struggle to meet in one present. Candidate #280. Mechanism identical to parent: letters cross a fixed time offset tied to the house.
