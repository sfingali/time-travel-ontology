# Beyond the Infinite Two Minutes (ドロステのはてで僕ら, 2020)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Nested Droste monitors → cascading near-future; one-take structure maps cleanly to diagrams.

## Short blurb
Café owner Kato finds his upstairs monitor shows two minutes ahead of the café TV (which shows two minutes behind); facing them creates a Droste tunnel into further futures.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Kato and friends exploit the window. Future Kato pushes him to ask out Megumi; she declines but he must still coach his past self to avoid paradox. Schemes entangle a gang rivalry; Kato uses foreknowledge and accumulated props to rescue Megumi. Time cops try to dose them with memory-wipe powder; sneezes create a paradox that erases the cops. Kato and Megumi talk about their lives. Shot to appear as a single long take (nagamawashi) on a tiny Kyoto-café budget (~¥3 million).
