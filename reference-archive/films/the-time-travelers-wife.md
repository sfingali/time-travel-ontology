# The Time Traveler's Wife (2009)

## Short blurb
Henry DeTamble involuntarily time-travels due to a genetic disorder; Clare builds a life around his absences.

## Detailed plot summary
Adaptation of Audrey Niffenegger’s novel. Henry drops naked into moments across Clare’s life—including her childhood—forming a non-linear marriage. Bootstrap elements (library book, shared memories) and tragic inevitability of Henry’s death structure the romance. Mechanism is involuntary biological, not a machine.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Strong bootstrap/romance kinship with Somewhere in Time.
- 2022 HBO series re-adapts the novel.
