# Future Cops (超時代鐵男, 1993)

## Short blurb
Wong Jing’s *Street Fighter* pastiche: future cops chase a criminal into the past (early-90s HK school/youth setting), spawning martial-arts anachronism chaos with Andy Lau, Jacky Cheung, and Aaron Kwok.
