# Returner (リターナー, 2002)

## Short blurb
From 2084’s last Tibetan fortress, soldier Milly jumps a portal to 2002 Tokyo to stop Earth’s first contact with aliens—the “Daggra”—from sparking the war that doomed her future, teaming with hitman Miyamoto against triad Mizoguchi.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Takashi Yamazaki sci-fi action; Anne Suzuki & Takeshi Kaneshiro. Milly arrives mid-Tokyo Bay shootout; Miyamoto thinks he shot her (metal plate in coat). She fakes a neck bomb to force his help finding the crashed alien held at NISS. They realize humanity started the war by capturing/killing the weakened first Daggra; Mizoguchi wants the tech for black market. Oil-rig climax: alien rescued; invisible force field (future tech) lets Miyamoto kill Mizoguchi; Daggra craft (disguised 747) retrieves their wounded and leaves—war ceases, Milly fades. Coda: Miyamoto quits violence, is shot by a spared thug, saved by a second metal plate slipped into his coat by a *second* Milly from a later pass—who also dropped the newspaper predicting his death. Budget ~$4M; Japan #14 of 2002; mixed/negative overseas reviews citing heavy borrowing (*Terminator*, *Léon*, *Matrix*).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Mission erase: successful prevention deletes Milly’s war-born future (she fades).
- Nested second Milly trip = bootstrap armor and newspaper drop.
- “Returner” also Miyamoto’s hitman nickname for disrupting illegal deals.
