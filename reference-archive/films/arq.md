# ARQ (2016)

## Short blurb
Engineer Renton wakes beside ex-lover Hannah as Bloc rebels raid his house for stolen Torus “scrips.” His ARQ device—thought to be perpetual motion—is actually generating a localized time loop; iterations escalate until nested outer resets and Torus mercenary Sonny learn the loop too.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Renton wakes with Hannah; three men (Father, Sonny, Brother) break in for scrips stolen from Torus Corporation. Renton dies escaping and **resets to the bedroom**. Loops accumulate with rising knowledge:

Early passes: bind and escape; cyanide-gas plans; Hannah revealed as Bloc operative (“Mother”) who resents Renton for abandoning her to Torus torture. Bargains break into shootings. After one death Hannah **retains memory**—Renton theorizes the ARQ is looping time, not making free energy. It recycles gasoline-catalyzed energy by replaying the interval, localized to the house.

Sonny is exposed as a Torus mole; he kills the room; later he retains memory too. Brother accidentally kills Renton after Father is recruited against Sonny. Renton suicides to deny Sonny the ARQ. Sonny then saves **Cuz** (a mercenary who dies by ARQ electrocution every prior loop) and massacres Bloc members. Renton and Hannah poison them; Sonny’s dying trap kills Hannah, so Renton lets the next loop start.

All parties become aware; hostages; Renton is forced to shut the machine; blackout deaths; escape proves the loop is **house-bounded**. They re-enter to destroy the ARQ; Cuz (Hannah’s torturer) dies; Sonny restarts it and dies.

**Nested structure:** Logs and a truncated video reveal an **outer loop of nine inner loops**—after nine, memories wipe and the nine-pack repeats. They have run thousands of outer cycles. On the ninth inner loop they record a message hoping future selves get the ARQ to the Bloc before Torus robots arrive. A robot kills them; Hannah gasps awake—implying another cycle or breakthrough awareness.

## Paradox analysis

- **Groundhog structure with knowledge bleed:** Memory retention spreads to hostiles—raising difficulty each aware pass.
- **Device ontology:** ARQ is not FTL/travel; it is a **temporal battery** that loops a volume to reuse energy—Elliott’s twist on perpetual motion.
- **Nested loops:** Outer wipe explains why “first” mornings feel fresh despite thousands of repeats.
- **No clean exit on screen:** Ending is recursive hope via message-in-a-bottle to future iterations.
- **Trust as time variable:** Hannah’s allegiance and Renton’s selfishness rewrite each pass until shared goal (deny Torus) stabilizes.
- **Localized causality:** Leaving the house exits the loop volume—rare hard spatial bound among Netflix loopers.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Netflix original; TIFF Discovery 2016; shot in Toronto in 19 days for under $2M. Elliott (later *Orphan Black*) had optioned the script years earlier; Netflix revived it after rights reverted. THR’s John DeFore praised paradox wrap-up relative to many low-budget loopers; Rotten Tomatoes sparse (~43% from few reviews). Runtime 88 minutes.

## Comparative notes / public commentary

Netflix algorithm-era looper: 88 minutes, single primary location, escalating knowledge. THR’s TIFF note praised the nested-nine reveal relative to many low-budget loop films that stall after the first twist. Elliott’s *Orphan Black* writers’-room background shows in faction politics (Bloc vs Torus) grafted onto house-siege grammar.

Spatial bound is the sharpest formal choice: leaving the property exits the battery’s volume—rare among Netflix/streaming loopers that treat the whole city as fair game. Memory bleed to antagonists (Sonny, then Cuz) converts Groundhog comfort into arms race, prefiguring *Happy Death Day 2U*’s “others learn the loop” turn without dimensional travel.

Logs/video-as-archive inside the fiction mirror this meta-archive’s project: future iterations need records because outer wipes erase minds. The closing robot + gasp refuses closure—hope is informational, not kinetic.

Teaching use: table the three layers beside *Edge of Tomorrow* (enemy adaptation) and *The Endless* (cult time bubbles). Energy-MacGuffin reading: ARQ as civil-war battery makes the loop a resource war, not only a character crucible.
