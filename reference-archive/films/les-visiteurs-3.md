# The Visitors: Bastille Day / Les Visiteurs 3 (Les Visiteurs: La Révolution, 2016)

## Short blurb
After Béatrice spikes the potion with Grand Marnier, knight Godefroy and squire Jacquouille overshoot into 1793 Reign of Terror—meeting Jacquouillet the public accuser and hunting an Eusæbius descendant for a ticket home.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Third *Les Visiteurs* film (after 1993 and 1998’s *Corridors of Time*). Clavier & Reno reprise; new cast Franck Dubosc, Karin Viard, Sylvie Testud (as Charlotte de Robespierre), et al. Ill-chosen liqueur during a wedding sends the medieval pair into revolutionary France. Jacquouille’s descendant Jacquouillet is a public accuser; Godefroy’s kin try to escape the Terror. Quest: find enchanter-line heir to reopen corridors. Shot in Czech Republic/Belgium; critical and commercial flop in France despite €-level budget (~$27M reported; ~$18.6M worldwide). Completes trilogy file set with archive’s `les-visiteurs.md` / `les-visiteurs-2.md`.
