# Ditto (2022 remake) (동감, 2022)

## Short blurb
Remake of the 2000 classic: teens in different years connect by radio/device and change each other’s lives—updated cast (Yeo Jin-goo, Cho Yi-hyun, Kim Hye-yoon) and era pairing for a new generation.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN wiki / KR coverage.) Faithful-spirit remake of Kim Jeong-kwon’s *Ditto* (2000), which linked 1979↔2000 via HAM radio. 2022 version refreshes the years and youth-drama texture while keeping the core: two young people converse across time, fall into care/attraction, and face the cost of altering the other’s timeline.
