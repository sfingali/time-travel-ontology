# River (リバー、流れないでよ, 2023)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Fixed 2-minute single-take loops at a Kyoto ryokan; memory carries across resets — maps cleanly like *Beyond the Infinite Two Minutes*.

## Short blurb
Staff and guests at a century-old Kibune inn (Fujiya) are trapped in repeating two-minute loops; memories persist while clocks and places snap back.

## Detailed plot summary
(Sources: English Wikipedia; Third Window Films; Cinemore JP interview paraphrase.) Mikoto, a waitress, stands by the Kibune River behind the annex when called back to work—then finds herself at the river again after two minutes. After the title, the film proceeds as two-minute single-take sequences. Everyone remembers prior loops and quickly recognizes the trap: hot sake that will not warm, endless *zōsui*, a bath that cannot be left. Strangers appear (e.g. a woman seeking help after a breakdown). Characters debate causes and try to break the cycle; cooperation in the final sequence ends the loop. Snow weather during production was folded into the narrative as the “worldline” glitching with characters’ emotions (Cinemore / Ueda). Same Europe Kikaku / Ueda–Yamaguchi team as *Beyond the Infinite Two Minutes*.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Uniform 2-min cycle; shared memory; single consistent timeline (Ueda: puzzle/flowchart logic, not parallel universes).
- Companion piece to *Beyond the Infinite Two Minutes* and *Summer Time Machine Blues*.
