# The Visitors II: The Corridors of Time (Les Visiteurs II, 1998)

## Short blurb
Sequel: Godefroy must recover the Duke's jewels and Sainte Rolande relic that Jacquouille smuggled into the 20th century—items keeping the corridors of time open—before a botched return drops them into the Revolution.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN/FR Wikipedia synopses.) As Godefroy prepares to marry Frénégonde, Duke Fulbert reports missing jewels including a fertility relic. Thief is Jacquouille, who hid them then recovered them in the present (prior film). Corridors stay open; descendant Jacquart is stranded in the Middle Ages. Godefroy returns to the present; farce with Béatrice, dentist Jean-Pierre (accidentally potion-dosed into 1123), Inquisition water-torture of Jacquart, wedding chaos, and jewel recovery from Cora. Wrong potion again: Godefroy and Jacquouille land in Revolutionary France—cliffhanger toward later sequels. Jean Reno / Christian Clavier comedy franchise continuation.
