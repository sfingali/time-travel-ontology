# The Myth (神話 / 神话, 2005)

## Short blurb
Archaeologist Jack dreams his past life as Qin general Meng Yi, escort of Korean princess Ok-Soo; present-day hunt for a meteorite zero-g tomb reunites him with still-immortal Ok-Soo trapped in the emperor’s floating mausoleum.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Stanley Tong martial-arts fantasy; Jackie Chan dual role. Qin arc: Meng Yi loves Ok-Soo but serves the emperor; retrieving the elixir, he dies after passing it to Nangong Yan; prince forces Ok-Soo and Nangong to test it—immortality + eternal imprisonment in the mausoleum. Present: Jack & William chase a Dasar floating tomb in India for anti-gravity meteorite fragments, duel insights unlock Meng Yi memories, anger funder Professor Koo. Qin mausoleum behind a waterfall is a floating palace powered by the strongest fragment. Ok-Soo mistakes Jack for Meng Yi; Koo raids for the elixir; William dies in mercury as the field collapses; Ok-Soo stays, refusing a false Meng Yi. Sequel *A Legend* (2024); 2010 CCTV series spin. Borderline TT via reincarnation + immortal stasis rather than machines.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Dual timeline: Qin past interleaved with modern dig.
- Immortality = frozen persons across millennia (soft time travel).
- Meteorite zero-g as SF MacGuffin binding eras.
