# Mark Antony (மார்க் ஆண்டனி, 2023)

## Short blurb
Tamil SF action-comedy: Mark finds scientist Chiranjeevi's telephone that can call any past date once—rewriting gangster history until rival Madhan steals the device and presents flip like musical chairs.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) In 1975 Chiranjeevi invents a phone that calls the past (one call per selected date). Gangsters Antony and Jackie fight Ekambaram; Antony dies and Jackie raises Antony's son Mark. In 1995 mechanic Mark discovers the phone, saves his mother, learns Jackie framed Antony, and calls the past so Antony kills Jackie. He wakes as the gangster kingpin with Ekambaram as guardian while Jackie's son Madhan is the mechanic. Madhan uses the phone to warn Jackie; timelines thrash until Antony returns from Colombia, kills Jackie and Madhan, then a final cliffhanger call tangle with Chiranjeevi and Jackie. Vishal and SJ Suryah in dual roles; released 15 Sep 2023; ~₹100 crore.
