# Timecrimes (Los Cronocrímenes) (2007)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Rich public diagrams/explainers exist.

## Short blurb
A man glimpses a woman, is chased by a bandaged figure, and falls into a scientist's time machine — becoming each role in a single afternoon loop.

## Detailed plot summary
Héctor sees a nude woman in the woods, is stabbed by a pink-bandage attacker, flees to a lab with a time machine, and emerges an hour earlier. As Héctor-2 he recreates events to preserve the loop (including creating the bandaged attacker from his own wound). Héctor-3 attempts further corrections involving a deadly van crash. The film is a compact causal knot.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Triple-Héctor overlap in one evening.
- Forced self-consistency.
