# Duckweed (乘风破浪, 2017)

## Short blurb
After a crash, race-car champion Xu Tailang (Ah Lang) wakes in 1998 and befriends his young father Ah Zheng—learning why the quiet, estranged man of the present became who he is.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Ah Lang wins the 2022 China Rally Championship as aging father Xu Zhengtai (Ah Zheng) watches. A reckless celebratory drive ends in a train collision. On the hospital gurney, Ah Lang is transported to 1998 in his birth town. A mugging leads younger Ah Zheng—now Ah Lang’s peer—to rescue him and claim him as a brother-in-arms. Ah Lang joins Ah Zheng’s gang, meets friends, and meets his mother (who, in the original history, died of postpartum depression soon after his birth). He witnesses the couple’s love and the events that hardened Ah Zheng. (Baidu/en Baike and Wiki agree on father–son reconciliation via past immersion; Wikipedia plot stops at learning intervening years.)
