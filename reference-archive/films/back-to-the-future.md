# Back to the Future (1985)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Family photo erasure and timeline ripple charts are pop-culture literacy.

## Short blurb
Marty McFly’s DeLorean trip to 1955 endangers his parents’ first meeting—he must make George punch Biff so Marty can exist to get back to a improved 1985.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Doc Brown’s plutonium-powered DeLorean hits 88 mph; Libyan terrorists kill Doc (apparently); Marty flees to 1955. He interrupts George and Lorraine’s meet, causing Lorraine to crush on Marty (Calvin Klein). With 1955 Doc he plans lightning-clock tower return. Enchantment Under the Sea dance: George finally floors Biff; kiss restores siblings on Marty’s photo. Marty returns to 1985 where Doc wears bulletproof vest, George is confident author, Biff waxes cars. Twin Pines → Lone Pine gag marks butterfly change.
