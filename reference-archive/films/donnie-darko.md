# Donnie Darko (2001)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Tangent Universe / Philosophy of Time Travel diagrams define fan culture; Director’s Cut inserts book pages.

## Short blurb
Teen Donnie is guided by Frank the rabbit through a Tangent Universe that must collapse—returning a jet-engine Artifact—or destroy the Primary Universe.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
October 1988. Donnie survives a jet engine crash because Frank lures him out. He sleepwalks, floods school, burns Downfall, dates Gretchen, and learns of Roberta Sparrow’s *Philosophy of Time Travel*: Tangent Universes, Living Receiver, Manipulated Dead/Living, Artifact. Climax: Gretchen killed; Frank (in bunny suit) shot by Donnie. To close the tangent, Donnie sends the engine back / stays in bed to die—Primary Universe restored; others wake with uncanny grief. Director’s Cut clarifies mythology; theatrical leaves more ambiguity.

## Temporal structure
- Tangent bubble with ~28-day limit.
- Artifact (engine) must return.
- Receiver’s death closes loop.
- Manipulated Dead (Frank) can contact Receiver.

## Paradox analysis
- Parallel/tangent rather than simple loop.
- Closed by sacrifice; primary world “forgets” explicitly but feels residue.
- Cult status drove endless explainers; sequel S. Darko widely dismissed.
