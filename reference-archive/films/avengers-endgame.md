# Avengers: Endgame (2019)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Time-heist path charts, Ancient One branching lecture stills, and fan Reddit/ThisIsBarry timeline diagrams are ubiquitous.

## Short blurb
Five years after the Snap, surviving Avengers use Quantum Realm “time GPS” and Pym Particles to steal past Infinity Stones, undo the Decimation in 2023, then return the Stones. Past-Thanos invades the present; Tony dies defeating him. Cap stays in the past with Peggy.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**2018:** After failing to reverse the Snap (Thanos has destroyed the Stones), Thor kills him. **2023:** Scott Lang escapes the Quantum Realm—five hours for him, five years outside—and proposes time travel. Banner/Hulk rejects *Back to the Future*-style rewriting: changing the past **branches** alternate realities; it does not overwrite their present. Stark builds the time suits.

### Time Heist (targets)

| Team | Destination | Stone |
|---|---|---|
| Banner, Lang, Rogers, Stark | 2012 New York | Time, Mind, Space (Space fails → 1970 Camp Lehigh redo) |
| Thor, Rocket | 2013 Asgard | Reality (Aether from Jane) |
| Barton, Romanoff, Nebula, Rhodes | 2014 Morag / Vormir | Power; Soul (Romanoff sacrifices herself so Barton can take it) |

Ancient One warns Banner: removing a Stone without returning it leaves her timeline defenseless—**branch realities**. Deal: return each Stone to the **exact moment** it was taken.

Complications: 2012 Loki escapes with the Tesseract (seed for *Loki* series). 2014 Nebula’s cybernetics sync with 2023 Nebula; 2014 Thanos learns the future and hitchhikes to 2023.

### Present climax
Banner snaps everyone back. 2014 Thanos destroys the Compound, intending a total remake of the universe. Portaled armies arrive; Stark steals the Stones and snaps Thanos’s forces away, dying.

Rogers returns Stones (and Mjolnir). He does not rematerialize on schedule; an elderly Cap appears on a bench and passes the shield to Sam Wilson—having lived a life with Peggy.

## Paradox analysis

- **Explicit anti-BTTF rule:** Banner’s lecture + Ancient One’s golden-timeline visual = MCU branching model (later explored in *Loki* / TVA).
- **Stone-return clause:** Meant to “erase” harmful branches chronologically so the Stone “never left.”
- **Cap debate (creators split):** Writers Markus/McFeely lean toward Cap as Peggy’s husband **in the main timeline** (no Stone removed by merely living there). Directors Russo argue he lived in a **branch** and used Pym Particles to return to main-timeline 2023 to hand off the shield.
- **2014 Thanos:** Killing him does not erase 2018’s Snap; he is a branched past self invading the present.
- **Closed-loop flavor:** Personal beats (Howard talk; Frigga; Peggy glimpse) without overwriting 2023’s history of loss.

## Endings / cuts
Theatrical ending as above. 2019 re-release added Stan Lee tribute / deleted material. 2026 *Endgame: Encore* re-release planned with bridging footage toward *Doomsday*.
