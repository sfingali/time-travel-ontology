# Triangle (2009)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Rich public diagrams/explainers exist.

## Short blurb
Friends board the deserted ocean liner Aeolus and enter a murderous loop where Jess must kill copies of her party to "reset."

## Detailed plot summary
Jess (Melissa George) and friends sail into a storm, board the Aeolus, and are attacked by a masked shooter. Jess realizes she is looping; seagulls and repeating deck events confirm. She kills versions of her friends, becomes the masked killer for the next iteration, then survives a wreck only to return home to a double — killing her and taking her place, then hitching to the harbor to restart the sailing trip. Grief over her disabled son fuels the loop.
