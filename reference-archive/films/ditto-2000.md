# Ditto (동감, 2000)

## Short blurb
Silla University students So-eun (1979) and Ji-in (2000) talk nightly over amateur radio across twenty-one years; future knowledge reshapes who she loves.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Yoon So-eun loves Dong-hee returning from army service; she chats on HAM radio with Ji-in. A planned clock-tower meeting fails because she waits at an unfinished 1979 tower in heat while he waits at the completed 2000 tower in rain. They realize the year gap (power-unplugged radio still works). Ji-in eventually convinces her with future details. She learns his parents are her friend Sun-mi and boyfriend Dong-hee—breaking her; she ends the romance so history can proceed. Ji-in finds yearbook evidence she once loved his father, then glimpses adult So-eun as a professor still single. The radio link ends; both move on.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Same campus, different decades; information-only travel until emotional consequences.
- Remade in 2022 (Yeo Jin-goo et al.).
