# Tokyo Revengers (live-action, 2021)

## Short blurb
Live-action adaptation of Ken Wakui's manga: Takemichi leaps to 2005 to stop the Tokyo Manji Gang's future crimes and save Hinata—distinct theatrical release from the anime.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia media section.) Directed by Tsutomu Hanabusa with Takumi Kitamura as Takemichi; released 9 July 2021 after COVID delays. Condenses early manga/anime arcs (joining Toman, Moebius conflict, protecting Mikey/Draken). Two-part sequel *Tokyo Revengers 2: Bloody Halloween* (Destiny / Decisive Battle, 2023) adapts the Valhalla/Bloody Halloween arc. Same leap mechanic (contact-triggered twelve-year jumps) as the series.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Theatrical continuity separate from anime seasons; same source timeline rules.
- Sequels deepen Bloody Halloween branching without replacing the TV adaptation.
