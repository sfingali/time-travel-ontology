# Your Name (2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
Teens Taki and Mitsuha swap bodies across time and must prevent a comet from destroying Itomori — memory fading as timelines realign.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) High-schoolers Mitsuha Miyamizu (rural Itomori) and Taki Tachibana (Tokyo) intermittently swap bodies, leaving notes. Swaps stop; Taki finds Itomori destroyed by Comet Tiamat fragments three years earlier (2013 vs his 2016). Drinking Mitsuha's kuchikamizake at Goshintai returns him to her body on festival morning; with Tessie and Sayaka he forces evacuation (substation sabotage, emergency broadcast). At twilight (kataware-doki) their timelines meet; he returns her braid; they write on each other's palms but night falls before she finishes. She persuades mayor-father to evacuate; he wrote "I love you" on her hand. Five years later near-amnesiac adults reunite on Suga Shrine steps asking each other's names. Fan timelines of swap days and comet impact are common.
