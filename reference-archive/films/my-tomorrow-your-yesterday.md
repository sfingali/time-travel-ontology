# My Tomorrow, Your Yesterday (ぼくは明日、昨日のきみとデートする, 2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Opposed time arrows; age-match window chart; film ends with Emi’s reverse montage.

## Short blurb
Takatoshi falls for Emi on a Kyoto train; she ages *backward*, meeting him only one month every five years—their overlapping age-20 month is their last romance before roles reverse across childhood rescues.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Takahiro Miki; Sota Fukushi & Nana Komatsu. Emi’s world runs reverse; she already lived Takatoshi’s future as her past, keeps a backward journal, and was the adult who saved drowning 5-year-old him. They can be lovers only when ages match at 20 for one month. After March 16 parting, he later meets 15-year-old and then 5-year-old Emi (festival fire rescue). Closing montage replays the film from her reverse POV ending at their first meeting—her last equal day. Theme: Back Number “Happy End.”

## Timeline/structure notes
