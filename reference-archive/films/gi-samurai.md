# G.I. Samurai / Sengoku Jieitai (戦国自衛隊, 1979)

## Short blurb
Sonny Chiba leads a JSDF unit that slips from modern maneuvers into the Sengoku period, allies with warlord Nagao Kagetora, and unleashes tanks and rifles on samurai armies—prototype Japanese military timeslip spectacle.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: EN Wikipedia / genre surveys.) During an exercise, a JGSDF element with vehicles and modern arms is displaced into the Warring States era. Lt. Yoshiaki Iba (Chiba) negotiates survival by siding with **Nagao Kagetora** (Uesugi-aligned figure in the film’s history mix). Anachronistic firepower wins battles but breeds political hubris and tragedy as men go native or die. Tone is darker than the 2005 remake-continuation *Samurai Commando: Mission 1549*. Foundational text for JP “SDF into history” cluster (*Zipang*, *Mission 1549*).

## Timeline/structure notes
