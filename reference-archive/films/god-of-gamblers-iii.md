# God of Gamblers III: Back to Shanghai (賭俠2之上海灘賭聖, 1991)

## Short blurb
ESP duel between Sing (Saint of Gamblers) and Tai-kun warps spacetime, dumping them into 1937 Shanghai—where Sing meets his grandfather, triads, Japanese forces, twin sisters, and a poker showdown that decides the city’s fate before he can return to 1991.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Sequel to *God of Gamblers II*; Chow Yun-fat’s God and Andy Lau’s Knight are absent despite English/Chinese title numbering quirks. Tai-kun regains ESP and attacks Sing; mutual full-power clash sends both to **Shanghai 1937**. Sing meets grandfather Chow Tai-fook and millionaire Ding Lik (Ray Lui, *The Bund* echo), navigates Ding’s enemies and Japanese military, confuses twin sisters Yu-san/Yu-mong (Gong Li internationally; Amber Fang Taiwan cut), learns who beat French “God of Gamblers” Pierre Cashon, and climaxes in a poker battle with Shanghai’s fate at stake before finding the way back to 1991 Hong Kong and Lady Dream.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Accidental ESP-induced timeslip; comedy ancestor/period pastiche.
- Dual Gong Li / Amber Fang versions for PRC vs Taiwan markets.
- Part of Chow’s God of Gamblers comedy spin-off line, not Chow Yun-fat trilogy continuity.
