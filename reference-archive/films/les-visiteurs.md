# Les Visiteurs (1993)

## Short blurb
After a witch's potion makes knight Godefroy kill his future father-in-law, a flawed time potion dumps him and squire Jacquouille into 1992 France—culture-clash farce and one of France's biggest hits.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) In 1123 Godefroy de Montmirail saves Louis VI and wins Frénégonde's hand, then hallucinates and fatally shoots Duke Fulbert. Mage Eusæbius's return-potion omits quail eggs; Godefroy and Jacquouille arrive in 1992. They wreck a mail van, meet descendant Béatrice de Montmirail (who resembles Frénégonde), and clash with nouveau-riche Jacquart (Jacquouille's descendant) who owns their castle. Rings from two eras collide; a modern medium Ferdinand (Eusebius's line) prepares a return potion. Jacquouille prefers 1992 freedom with homeless Ginette and swaps places so Jacquart is sent medieval instead. Godefroy returns in time to deflect the crossbow bolt onto the witch; Jacquart flails in 1123 mud.
