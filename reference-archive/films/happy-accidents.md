# Happy Accidents (2000)

## Short blurb
Ruby Walters falls for Sam Deed, who calmly insists he is from the year 2470 sent back to stop her murder—until therapy, evidence, and genre confirmation force the question of delusion versus destiny.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Indie NYC romance-thriller: Ruby (Tomei), burned by bad relationships, meets eccentric Sam (D’Onofrio). His future-claim includes specifics about her death and behavioral “corrections” he must teach her. Friends and a therapist read psychosis; Ruby oscillates between love and fear. Brad Anderson (*Session 9*) keeps ontology unstable for most of the runtime—kin to *Safety Not Guaranteed*’s “is the ad real?” engine—before SF elements pay off enough to reframe prior oddities as mission parameters. Tone blends rom-com warmth with uncanny dread.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Meet-cute + impossible biography.**
2. **Behavioral training as love language / control.**
3. **Therapy & friend interventions.**
4. **Evidence tipping toward truth (or deeper delusion).**
5. **Climax that resolves the murder-prevention claim.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Ambiguous traveler:** Audience shares Ruby’s uncertainty—formal suspense.
- **Bootstrap romance:** Future knowledge shapes the relationship that may create the traveler’s motive.
- **Ethics of prevention:** Can/should Sam rewrite Ruby’s fate without consent?
- **Soft confirmation:** Less Primer diagram, more emotional proof.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

IFC/indie release; cult following among TT-romance fans. Anderson’s bridge between drama and genre. Pair with *Safety Not Guaranteed* and *The Time Traveler’s Wife*.
