# Time Bandits (1981)

## Short blurb
Boy Kevin joins six dwarfs who stole the Supreme Being’s map of creation’s holes and are looting history—Napoleon, Robin Hood (Cleese), Agamemnon (Connery)—before Evil genius traps them in the Fortress of Ultimate Darkness.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Suburban Kevin’s bedroom wall opens; dwarf robbers flee the Supreme Being into historical heists via map portals. Kevin tags along through set-piece eras (Italy’s Napoleonic night, Sherwood’s bureaucratic Robin, Mycenaean Agamemnon who nearly adopts him, RMS Titanic). Evil (David Warner) lures them to the Fortress to seize the map and remake creation. The Supreme Being arrives as a fussy bureaucrat, defeats Evil (leaving concentrated Evil chunks), reclaims the map, and forgets a bit of Evil in Kevin’s home—which combusts; parents touch the remnant and vanish. Kevin, returned, faces firefighters as the only one who understands.

Gilliam’s middle film of the “Dreams” trilogy (*Jabberwocky* / *Brazil* adjacency). Comedy cosmology: time travel as bureaucratic defect in Creation.

## Paradox analysis

- **Map holes:** Portals as cosmology bugs, not science.
- **Tourism without rewrite ethic:** Theft from history; little butterfly anxiety.
- **Evil leftover:** Suggests cycles will continue—Kevin’s education incomplete.
- **God as admin:** Satire of orderly time.

## Endings / production notes

HandMade Films; George Harrison money. Cult classic; Apple TV+ remake series announced in later years. Connery’s heroic turn filmed quickly.
