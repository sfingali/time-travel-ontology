# Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Multiverse “glitch” overlays and Earth-label explainers exploded after release; Across/Beyond continue the diagram culture.

## Short blurb
Brooklyn teen Miles Morales is bitten by a radioactive spider as Kingpin’s super-collider tears open parallel universes. Displaced Spider-people—jaded Peter B. Parker, Gwen, Noir, Peni, Spider-Ham—teach him the mask while cellular decay races the clock to shut the machine down.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Miles struggles under police-officer father Jefferson, who distrusts Spider-Man. Uncle Aaron takes him to an abandoned subway to paint; a radioactive spider bites him. Returning, Miles finds **Wilson Fisk / Kingpin**’s Alchemax collider, built to pull alternate versions of dead wife Vanessa and son Richard into Earth-1610. Local Peter Parker fights Green Goblin and Prowler to shut it down, is shoved into the beam, and—dying—gives Miles a USB kill-drive, warning reactivation could destroy the city. Kingpin murders Peter as Miles watches.

At Peter’s grave Miles meets **Peter B. Parker**, a burned-out 38-year-old from another Earth, pulled through by the collider. They raid Alchemax; Olivia Octavius (Doc Ock) reveals visitors suffer **cellular decay** if they stay. Gwen Stacy / Spider-Woman saves them. Aunt May shelters more arrivals: **Spider-Man Noir, Peni Parker / SP//dr, Spider-Ham**—all glitching. Miles offers help; the veterans bench him as unready.

Miles flees to Aaron’s and learns Aaron **is Prowler**. Kingpin’s team attacks May’s; Miles’s identity spills; Aaron refuses to kill his nephew and is shot by Kingpin. Jefferson arrives and mistakes Spider-Man for Aaron’s killer. Peter B. restraints Miles and plans to stay behind as the sacrifice operator. Jefferson’s hallway apology (“I see this spark in you”) unlocks Miles’s powers; he builds his own suit with May, frees himself, and joins the fight. He sends each Spider home with a new drive and hurls Kingpin into the kill switch. Jefferson, seeing Spider-Man save the city and learning Kingpin killed Aaron and Peter, softens. Miles accepts the role. Gwen later contacts him cross-dimension. Mid-credits: Miguel O’Hara / Spider-Man 2099 argues with Earth-67’s Spider-Man—franchise bridge to *Across*.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Origin bite & collider discovery** — personal power + portal MacGuffin.
2. **Peter’s death / USB charge** — mentor handoff; “destroy the collider” quest item.
3. **Peter B. arrival** — first stable parallel contact; mentor-as-screwup.
4. **Alchemax / decay rule** — visitors have a timer; stakes become go-home-or-die.
5. **Spider-team assemble at May’s** — five art-style universes in one room.
6. **Prowler reveal / Aaron’s death** — family tragedy as Kingpin cost.
7. **Leap of faith** — Miles self-actualizes; refuses others’ sacrifice script.
8. **Collider destroy / go-home** — parallel visitors returned; Earth-1610 sealed (for now).
9. **Tease 2099 / 67** — bridge to Across’s canon-event theology.

Not classical time travel: **lateral universe travel** with irreversible local deaths. *Across the Spider-Verse* later reframes some events as “canon,” but Into’s text is grief, mentorship, and portal physics.

## Paradox analysis

- **Parallel worlds, not rewind:** Kingpin wants alternate living family, not his own past restored—ethical theft of other lives.
- **Decay as conservation:** Soft rule preventing permanent multiversal immigration.
- **Glitch aesthetic = ontology:** Visual error states signal wrong-universe occupancy.
- **No grandfather loop:** Miles does not prevent Peter’s death; he inherits the aftermath.
- **Sequel tension:** Across’s “canon events” retcon how obligatory Peter’s death was—Into itself plays it as tragedy Miles must grow past, not destiny to enforce.
- **Collider as wormhole engine:** Opens many worlds at once; destruction is the only local fix.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Sony Pictures Animation / Columbia; Oscar Best Animated Feature; rare A+ CinemaScore. Landmark mixed 2D/CGI “comic panel” look (stepped animation, Ben-Day, misregistered color, no motion blur). Lord/Miller producing; largest Imageworks crew then assembled. Dedicated to Stan Lee & Steve Ditko. Sequel *Across* (2023); *Beyond* scheduled 2027. Gross ~$395M on ~$90M.
