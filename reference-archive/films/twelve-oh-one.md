# 12:01 (1993)

## Short blurb
Office worker Barry Thomas relives the same day after a lab accident—racing to save a scientist and escape the loop.

## Detailed plot summary
Fox TV movie adaptation of Lupoff’s 12:01 PM idea, released the same year as Groundhog Day. Barry alone retains memory; must prevent a murder and correct the experiment. Often cited in loop-film genealogies beside Groundhog Day.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Distinct from 1990 short 12:01 PM (Kurtwood Smith).
