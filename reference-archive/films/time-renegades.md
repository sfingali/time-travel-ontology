# Time Renegades (시간이탈자, 2016)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Dual eras (1983 / 2015) linked by dream POV; same woman across decades; shared serial-killer investigation.

## Short blurb
A 1983 teacher and a 2015 detective, stabbed/shot on the same calendar day decades apart, begin seeing through each other’s eyes in dreams and race to stop a killer targeting the women they love.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) On New Year’s celebrations, high-school teacher Ji-hwan (1983) proposes to Yoon-Jung; after a purse theft he is fatally stabbed chasing the robber. In 2015, detective Gun-woo is fatally shot pursuing a suspect. Both reach the same hospital on the same day/time 32 years apart, survive, and begin dreaming each other’s POV. Gun-woo’s cold-case work surfaces an unsolved 1980s murder with Yoon-Jung as victim. Ji-hwan learns of her coming death via the dreams and tries to guard her on wedding-dress day; air-raid street blocks and a chase through a gambling house fail—he finds Yoon-Jung dead in a restroom after confronting a bloody-handed suspect. The men collaborate across time to identify the killer and protect Yoon-Jung / her 2015 counterpart So-Eun (Im Soo-jung dual role per coverage).
