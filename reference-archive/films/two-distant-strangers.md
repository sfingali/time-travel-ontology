# Two Distant Strangers (2020)

## Short blurb
Oscar-winning short: a Black man relives the day a police encounter kills him, seeking a way out.

## Detailed plot summary
Cartwright tries endless strategies to survive Officer Merk’s violence; loop resets on death. Explicit allegory for recurring police brutality. Contested plagiarism discourse vs earlier short Groundhog Day for a Black Man noted in public sources.
