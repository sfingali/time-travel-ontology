# Cyborg She / My Girlfriend Is a Cyborg (僕の彼女はサイボーグ, 2008)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Three nested timelines (paralyzed-Jiro→cyborg; cyborg-rescue→museum girl; girl’s second trip). EN Wikipedia hosts a dedicated Timelines section.

## Short blurb
Lonely Jiro meets a mysterious girl on his 20th birthday; a year later she returns as a future cyborg built by his older self to stop a restaurant shooting—and a third traveler from further future chooses to stay.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot + Timelines.) Kwak Jae-yong (*My Sassy Girl*) directs Haruka Ayase / Keisuke Koide. **T1:** Girl charms Jiro on 22 Nov 2007 then leaves; on his 21st birthday a gunman cripples him; lottery winnings fund a cyborg copy of the girl, sent to 22 Nov 2008 with a message from elderly Jiro. **T2:** Cyborg saves the shooting, becomes protector (rescues truck-boy, soccer team, knife attack), cannot “feel” love; Jiro drunkenly banishes her; she dies saving him in the 3 Apr 2009 Tokyo quake; he spends 61 years rebuilding her and dies 2074. Decades later a lookalike human buys the museum cyborg, downloads memories, and time-travels to Jiro’s 20th birthday (explaining the first meeting). **T3:** She returns again to the quake rubble moment, declares she can feel his heart, and stays—erasing the cyborg-building life. Also known as *Cyborg She*.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Bootstrap romance: cyborg exists because of the girl who exists because of the cyborg’s memories.
- Explicit three-timeline taxonomy on Wikipedia.
- Quake date fixed; emotional choice branches the third line.
