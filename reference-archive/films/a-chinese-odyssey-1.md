# A Chinese Odyssey Part One: Pandora's Box (1995)

## Short blurb
Reincarnated Monkey King "Joker" bungles destiny with a moonlight time box—failing three times to stop Bai Jingjing's suicide before overshooting 500 years into Zixia's era.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia series synopsis.) Five centuries after Monkey betrayed Longevity Monk, he lives as outlaw chief Joker with no memory. Demonesses Spider Woman and Bai Jingjing attack; Joker falls for Bai Jingjing. Grandpa Buddha gives a true-form mirror. In a cave fight Joker finds Pandora's Box, which opens a time portal under moonlight when "Prajñāpāramitā" is chanted, and hears Guanyin remind him of scripture-quest destiny. Bai Jingjing, believing Joker betrayed her, kills herself. Joker uses the box to rewind and stop the suicide—fails three attempts—then on the fourth overshoots to 500 years earlier, where fairy Zixia confiscates the box and brands him her servant. Cliffhanger into Part Two.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Magical box = limited rewinds with targeting error; karmic rather than tech.
- Part One is the setup loop; Part Two completes the city-gate tragedy/romance.
