# Synchronic (2019)

## Short blurb
Paramedics find a drug that sends the taker’s mind/body to another time for minutes—with lethal risks.

## Detailed plot summary
From Endless/Resolution directors. Synthetic drug Synchronic causes single-person time slips; physics quirks (Earth rotation, location) matter. Personal stakes escalate when a loved one is lost in the past. Grounded, bleak, inventive mechanism.
