# Back to the Future Part II (1989)

## Short blurb
Doc takes Marty to 2015 to fix future Marty Jr.; old Biff steals the Almanac, creating 1985A hellscape—requiring a 1955 redo alongside Part I’s dance night.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
2015 Hill Valley comedy-future; Gray’s Sports Almanac temptation. Old Biff in stolen DeLorean gives young Biff the book → **1985A** where Biff owns the town, Doc committed, Lorraine trapped, Marty “killed” in South Africa scheme. Marty and Doc go to 1955 to retrieve Almanac as young Biff wrecks the car; they operate in parallel to Part I events without colliding badly. Doc is struck by lightning → Western Union 70-year letter → Part III setup. Jennifer’s 2015 knockout subplot.

## Temporal analysis
- Clearest franchise branching: 1985 vs 1985A.
