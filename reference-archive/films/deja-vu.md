# Deja Vu (2006)

## Short blurb
After a New Orleans ferry bombing kills 543, ATF agent Doug Carlin joins a team whose “Snow White” system peers—and can send matter—~4.5 days into the past. He falls for victim Claire, leaps back, and dies saving the ferry while his present-timeline self meets the surviving Claire on the pier.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Mardi Gras ferry explosion. Carlin finds Claire Kuchever’s body staged as a blast victim though she died earlier—abducted by bomber Carroll Oerstadt. FBI’s Pryzwarra recruits Carlin into Denny’s Snow White unit: ostensibly satellite triangulation of the past, actually a wormhole time window. Watching Claire’s last days, Carlin tracks the bomber via her for-sale truck. He talks the team into sending a note to the past; partner Minuti finds it, is shot. Mobile Snow White follows the suspect to the hideout.

Oerstadt is arrested in the present and confesses; case “closed.” Carlin insists history can still be altered and is sent physically to the morning of the bombing (surviving via ER crash-in). He stops Claire’s murder, is shot, convinces her at her apartment (she calls ATF—the call his office received). On the ferry he kills Oerstadt; with the bomb armed they drive the truck into the river, saving passengers. Claire swims free; Carlin drowns. On the pier, mourning Claire meets the Doug from her present timeline—implying a successful branch where he now investigates a non-explosion or its aftermath differently.

Filmed post-Katrina in New Orleans; ferry explosion used a real boat with pyrotechnics. Brian Greene consulted on wormhole framing. Writers Marsilii/Rossio later criticized Scott for muddying their tighter Sixth-Sense-ish design toward spectacle.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Bombing + Claire anomaly.**
2. **Snow White reveal** as time window.
3. **Surveillance courtship** of Claire-in-past.
4. **Note attempt / Minuti death.**
5. **Present arrest ≠ Carlin’s satisfaction.**
6. **Bodily jump → hideout save → ferry sacrifice.**
7. **Pier meeting** with surviving Claire / present Doug.

## Paradox analysis

- **Surveillance → travel:** Information channel upgrades to matter channel.
- **Branching vs fulfillment:** Pier coda suggests a world where Claire lives and meets a Doug who never needed to die—or a healed branch; critics debate.
- **Self-sacrifice loop lite:** Dying Doug enables living Doug’s meet-cute.
- **McVeigh echo:** Oerstadt as domestic-terror mirror (creators acknowledged kinship).
- **Writers vs director:** Claimed “airtight” script vs perceived holes after shooting changes.

## Endings / cuts / production notes
