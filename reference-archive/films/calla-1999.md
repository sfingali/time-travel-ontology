# Calla (카라, 1999)

## Short blurb
Early Korean time-slip romance: a man gets a chance to revisit the past and undo tragedy around the woman he loves—prefiguring the 2000s mailbox/radio TT wave (*Il Mare*, *Ditto*).

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: EN/KR film summaries.) Song Seung-heon and Kim Hee-sun star in Song Hae-sung’s late-90s fantasy romance. After loss, the protagonist experiences a slip that lets him re-enter earlier moments tied to his beloved, attempting to prevent fatal outcomes. Tone is melodrama-first; temporal rules stay soft.
