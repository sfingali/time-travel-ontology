# Heaven's Soldiers (천군, 2005)

## Short blurb
North and South Korean commandos fighting over a secret nuke are yanked by a comet’s 433-year time rift to 1572, where they must forge young Yi Sun-sin into the admiral both Koreas revere—or history (and reunification allegory) collapses.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Period action-comedy (Park Joong-hoon, Kim Seung-woo, Hwang Jung-min, Gong Hyo-jin). During a DMZ handover of a jointly designed 50 Mt warhead, N. Korean Maj. Kang steals it; S. Korean Maj. Park’s Naval Special Forces intercept. A comet opens a rift linking perigees 433 years apart; six modern soldiers plus scientist Dr. Kim Su-yeon arrive mid-Joseon/Jurchen skirmish and are dubbed “Heavenly Soldiers.” They meet a young Yi Sun-sin who is a failed exam-taker turned ginseng smuggler/petty thief—nothing like the later hero. Park tries to train him; Kang hunts the missing warhead. After Jurchen capture of Yi and village atrocities, the moderns delay their return comet window to help defend the village; Yi finds tactical brilliance. Only Park, N. Korean Sgt. Choi, and Yi survive; Dr. Kim and the warhead return to 2005, where generals still surrender the device. Coda: Battle of Myeongnyang—Yi commands with Park and Choi as staff officers. Budget US$7–8M; reunification-themed commercial success.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Comet cycle = scheduled portal, not arbitrary machine.
- Deliberate stay-behind alters Yi’s path into legendary competence.
- Dual-Korea present → shared Joseon past allegory.
