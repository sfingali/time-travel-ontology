# The Jacket (2005)

## Short blurb
Gulf War vet Jack Starks, subjected to experimental morgue-drawer treatment, flashes to 2007 and tries to change fate.

## Detailed plot summary
After wrongful imprisonment, experimental therapy sends Jack forward; he meets Jackie and learns of his recorded death date. Attempts to rewrite personal and others’ futures. Bleak, subjective, possibly unreliable.
