# Sagrada Reset / Sakurada Reset (サクラダリセット, 2017)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Ability interaction charts (Reset × Memory × Precog × Erase); Bureau/Witch causality webs in fan explainers.

## Short blurb
In ability-town Sakurada, Misora Haruki can reset up to three days—but loses the memories of the interval—so eidetic Kei Asai becomes her Service Club partner, pulling them into Administration Bureau plots steered by a confined Witch.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Seven Sneaker Bunko novels (2009–12). Haruki’s Reset reverts the town to a Save point (max 3 days; 24h cooldown after use) and wipes her own interval memories; only Kei’s photographic memory survives. Sumire’s emotion-based precognition and suicide trigger the series’ larger chain. Bureau tools: Urachi’s 2-year object/person reset, Kagaya’s time-lock, Sakuin’s lie-colors, the Witch’s ultra-accurate simulations (Reset already priced in). 24-ep David Production anime (2017); two-part live-action films (Mar/May 2017). Entry covers franchise; film halves and anime share core mechanics.

## Timeline/structure notes

- Information that survives Reset (Kei, Tomoki’s delayed voice, Witch sims) drives strategy.
