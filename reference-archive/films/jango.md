# Jango (ஜாங்கோ, 2021)

## Short blurb
Neurosurgeon Gowtham relives the same span of days to stop a killer from murdering his estranged wife Nisha—Tamil loop thriller marketed (controversially) ahead of stronger prior Tamil loops.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: EN Wikipedia + TOI/India reviews.) **Gowtham** (Satheesh Kumar), a neurosurgeon separated from **Nisha** (Mirnalini Ravi), becomes trapped in a repeating temporal window after a conspiracy/assassin plot targets her. Each loop he retains knowledge, racing to identify the murderer with help from police/scientist figures (Hareesh Peradi’s Michael among supporting). Romantic backstory occupies substantial front half before the loop engine dominates—reviews call the concept stronger than pacing. Marketing billed it among India’s early explicit time-loop features; wiki and critics note earlier Tamil loop cinema (*Maanaadu*, *Indru Netru Naalai*) already occupied that niche. Mixed notices; useful as Tamil loop corpus sibling rather than genre peak.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Personal closed loop; win condition = identify/stop assassin before Nisha’s death sticks.
- Information carryover without public world reset awareness.
