# A Chinese Odyssey Part Two: Cinderella (1995)

## Short blurb
Continuation: Joker only wants the box to save Bai Jingjing, but Zixia loves him; accepting Monkey King destiny means abandoning worldly love—until a final possession-kiss on the city gate seals the legend.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia Part Two synopsis.) Still in the past, Joker reunites with Longevity Monk, Pigsy, and Sandy but cares only about reclaiming Pandora's Box to return and save Bai Jingjing. Zixia falls for him after he alone can unsheathe her sword (her marriage vow). When Bull King captures Zixia and the Monk, Joker falls from a cliff, is killed by Spider Woman, and only then realizes Zixia is his true love. As a ghost he accepts Guanyin's destiny, dons the golden headband, becomes Monkey, and defeats Bull King—but must relinquish love; Zixia dies saving him. Monkey uses the box to escape with the pilgrims, then awakens to see incarnations of Joker and Zixia on a city wall: he possesses Joker for one long kiss before walking away to the West. Embracing couple continue; Monkey resumes the scripture quest. Cult classic; "10,000 years" line entered Chinese pop lexicon.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Completes Part One's failed-rescue loops with destiny vs desire.
- Final kiss is a cross-incarnation closed emotional loop, not a tech paradox.
