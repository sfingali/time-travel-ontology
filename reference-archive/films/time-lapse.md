# Time Lapse (2014)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Especially rich public diagrams/explainers.

## Short blurb
Roommates find a camera that prints Polaroids of their apartment 24 hours ahead—and become trapped following the pictures.

## Detailed plot summary
Discovering neighbor Mr. Bezzerides’s rig, three friends see tomorrow’s living-room image daily. They try to game gambling and relationships; deviations cause horror. The camera’s images form a self-consistent noose—especially around murder and betrayal. Indie favorite for predestination diagrams.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Strong “must fulfill the photo” Novikov flavor.
- Chamber-piece thriller.
