# Another Earth (2011)

## Short blurb
When a duplicate Earth appears in the sky, a young woman guilty of a fatal crash seeks contact with her other self as a path to forgiveness.

## Detailed plot summary
Rhoda (Brit Marling) ruins composer John Burroughs's family in a DUI. Synchronicity with "Earth 2" suggests counterparts. She wins a ticket to travel there, then gives it to John so he might meet living counterparts of his family; she stays to face herself.

## Timeline/structure notes
