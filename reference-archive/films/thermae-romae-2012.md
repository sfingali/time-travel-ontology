# Thermae Romae (テルマエ・ロマエ, 2012 live-action film)

## Short blurb
Theatrical feature of Mari Yamazaki’s hit: Roman bath architect Lucius Modestus keeps drowning into modern Japanese sentō, misreads gadgets as slave tech, and rebuilds Hadrian-era thermae for laughs and box office.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia franchise pages; ANN.) Separate slug from the 2012 DLE flash TV and 2022 Netflix ONA. Hiroshi Abe plays Lucius; Japanese bath culture gags (showers, massage chairs, toilets, public baths) drive each portal beat. Emperor Hadrian commissions escalate as Lucius “invents” from future Japan. Companion **Marcus**; modern encounters include polyglot/history-savvy Japanese characters depending on cut. Released 28 Apr 2012 (Japan); spawned *Thermae Romae II* (2014). Soft portal rules: water/steam tunnels, no paradox policing.
