# Voices of a Distant Star (ほしのこえ, 2002)

## Short blurb
Middle-school friends Mikako and Noboru stay linked by text messages as Mikako’s mecha deployment carries her light-years away—each reply delayed by relativity until years yaw between “now”s.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: EN Wikipedia.) Shinkai’s breakthrough short feature (famously home-produced). **Mikako Nagamine** joins a UN space force chasing alien Tarsians; classmate **Noboru Terao** remains on Earth. They exchange emails/texts, but as Mikako’s ship travels farther, light-speed lag turns conversation into asynchronous time travel: her “present” messages arrive in his future; his replies reach a Mikako who has aged through battles he has not yet imagined. Culminates in emotional lag measured in years. Not a machine TT—**relativity as temporal mechanism**—but core archive adjacent for communication-across-time romance (lineage to *Your Name*’s body-swap comet).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Delay ∝ distance; no return to shared simultaneous present.
- Prototype Shinkai motif: distance = time.
