# Mirai (未来のミライ, 2018)

## Short blurb
Jealous four-year-old Kun meets future teenage sister Mirai, a humanized family dog, and relatives across decades in the garden of his Yokohama house—Hosoda's intimate family timeslip fable.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot.) Kun Ota lives in a tree-centered house designed by his architect father. After baby sister Mirai is born he grows jealous. Tantrums send him to the garden where he meets: Yukko the dog as a "prince"; 14-year-old future Mirai, who must put away Hina dolls (each day left out delays her marriage a year); his mother as a child; and his great-grandfather (a wartime aircraft worker with an injured leg). A later garden trip opens Isogo Station; boarding a terrifying train toward "Lonely Land," Kun rescues baby Mirai and finally accepts being her older brother. Future Mirai flies him through the family tree of past and future; adult Kun was the station warning. Kun rejoins his family more open-hearted. Oscar/Golden Globe–nominated animated feature.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Garden as soft portal among family past/future; not tech time machines.
- Small causal beats (dolls, bike training) tie emotional growth to temporal visits.
