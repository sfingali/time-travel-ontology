# Secret (不能說的·秘密, 2007)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Piano-score rules (allegro/lento; first-sight visibility); 1979↔1999 fan charts ubiquitous in Chinese fandom.

## Short blurb
Music student Ye Xianglun meets Lu Xiaoyu via a forbidden melody on Tamkang’s oldest piano—she is a 1979 traveler visible only to the first person she sees, and graduation-day demolition forces him to play “Secret” into her past.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia plot + plot-analysis section.) Jay Chou directorial debut. 1999: Xianglun hears Xiaoyu play “Secret”; romance, piano battles, jealousy with Qingyi. She vanishes; classmates swear he was always alone. Father (Anthony Wong) reveals 1979 Xiaoyu’s confession: the score on the antique piano time-travels; first person seen is the only witness; allegro returns to past, lento to future. Believing him unfaithful, she dies of asthma after writing desk farewell. Xianglun plays through demolition wrecking balls into an earlier 1979, appearing in her class photo. Golden Horse wins (song, VFX, Outstanding Taiwanese Film). KR/JP remakes later.
