# Summer Time Machine Blues (2005)

## Short blurb
University sci-fi clubbers break an AC remote, find a time machine left by a mysterious boy, and botch a simple yesterday trip into nested summer chaos—stage-play farce by Makoto Ueda.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) After a baseball-viewing mishap breaks the remote, photography club photos show a stranger in the room. A boy flees leaving a device; testing sends Soga to August 19 (he is the photo figure). Ishimatsu, Koizumi, and Niimi travel to steal the unbroken remote but get distracted. Further trips (including a swamp village that mistakes a traveler for a deity) knit a self-consistent comedy knot restoring the present.
