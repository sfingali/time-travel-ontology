Opening recreates 2029/1984 beats then fractures: Sarah Connor is battle-trained under “Pops” (T-800). Timelines multiply as Genisys (consumer OS) becomes Skynet’s vector. John Connor is corrupted into a nanotech villain. Critically mixed; diagram culture maps Genisys branches against prior films.
