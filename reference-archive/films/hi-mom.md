# Hi, Mom (你好，李焕英, 2021)

## Short blurb
After her mother Li Huanying is fatally injured in 2001, Jia Xiaoling slips to 1981, befriends her young mother, and tries to "upgrade" mom's husband, daughter, and life—semi-autobiographical smash hit.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Grief-stricken Xiaoling becomes Huanying's close friend in 1981 Xiangyang factory culture. Feeling she failed as a daughter, she engineers setups (including factory manager's son Shen Guanglin) hoping Huanying gets a better marriage and child than historical Jia Ling's family had. Comedy and filial piety themes drive the Spring Festival blockbuster (~US$841M), directed by and starring Jia Ling as tribute to her real mother Li Huanying (d. 2001).

## Timeline/structure notes
- Adapted from Jia Ling's 2016 comedy sketch; Chinese title is the mother's name, not "Mom."
- Time travel as reconciliation fantasy more than paradox puzzle.
