# Happy Death Day 2U (2019)

## Short blurb
The day after Tree’s escape, roommate Ryan’s quantum reactor is revealed as the loop engine. A Babyface murder and reactor pulse shove Tree into a parallel September 18 where her mother lives—forcing a choice between dimensions while nested killers and DARPA close in.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**September 19:** Ryan Phan wakes in a car-loop of his own, walks in on Tree and Carter, and resumes work on an experimental quantum reactor with Samar and Dre. Dean Bronson shuts the project. Ryan is murdered by Babyface and resets. Tree recognizes the pattern; they deduce the reactor caused her original loops. A second Ryan (from another loop layer) insists the original must die; terrified Ryan pulses the reactor.

Tree wakes on **September 18 again**—but wrong: Carter dates Danielle; her mother Julie is alive. She wants to stay. Hospital night: Tombs escapes; Lori is *not* the killer here and is stabbed; Tree falls from the roof and resets. She drafts Ryan’s team as algorithm testers, suiciding as recorder until injuries hospitalize her. She kills Tombs, then a second Babyface, and suicides again.

Algorithm ready; Tree initially chooses the mother-alive world. News reports Carter died saving Lori—Tree blows a power station to force a restart, then commits to returning home. She warns Lori about Gregory, exposes Danielle’s cheating, and says goodbye to her mother. Bronson confiscates the reactor; Danielle distracts him for a heist. At the hospital Tree saves Lori from Tombs but faces second Babyface = **Gregory** burying the affair; wife Stephanie shoots Lori then is betrayed/killed by Gregory; Tree kills Gregory. Reactor fires; Tree returns to original dimension on September 19.

Tag: DARPA takes the team and reactor; Tree volunteers Danielle as test subject—Danielle wakes screaming.

## Paradox analysis

- **Mechanism reveal:** Film 1’s unexplained loop = accidental reactor side-effect.
- **Nested / branched loops:** Ryan loop + Tree parallel loop; “second Ryan” implies multi-instance bleed.
- **Parallel not pure rewrite:** Mother-alive world is a different universe, not an edit of Tree’s original past.
- **Emotional conservation:** Carter argues grief shaped Tree—staying would steal a life that isn’t hers.
- **Double Babyface:** Parallel rules swap Lori innocence for Gregory/Stephanie conspiracy—same mask, new ontology.
- **DARPA sequel hook:** Institutional capture of the device.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Universal/Blumhouse; $9M → ~$64.6M (soft vs original). Release shifted off Parkland anniversary date. Shot New Orleans May 2018. Third film (*Happy Death Day to Us*) repeatedly teased; Rothe/Landon have said it is “moving forward” as of 2025 reports.
