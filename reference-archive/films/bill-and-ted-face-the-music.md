# Bill & Ted Face the Music (2020)

## Short blurb
Middle-aged Bill and Ted must create the song that saves reality; their daughters recruit historical musicians.

## Detailed plot summary
Third film: utopian future warns the song never came. Parallel plots—Bill/Ted hop futures looking for themselves; daughters build a band across time. Themes of creative failure, marriage, and still being “excellent.” Closes trilogy loops with character warmth.
