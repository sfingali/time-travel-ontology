# The Terminator (1984)

## Short blurb
Skynet sends a T-800 to kill Sarah Connor; Resistance sends Kyle Reese—who becomes John Connor’s father, bootstrapping the leader Skynet tries to erase.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
1984 LA: naked cyborg vs naked Reese. Sarah is targeted; Reese explains Judgment Day 1997 and future war. Love story; Tech-Noir assault; factory climax—T-800 crushed, arm/CPU left behind (seed for Cyberdyne in sequels). Reese dies; Sarah pregnant with John, drives toward storm—future war inevitable tone.

## Paradox analysis
- **Bootstrap:** John’s existence depends on Reese sent by John.
- Closed causal loop vs later films’ “no fate” branching (T2).
- Time sphere displacement; one-way.
