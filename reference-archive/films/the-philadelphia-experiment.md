# The Philadelphia Experiment (1984)

## Short blurb
1943 sailors David Herzig and Jim Parker are flung from destroyer escort Eldridge’s failed invisibility test into 1984 Nevada. A related modern experiment threatens a spacetime rupture; they must stop both eras’ machines and decide whether to return.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Urban-legend frame: WWII U.S. Navy attempts to radar-cloak a ship; the experiment instead teleports crew through time. David and Jim surface near a 1984 desert test site linked to the same physics. Fish-out-of-water comedy (cars, culture) mixes with thriller stakes as scientists realize continuing the modern test will tear reality. Romance with Allison (Nancy Allen) anchors David’s possible stay. Climax requires intervening in the original 1943 event / shutting the cascade—soft closed-loop repair rather than Primer accounting. Cover-up apparatus looms.

Sequel *Philadelphia Experiment II* (1993) explores a Nazi-victory branch from related meddling.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **1943 test failure** — sailors vanish.
2. **1984 arrival** near modern related experiment.
3. **Culture shock + investigation.**
4. **Rift escalation** if modern test proceeds.
5. **Cross-era shutdown / personal stay-or-return choice.**

## Paradox analysis

- **Legend-as-premise:** Not hard physics—folklore TT.
- **Linked experiments:** Past and present machines as coupled resonators.
- **Repair mission:** Stop the cascade = set-right logic.
- **Sequel branching:** II makes alternate history explicit.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

New World Pictures era cult title; Paré star vehicle. Soft SF action rather than paradox puzzle. Frequently cited in “is the Philadelphia Experiment real?” pop discourse (it is not).
