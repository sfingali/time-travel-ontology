# Terminator 2: Judgment Day (1991)

## Short blurb
A protector T-800 and young John Connor flee the liquid-metal T-1000 while Sarah decides to prevent Skynet’s birth at Cyberdyne—“the future is not set.”

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
1995: reprogrammed T-800 vs T-1000. Asylum break; Dyson/Cyberdyne plot; liquid nitrogen then smelting climax. T-800 self-terminates to destroy chip. Judgment Day seemingly averted—until later sequels re-open fate. Theme shift from T1 predestination to branching hope.

## Analysis
- Explicit anti-predestination thesis vs T1 bootstrap.
- Extended Special Edition adds Dream/Dyson scenes.
- Apex of series diagram/action influence.
