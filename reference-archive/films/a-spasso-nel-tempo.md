# A spasso nel tempo (1996)

## Short blurb
Rival Italian tourists board Professor Mortimer's "Machine of Time" at Universal Studios and ricochet through eras—cinepanettone riff on *Back to the Future*.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Ascanio Colonna and Walter Colombo jam the ride; sent to prehistory then various ages of Italian history as Mortimer tries to retrieve them. Christmas comedy hit; sequel *L'avventura continua* (1997). Adam O'Leary: "farcical elaborations of the Back to the Future films."
