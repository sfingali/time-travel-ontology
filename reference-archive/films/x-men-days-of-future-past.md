# X-Men: Days of Future Past (2014)

## Short blurb
In a Sentinel-ravaged future, Kitty Pryde sends Wolverine’s mind into his 1973 body to stop Mystique from assassinating Bolivar Trask—the act that creates the Sentinel program—and to reconcile young Xavier and Magneto before history locks into extinction.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Future war: adaptive Sentinel robots hunt mutants and collaborating humans. Surviving X-Men (Storm, Bishop, Colossus, Iceman, Blink, etc.) with old Xavier and Magneto plan a temporal hail-mary: Kitty projects Logan’s consciousness decades back. Mission: prevent Mystique’s public killing of Trask after he unveils anti-mutant tech, which historically justifies Sentinel production and Nixon-era crackdowns.

1973 Logan must sober up young Xavier (broken, addicted), spring Magneto from a Pentagon-adjacent prison (plastic isolation), and intercept Mystique—who seeks revenge for experimented mutants. Spectacle beats: stadium lift, White House confrontation, Quicksilver kitchen rescue (FOX cut). Success yields a healed future classroom coda: Logan wakes among living students and a smiling Xavier—prior grim future overwritten. The film functions as soft reboot stitching First Class era to later continuity.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Future hellscape** establishes stakes.
2. **Mind-send rules** (body stays; consciousness rides past self).
3. **1973 recruitment** of broken Xavier / imprisoned Magneto.
4. **Trask/Mystique chase** across USA.
5. **Climax prevention** of the assassination cascade.
6. **Classroom sunrise** — branch confirmed.

## Paradox analysis

- **Consciousness travel:** Logan’s future knowledge in past body—no duplicate Wolverines on-screen.
- **Historical hinge:** One assassination → Sentinel apocalypse; prevention = civilizational branch.
- **Character reconciliation as physics:** Xavier’s hope is treated as necessary condition for the good future.
- **Franchise reboot tool:** Erases inconvenient deaths/continuities via successful branch.
- **Comic fidelity:** Claremont/Byrne DOFP is the ur-“fix the future by fixing the past” mutant story.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Fox; major box-office hit; Oscar-nominated visual effects. Extended/Rogue Cut adds footage. Quicksilver scene became meme-iconic. Followed by *Apocalypse* in the revised timeline.
