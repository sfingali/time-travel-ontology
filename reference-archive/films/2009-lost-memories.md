# 2009: Lost Memories (2009 로스트메모리즈, 2002)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Opening alt-history timeline (1909–2002 divergences); Lunar Soul / temple stone logistics.

## Short blurb
In an alternate 2009 where Japan still rules Korea, JBI agent Sakamoto learns a time-travel temple let nationalist Inoue save Itō Hirobumi in 1909—and allies with terrorists to send himself back and restore An Jung-geun’s assassination.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Sci-fi action adapted (loosely) from Bok Geo-il’s novel *Looking for an Epitaph*; Jang Dong-gun & Toru Nakamura. Opening crawl lists divergences: failed 1909 assassination → Japan as WWII ally of the US → Berlin A-bombed → Korea still Chōsen, 1988 Olympics in Nagoya, etc. Sakamoto and partner Saigo stop Hureisenjin hostages at a Keijō museum; a crescent “Lunar Soul” artifact links the group to the Inoue Foundation. Investigation reveals: archaeologists uncovered a time-travel temple; uyoku agent Inoue used it to prevent Itō’s death, becoming second Governor-General; a Korean researcher who followed him founded Hureisenjin to undo the rewrite. After Takahashi’s murder frames Sakamoto, he joins Hureisenjin leader Oh Hye-rin. Temple activates mid-gunfight; Sakamoto arrives Harbin 1909, pursued by Saigo (who wants to keep his wife’s Hiroshima family alive in the alt timeline). Sakamoto kills Inoue and stops Saigo so An can assassinate Itō. He bonds with original-timeline Hye-rin; Independence Hall photos in restored 2009 show them together. Author Bok sued to remove his name. Co-production timed with 2002 World Cup.

## Timeline/structure notes
