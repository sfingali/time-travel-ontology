# The Place Promised in Our Early Days (雲のむこう、約束の場所, 2004)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Alternate Cold War Japan maps + Tower overwrite-radius / Sayuri dream-world explainers are common.

## Short blurb
In a divided Japan where the Union builds a Hokkaido Tower that swaps local matter with other universes, two friends rebuild a plane to reach it—and to wake the girl whose coma-dream is channeling the Tower’s energy.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Alt-history: WWII ends with **Hokkaido/Ezo** occupied by the Union (USSR-analogue), rest of Japan under U.S. influence. Union erects a mysterious Tower (designer Ekusun Tsukinoe). In 1996 Aomori, teens **Hiroki**, **Takuya**, and **Sayuri** vow to fly a rebuilt drone plane to the Tower. Sayuri vanishes that summer into extreme narcolepsy—her mind trapped in an empty parallel universe.

Three years later Hiroki and Takuya have split: Takuya researches parallel universes for an Alliance/NSA lab; the Tower’s matter-replacement radius is ~2 km and growing risk. Uilta Liberation Front (Okabe) plans to bomb the Tower to force reunification war. Sayuri’s grandfather (Union physicist) used her to dump the Tower’s unstable dimension energy off-Earth; without her the overwrite radius could cover the world. Hiroki and Takuya reunite, finish the two-seat plane; Hiroki flies Sayuri plus a missile. She wakes circling the Tower as it activates and expands across Hokkaido; she loses dream-memories (including love for Hiroki). Hiroki fires, destroys the Tower, stops the transformation; they restart their relationship.
