# Summer Wars (サマーウォーズ, 2009)

## Short blurb
Math whiz Kenji is dragged to a sprawling Ueda family birthday and falsely blamed when rogue AI Love Machine hijacks the global virtual world OZ—threatening both cyberspace and real infrastructure.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) In 2010 Kenji Koiso moderates OZ with friend Sakuma. Classmate Natsuki Shinohara brings him to great-grandmother Sakae Jinnouchi's 90th birthday, introducing him as her fiancé. Kenji cracks a cryptic email code; Love Machine uses his account to hijack OZ, causing cascading real-world failures. Half-great-uncle Wabisuke reveals he built the AI and sold it for military testing. After Sakae dies when Love Machine disables her heart monitor, the clan fights back: ice-cooled supercomputer capture attempt fails; Love Machine redirects an asteroid probe toward a nuclear plant then the estate. The family plays Hanafuda Koi-Koi in OZ wagering accounts; worldwide users pile onto Natsuki's side; King Kazma (cousin Kazuma's avatar) destroys Love Machine; Kenji redirects the probe. The film ends with family celebration and Kenji/Natsuki confession.
