# Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022)

## Short blurb
Laundromat owner Evelyn verse-jumps across lives to stop Jobu Tupaki—her daughter’s nihilist god-self born from Alphaverse overload—choosing kindness over the Everything Bagel.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
IRS audit day. Alpha-Waymond recruits Evelyn: verse-jumping loads skills from other lives via absurd acts. Jobu, fractured across all universes, wields an Everything Bagel singularity of nihilism. Battles span hot-dog fingers, rock universe, raccoon chef nods, etc. Climax: Evelyn nearly mirrors Jobu’s bagel, then chooses googly-eye compassion, reconciling with Joy/Jobu and Gong Gong, repairing marriage. Multiverse as immigrant/family allegory.
