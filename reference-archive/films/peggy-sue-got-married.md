# Peggy Sue Got Married (1986)

## Short blurb
Facing divorce, Peggy Sue faints at her high-school reunion and wakes as her 1960 teen self with adult memory—tempted to dump future ex Charlie, skip mistakes, and chase astronomy dreams—before choosing a softened path back.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Peggy Sue (Turner), separating from philandering Charlie (Cage), attends a reunion, collapses, and awakens in 1960. She tests knowledge, re-evaluates parents and friends, sleeps with the poetic Michael instead of only Charlie, and visits grandfather about “time.” Ultimately she still connects with Charlie—seeing the germ of the man she loved—and returns to a present where reconciliation is possible and a mysterious stranger (adult Michael?) brushes by. Softer than *Butterfly Effect*: regret tempered by compassion.

- **Charlie problem:** Can people change, or only our stance toward them?
