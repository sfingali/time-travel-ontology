# The Map of Tiny Perfect Things (2021)

## Short blurb
Two teens stuck in the same day map its tiny wonders; motives for staying diverge.

## Detailed plot summary
Mark treats the loop as playground; Margaret uses it with hidden purpose tied to family illness. They collaborate on a map of perfect moments. Soft exit conditions; emotional honesty over puzzle-box rules. Based on Lev Grossman’s short story.
