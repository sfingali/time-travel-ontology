# Star Trek IV: The Voyage Home (1986)

## Short blurb
After Genesis/Trillogy fallout, Kirk’s crew slingshots a stolen Klingon Bird-of-Prey around the Sun to 1986 San Francisco to retrieve humpback whales—extinct in the 23rd century—and answer a planet-disabling alien probe with whale song.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

**2286:** An alien probe vaporizes oceans and powers while broadcasting a signal none can answer—because it seeks humpback whales, hunted to extinction. On Vulcan, Kirk’s renegade crew (post–*Search for Spock*) flies the cloaked Bird-of-Prey *HMS Bounty* toward Earth to face court-martial, then diverts into a **time warp via solar slingshot** to the late 20th century.

1986 San Francisco fish-out-of-water comedy: Chekov and Uhura hunt nuclear “power”; Scotty and McCoy invent transparent aluminum to fund a whale tank; Kirk and Spock track whales **George and Gracie** at the Cetacean Institute under Gillian Taylor’s care—due to be released into open ocean (and whaling risk). Chekov is injured aboard USS *Enterprise* (the aircraft carrier); team hospital-rescues him. Gillian learns the truth and beams aboard as the ship returns via second slingshot with the whales.

Whales’ song answers the probe; Earth recovers. Federation council still tries Kirk et al.; charges dropped save one demotion: Kirk reduced to Captain, rewarded with a new *Enterprise*-A. Gillian joins a science vessel.

## Beat-by-beat temporal structure

1. **Probe crisis** — future needs extinct past life.
2. **Slingshot to 1986** — classical Trek time method (also used in TOS “Tomorrow Is Yesterday” lineage).
3. **Mission beats in parallel** — reactor fuel, tank metal, whale pair, Chekov rescue.
4. **Return slingshot + whale song** closes ecological loop.
5. **Court-martial soft landing** — character status reset for TNG-era films.

No alternate-timeline Borg rewrite (*First Contact*); this is a **closed success mission** that restores the “proper” 2286.

## Paradox analysis

- **Closed ecological loop:** Future crisis caused by past extinction; past retrieval restores future—Grandfather-adjacent but species-level.
- **Minimal butterfly:** Crew tries not to contaminate (mostly fails comedically: transparent aluminum may be a future-tech bootstrap).
- **Gillian emigration:** One present human permanently joins the future—soft displacement.
- **Probe as external timer:** Not a paradox engine; a MacGuffin enforcing the fetch quest.
- **Tone:** Softest-stakes Trek time travel—conservation fable + fish-out-of-water.

## Endings / cuts / production notes

Paramount; Nimoy directed. Huge box-office hit; Academy Award nominations (cinematography, sound, sound effects, score). Famous for no-phaser-gun violence marketing. Whales filmed with full-size animatronics and footage. Directly continues cast from III; leads into V.

## Adaptation / related text notes

Screen story evolved from Eddie Murphy vehicle pitches and comedy-forward drafts. Memory Alpha is the canonical franchise beat sheet. Pair with *First Contact* for “Trek time travel: comedy mission vs war for history.”
