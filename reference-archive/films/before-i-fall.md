# Before I Fall (2017)

## Short blurb
Popular senior Sam Kingston relives the Friday of a fatal crash until she changes who she is.

## Detailed plot summary
Adaptation of Lauren Oliver. Mean-girl Sam loops the day leading to a car accident involving classmate Juliet; empathy and sacrifice exit conditions mirror Groundhog Day via YA tragedy.
