# La Belle Époque (2019)

## Short blurb
Disillusioned ex-draftsman Victor hires *Les Voyageurs du temps*—a company that stages immersive historical nights—to relive 16 May 1974, the Lyon café week he met wife Marianne, and falls for the actress playing her.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Nicolas Bedos romantic comedy-drama; Daniel Auteuil, Fanny Ardant, Guillaume Canet, Doria Tillier. Cannes 2019 out-of-competition. Victor’s marriage to Marianne is collapsing amid tech-age alienation. Son Maxime gifts a session with Antoine’s company, which mixes sets and actors so clients can “dine with Hitler,” join 17th-century aristocrats, etc. After Marianne throws him out, Victor books the week of their first meeting at café La Belle Époque. Margot plays young Marianne while living her own messy affair with control-freak ex-screenwriter Antoine. Victor loses himself in the reconstitution until simulation and desire blur. Soft TT: no physics machine—theatrical time travel / memory tourism adjacent to *Midnight in Paris* and *Synecdoche*.
