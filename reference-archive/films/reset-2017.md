# Reset (逆时营救, 2017)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Stacked Xia Tian doppelgängers across parallel hours; aggressiveness/cell-decay rule.

## Short blurb
Scientist Xia Tian hops to parallel universes ~1h50m earlier via experimental wormhole tech to stop her son's murder—meeting increasingly unstable copies of herself.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Nexus Corp wormhole research; rival IPT hires Tsui Hu to steal data. He kidnaps Dou Dou; Xia Tian is late and the boy dies. She jumps to a parallel timeline, recovers him with fake data, but he dies again. Third universe: three Xia Tians; #1 (aggressive from travel) and #2 save Dou Dou; Director is revealed as Tsui Hu's co-conspirator (fired, wants proof the tech works). Tsui Hu (ex-IPT test subject hoping to save his own family) dies warning the copies will turn on each other. #1 killed by #2; unchanged #3 reunites with Dou Dou; #2's fate in exploding lab ambiguous. Yang Mi, Wallace Huo; ~$30M BO.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Parallel-universe hops with time offset, not pure single-timeline rewind.
- Test-subject aggression as cost of travel.
