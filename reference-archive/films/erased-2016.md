# Erased — live-action film (僕だけがいない街, 2016)

## Short blurb
Theatrical cut of *Boku dake ga Inai Machi*: manga artist Satoru Fujinuma’s “Revival” ability flings him to 1988 after his mother’s murder so he can stop a child serial killer—and save classmate Kayo.
