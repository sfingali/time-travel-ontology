# Sexmission (Seksmisja, 1984)

## Short blurb
Max and Albert volunteer for a 1991 hibernation test, overshoot into 2044’s underground League of Women regime that claims males are extinct—then cut through a painted “dead surface” into a livable world run by a cross-dressing Excellency.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia.) Polish cult SF satire. Instead of waking in 1994, the men find a drug-dampened all-female society reproducing by parthenogenesis after Kuppelweiser’s war agent permanently destroyed male genes. Attraction to Lamia, trial over “naturalization” (forced sex reassignment) vs liquidation, escapes through decadent anarchist nests, and a periscope showing Kuppelweiser-radiated wasteland ensue. Lamia helps them reach a surface capsule (password: a Polish profanity). Suits, invisible tent-painted desert, then beach/forest/villa—radiation was a control myth. Twist: Her Excellency is a hidden male raised in disguise. Deal struck; the men spike incubation flasks with male gametes. Final gag: nurse unwraps a newborn with a penis. Read as allegory of late PRL totalitarianism, propaganda about the West, and gender satire; feminist criticism of emancipatory framing. Best-Polish-film-of-30-years poll darling (2005) despite critical coolness.
