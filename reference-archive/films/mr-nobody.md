# Mr. Nobody (2009)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Rich public diagrams/explainers exist.

## Short blurb
Nemo Nobody, last mortal in a quasi-immortal future, recounts contradictory lives based on childhood choices — trains, wives, disasters — as speculative parallel biographies.

## Detailed plot summary
Young Nemo's choice between mother and father forks into lives with Anna, Elise, or Jean. The film intercuts possible adulthoods, arguing every unlived path "exists" in possibility space. Framing device: 118-year-old Nemo before universal rebirth.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Not machine travel; cinematic many-worlds of choice.
