# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (2004)

> **DIAGRAM FLAG:** Dual-pass evening timelines (first viewing vs Time-Turner pass) are standard classroom/fan diagrams; Lexicon and HP Wiki map the closed loop.

## Short blurb
Hermione’s Ministry Time-Turner—first used to stack classes—lets her and Harry rewind three hours. They do not rewrite history: they *become* the mysterious helpers who already saved Buckbeak and Sirius, and Harry casts the Patronus he thought was his father’s.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)

Third year: Sirius Black has escaped Azkaban; Dementors guard Hogwarts. Hermione secretly uses a **Time-Turner** to attend overlapping classes. After the Shrieking Shack confrontation—Pettigrew exposed, Sirius innocent, Lupin transforming—Pettigrew flees. Dementors overwhelm Harry and Sirius by the lake; a glowing Patronus stag drives them off. Harry believes it was his dead father.

In the hospital wing, Dumbledore prompts Hermione: “What is the one thing?” She and Harry use the Time-Turner (**three turns**) to revisit the evening.

### Second pass (closed loop fulfilled)
- They free **Buckbeak** before execution; the axe hits a pumpkin/fence—what Harry heard earlier.
- Hermione pelts stones/signals to avoid past-Harry noticing them too soon.
- They watch the lake: Harry realizes *he* must cast the Patronus. He does—the stag that saved them.
- They free Sirius from the tower with Buckbeak’s flight; Fudge and Snape find an “empty” execution scene.

They return to the hospital wing moments after leaving. Hermione later surrenders the Time-Turner; Order of the Phoenix later destroys the Department of Mysteries stock (Rowling’s fix for plot holes).

## Paradox analysis

- **Novikov self-consistency:** Nothing is changed; observed mysteries are explained as future-selves’ actions (“you already changed the past”).
- **Bootstrap Patronus:** Harry can cast it because he saw himself cast it—confidence loop.
- **Danger rules (diegetic):** Don’t be seen; wizards killing past selves; Mintumble case (Pottermore) shows longer jumps can un-birth people—*Cursed Child* later breaks PoA’s clean Novikov model with alternate timelines.
- **Rowling’s regret note:** Official Wizarding World essay admits Time-Turners opened “vast problems”; limited to ~hours and then smashed.

## Adaptations
Film follows book’s loop logic closely; *Cursed Child* stage play uses a different (branching) Time-Turner mythology—treat as separate continuity for paradox taxonomy.
