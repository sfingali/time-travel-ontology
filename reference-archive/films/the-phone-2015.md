# The Phone (더 폰, 2015)

## Short blurb
A man under life-destroying surveillance discovers his phone can reach someone in the past—and uses the link in a thriller bid to undo framing, family threat, and corporate conspiracy.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia / KR film coverage.) Son Hyun-joo plays a man whose life collapses under phone-based persecution; the device’s anomalous connection to an earlier time (or earlier version of events) becomes the tool to reverse the trap. Uhm Ji-won co-stars. Tone is paranoid tech-thriller rather than soft romance TT—closer to *The Call* (2020) lineage of telephony as temporal weapon. Spoilers in KR reviews stress escalating calls that rewrite who holds power in the present.
