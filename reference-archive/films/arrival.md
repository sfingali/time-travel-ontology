# Arrival (2016)

> **DEBATE NOTE:** Is this “time travel”? Archive includes it as non-linear consciousness / block-universe perception that enables future-memory bootstrap acts—not a machine.

## Short blurb
Linguist Louise Banks learns Heptapod B and begins experiencing her life non-linearly—including a future daughter’s life and death—using future knowledge to avert global war.

## Detailed plot summary (spoilers)
Twelve alien vessels. Louise and physicist Ian decode circular logograms. As fluency grows, Louise’s “flashbacks” are revealed as **memories of the future**. She phone-calls General Shang with his dying wife’s words—information she only learns later—bootstrapping peace. She chooses to have Hannah knowing Hannah will die of illness; Ian eventually leaves when he learns she knew. Theme: free will within known future; language reshaping cognition (weak Sapir-Whorf SF).

## Why include / debate
- No DeLorean; time does not “change.”
- Fits destiny_manipulation / block_universe / bootstrap tags.
- Chiang’s story is explicitly about variational physics and teleology.
