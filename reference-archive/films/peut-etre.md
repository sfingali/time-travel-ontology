# Peut-être (1999)

## Short blurb
At a Buck Rogers New Year’s party, Arthur finds a bathroom ceiling portal to a desertified future Paris where aged Ako—his yet-unconceived son—begs him to impregnate girlfriend Lucie so Ako can exist.

## Detailed plot summary
(Source: English Wikipedia stub + Scarecrow/Mazdon citations.) Cédric Klapisch; Romain Duris, Jean-Paul Belmondo, Géraldine Pailhas. After sex with Lucie, Arthur climbs into a sun-baked future where Belmondo’s Ako lobbies for his own birth. Soft comedy of commitment-as-time-travel: the future depends on a maybe. Budgety French SF comedy (~$13.8M) with modest box office; EN wiki remains thin—plot kept to verified premise without invented set pieces.
