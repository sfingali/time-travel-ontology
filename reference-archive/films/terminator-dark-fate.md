# Terminator: Dark Fate (2019)

## Short blurb
After John’s early death, a new future war sends Grace and a Rev-9 after Dani Ramos—Sarah Connor hunts machines again.

## Detailed plot summary
Produced with James Cameron’s involvement as a direct follow-up to T2 (ignoring 3/Genisys). John is killed by a remaining T-800 years after Judgment Day was averted; that T-800 (“Carl”) later allies with Sarah. Augmented soldier Grace protects Dani, revealed as future Resistance leader. Themes: legacy trauma, found family, refreshed future war without John.

## Timeline/structure notes
- Canon stance: continues from T2; sidelining intervening sequels.
- Rev-9 dual-entity liquid/endoskeleton threat.
