## What the diagrams show

### Nodes
- Primary Universe / Tangent Universe
- Artifact (jet engine), Living Receiver (Donnie), Manipulated Dead (Frank), Manipulated Living
- Calendar: Oct 2 → Oct 30 (± Philosophy of Time Travel clock)

### Loops / portals
- Classic A–B–C diagrams: split at A, film events B→C in Tangent, Artifact returned C→A, Donnie dies in Primary.
- TimeTravelMovies.org diverging-timeline PNGs: fork then restore.

### Branches
