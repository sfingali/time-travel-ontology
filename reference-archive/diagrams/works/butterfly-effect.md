# The Butterfly Effect (2004) — diagram archaeology

**Type focus:** timeline / branching life-paths  
**Model:** Evan’s journals as access points; each overwrite produces a new present; theatrical vs director’s endings differ.

## What the diagrams show

### Nodes
- Childhood trauma beats (block party, basement, etc.) as **branch points**
- Adult Evan + Kayleigh / Lenny / Tommy outcome states
- Journal / blackout memory as the travel mechanism

### Branches
- Diagrams (where they exist) are **trees of endings**, not closed loops: changing one past node cascades present relationships, disabilities, incarceration, etc.
- Conceptual overlap with chaos “butterfly” classroom diagrams (Lorenz), often linked metaphorically rather than film-accurate.
