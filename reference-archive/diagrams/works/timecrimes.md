# Timecrimes (Los Cronocrímenes, 2007) — diagram archaeology

**Type focus:** causality (deterministic triple loop)  
**Model:** Héctor experiences ~one hour three times; becomes his own bandaged pursuer; scientist helps close the loop.

## What the diagrams show

### Nodes
- Héctor1 / Héctor2 (bandaged) / Héctor3
- Lab machine, woods, house roof, cyclist woman
- Scientist with walkie-talkie

### Loops
- Astronomy Trek numbered 1–10: each fear/event is caused by a later Héctor ensuring earlier memories.
- Roy Christopher Fig.1: **one straight corridor** of experience that happens to wind through the same hour thrice (not three parallel movies).
