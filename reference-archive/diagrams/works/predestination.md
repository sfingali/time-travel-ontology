# Predestination (2014) — diagram archaeology

**Type focus:** causality / bootstrap identity loop  
**Model:** Closed self-consistent loop (Novikov-flavoured): one person is mother, father, child, agent, and bomber.

## What the diagrams show

### Nodes
- Jane / John / Barkeep / Fizzle Bomber (same continuum of identity)
- Temporal Bureau HQ (1992), orphanage (1945), 1963 romance, 1970 burns, 1975 bomber confrontation
- Field kit (time machine)

### Loops
- **Bootstrap genealogy:** baby delivered to orphanage is the adult who births and fathers the baby.
- **Predestination of the bomber:** agent killing future self becomes the path that creates the bomber.
- Diagrams typically draw a **circle or Möbius** of life stages rather than a branching tree.

### Branches
- Film asserts no successful branch; diagrams rarely show multiverse forks (contrast Steins;Gate / Dark).
